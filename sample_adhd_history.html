📅 The Name Game: A Timeline
1902

📚 The Beginning

Sir George Still - British pediatrician

  • What he called it: "Defect of Moral Control"
  • His observation: Kids with normal intelligence but couldn't control behavior
  • Red flag language: "Moral defect" (yikes!)
  • What he got right: Recognized it wasn't about intelligence
1960s

⚡ The Hyperactivity Era

Official name: "Hyperkinetic Reaction of Childhood"

  • Focus: Physical restlessness and overactivity
  • Problem: Only described the "bouncing off walls" kids
  • Missing: The quiet dreamers, the internal hyperactivity
  • Treatment: Mostly medications to "calm down" kids

🔍 What This Missed:

  • Girls with ADHD (who often internalize symptoms)
  • Attention problems without hyperactivity
  • Adults with ADHD
  • The positive aspects of ADHD traits
1980

🎯 The Attention Revolution

DSM-III introduced: "Attention Deficit Disorder (ADD)"

🌟 Major Breakthrough:
  • With hyperactivity (ADD-H)
  • Without hyperactivity (ADD-WO)
  • Finally recognized: Attention problems were the core issue

Why this mattered:

  • ✅ Validated the "quiet" ADHD kids
  • ✅ Shifted focus from behavior to cognition
  • ✅ Opened door for more girls to be diagnosed
1987-94

🔄 The Combo Era

New name: "Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)"

The Three Types (1994-2013):
ADHD
├── ADHD-I (Inattentive)
│ └── "The Dreamers"
├── ADHD-HI (Hyperactive-Impulsive)
│ └── "The Movers"
└── ADHD-C (Combined)
    └── "The Mix"
2013

🌈 The Modern Understanding

DSM-5 Changes: "ADHD Presentations" (not types)

🎯 Key Shifts:

  • "Types" → "Presentations" (acknowledges symptoms can change over time)
  • Age of onset: Must be present by age 12 (was 7)
  • Adult ADHD: Finally officially recognized!
  • Severity levels: Mild, Moderate, Severe
🧬 The Science Evolution

Early Theories (1960s-80s)

❌ Old Myths

"Minimal Brain Damage"

Thought ADHD = brain injury

Problem: Most ADHD kids had no brain damage

"Poor Parenting"

Blamed mothers (especially working moms)

Damage: Tons of parent guilt and shame

✅ Current Science

Neurodevelopmental Difference

Genetics: 70-80% heritable

Brain differences: Real, measurable differences

Executive function: Core challenge with brain's "CEO" skills

👥 Who Got Left Behind (And Why)

🚺 Women & Girls

Why they were missed:

  • Symptoms often internalized
  • Better masking/coping strategies
  • Research bias toward hyperactive boys

📈 Current stats: Diagnosis gap is closing!

🌍 Cultural Minorities

Ongoing challenges:

  • Misdiagnosis as behavioral problems
  • Cultural differences in symptom expression
  • Access to quality assessment

🎯 Quick Review

🔑 Evolution Summary:

  1. 1902: "Moral defect" → 2024: Neurodevelopmental difference
  2. Focus shifted: Behavior → Attention → Executive function
  3. Recognition expanded: Boys only → All genders, all ages
  4. Perspective changed: Disorder → Difference (for many)

📚 Study Connection:

  • Notice how scientific understanding evolves
  • Language shapes how we see conditions
  • Social context influences medical definitions
  • Personal experience drives research questions
