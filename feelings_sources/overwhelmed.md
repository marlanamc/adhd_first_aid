# 📚 Sources: Feeling Overwhelmed with ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of overwhelm and offer strategies for externalizing thoughts, reducing demands, creating structure, and practicing self-compassion.

---

## 🧠 Understanding ADHD Overwhelm

- **You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Offers a **“Basic First-Aid Plan for Overwhelm”**, explains how overstimulation leads to **stuckness**, and encourages **breaking tasks into smaller steps** with compassion.
    
- **Scattered Minds** – _Gabor Maté_  
    Explores the **emotional roots of people-pleasing, guilt**, and avoidance. Emphasizes the importance of **learning to sit with discomfort** and protect emotional energy.
    
- **Healthy Happy ADHD** – _Lisa Dee_  
    Connects **nervous system dysregulation** with overwhelm and advocates for **saying no**, rest, and movement to reset the system.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy Ratey_  
    Identifies how **choice paralysis** and executive overload lead to inaction. Discusses the ADHD habit of relying on **“last-minute pressure”** for activation.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Describes the **drive for information** and emotional aftermath of disorganization. Highlights the need for **external structure and support** as a “bridge.”
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk_  
    Explains how **trauma and hyperarousal** show up in the body and provides **bottom-up regulation strategies** like breath and movement.

## 🧰 Tools to Calm the Chaos

### 🧠 Externalize, Organize, Simplify

- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Offers **brain dump tools**, **emotion trackers**, and systems for working _with_ your ADHD brain. Encourages planning support without shame.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Recommends **brain dumps**, **clear workflows**, and processing **shame and disappointment** with compassion.
    
- **Building a Second Brain** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Teaches systems for **offloading mental clutter** into organized notes. Emphasizes action-focused storage and **psychological safety** through design.
    
- **The PARA Method** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Recommends **archiving everything** and starting fresh when overwhelmed, giving permission to **reset without shame**.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** – _Dawson & Guare_  
    Offers strategies to **“off-load” tasks and ideas**, break overwhelm into **manageable actions**, and build executive function skills over time.
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Validates the **emotional toll of disorganization** and shares tools like **“Someday Maybe” lists** to release unnecessary pressure.
    

---

### 🪜 Baby Steps & Reducing Demands

- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Demonstrates how **tiny changes** build momentum. Encourages starting where success feels inevitable.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Suggests **affect labeling**, self-reflection, and gentle experimentation to reduce overwhelm and understand emotions beneath avoidance.
    
- **Feel-Good Productivity** – _Ali Abdaal_  
    Helps identify **emotional blockers**, refocus energy, and integrate rest as a productivity tool.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Designed for **executive dysfunction and overwhelm**. Offers playful tools like **“How to Get Out of Doing Shit”**, **“Draw Your Feels,”** and **“Commitment Lists.”**
    
- **Start Finishing** – _Charlie Gilkey_  
    Encourages pausing, reflecting, and **lowering expectations** until clarity and motivation return.
    

---

### 🧘‍♀️ Emotional Regulation & Support

- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Offers **self-compassion tools**, **soothing rhythm breathing**, and **burnout planning strategies**. Emphasizes emotional regulation through sensory tools and community.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Redefines self-care as setting boundaries, addressing burnout, and practicing **“kind and flexible thinking”** — not checklists.
    
- **Motivational Interviewing** – _Miller & Rollnick_  
    Advocates for **partnership-based support**, **gentle guidance**, and validation of ambivalence when feeling stuck.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Encourages **creative workarounds** and recommends professional support when personal systems fall short.
    
- **The Coaching Habit** – _Michael Bungay Stanier_  
    Provides scripts and strategies to **say “no,” set limits**, and **avoid overload through mindful offers** instead of reflexive agreement.
    
- **The ADHD Effect on Marriage** – _Melissa Orlov_  
    Describes how **overwhelm in relationships** can stem from fear, shame, or miscommunication. Stresses **co-regulation and shared responsibility**.
