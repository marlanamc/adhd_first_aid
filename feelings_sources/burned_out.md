

# 📚 Sources: Burnout & ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of burnout and offer strategies for self-compassion, energy recovery, boundary setting, and reducing overwhelm for ADHD brains running on empty.

---

## 🔋 Understanding Burnout & Energy Depletion

- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Addresses burnout through **self-compassion, energy triage, and visual tools**. Offers exercises like “Recharge Your Batteries” and “Draw Your Feels,” with strong emphasis on emotional validation and guilt reduction.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson_  
    Defines **neurodivergent burnout** and its causes. Offers strategies for **emotional regulation, demand reduction, interoception**, and asking for help — all crucial in ADHD recovery.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Tackles burnout from a **feminist and trauma-informed lens**, affirming that **“guilt is not your compass”** and boundaries are a form of healing, not selfishness.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright_  
    Focuses on **healing shame, embracing joy, and managing distractions**. Encourages using brain dumps, tracking tools, and **verbal processing** to lighten the mental load.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Describes the emotional exhaustion of ADHDers trying to meet unrealistic expectations. Recommends coaching and **whole-person care** for sustainable energy management.

## 🧠 Brain Clutter, Systems, & Recovery

- **Building a Second Brain** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Offers systems to offload cognitive load and avoid **digital burnout**. Encourages “externalizing everything” to reclaim focus and mental clarity.
    
- **The PARA Method** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Expands on the concept of “digital bankruptcy” — a clean-slate system reset that mirrors **brain dumps and radical simplification**.
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Reframes failure as **neutral data**, not shame. Encourages verbalizing and offloading overwhelm as a means to **prevent collapse**. Promotes doing less to unlock more.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** – _Peg Dawson & Richard Guare_  
    Teaches how to **delegate, offload, and manage stress**. Recommends asking for help and using mantras like “I do this as a gift to my future self.”

## 🌱 Tiny Steps, Self-Compassion, and Reframing

- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Provides a blueprint for **starting small** when motivation is gone. Aligns with “baby step” strategies that rebuild momentum gently.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Embraces **intentional imperfection** and “doing the minimum” as acts of curiosity, not failure. Encourages **labeling emotions** to ease internal pressure.
    
- **Feel-Good Productivity** – _Ali Abdaal_

## 💬 Emotions, Guilt & ADHD Recovery

- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Highlights that **resilience is built through connection and self-awareness**, not white-knuckling it alone.
    
- **Healthy Happy ADHD** – _Lisa Dee_  
    Offers lifestyle frameworks for reducing shame and **recharging through nourishment, rest, and fun**.
