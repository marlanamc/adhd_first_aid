# 📚 Sources: Feeling Overstimulated with ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of overstimulation and offer strategies for calming your nervous system, reframing urgency, and creating sensory-safe environments.

---

## 🧠 Why ADHD Gets Overstimulated So Easily

- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Explains how **emotional dysregulation, sensory overload**, and **burnout from masking** can lead to nervous system shutdown or hyperarousal. Offers interoception tools, stimming strategies, and tips for recognizing early overwhelm.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk_  
    Details how trauma alters **amygdala sensitivity** and disrupts **sensory filtering**. Recommends regulating the nervous system through **breath, movement, and touch**.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Clarifies that emotions are **predictive, not reactive**, and overarousal can create **mental rigidity**. Encourages reframing through action and body awareness.
    
- **Scattered Minds** – _Gabor Maté_  
    Explores how ADHD brains often default to **ceaseless stimulation**, chasing **dopamine hits** to avoid discomfort. Connects this to unprocessed pain, grief, and **emotional avoidance**.
    
- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Describes ADHD overstimulation as a state of **mental overload and shutdown**, where too much internal stimulation overwhelms processing. Links to **dopamine/norepinephrine imbalance**.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Notes that many ADHDers live in **chronic stress mode**, driven by **overcompensation and anxiety**. Suggests **brain dumps** and externalization tools for relief.
    
- **It All Makes Sense Now** – _Meredith Carder_  
    Explains how **sensory input varies by environment and health**, and outlines steps for recognizing early signals like irritability or physical agitation. Offers tools like the **“Stimulation Bank Worksheet”**.

## ⚡ Dopamine-Seeking, Adrenaline & Sensory Spiral

- **ADHD 2.0** – _Hallowell & Ratey_  
    Emphasizes the need for **transitions and downtime**, recommending **screen-free wind-down time** and physical movement to regulate stimulation.
    
- **Healthy Happy ADHD** – _Lisa Dee_  
    Discusses the role of **dopamine**, **vagal tone**, and **cortisol** in ADHD overstimulation. Highlights Maté’s concept of **“hyperfunctioning on top of distress.”**
    
- **Brain Brilliance** – _Lucinda Miller_  
    Highlights **noise sensitivity** and the link between nutritional support (e.g., **magnesium, omega-3s**) and nervous system regulation.
    
- **Nutrition for ADHD and Dyslexia** – _Emma Derbyshire_  
    Reviews effects of **caffeine**, **melatonin**, and **magnesium** on stimulation and sleep regulation.

## 🧰 Tools to Regulate, Reframe, and Recover

### 🔎 Recognizing Triggers & Early Warnings

- **The Coaching Habit** – _Michael Bungay Stanier_  
    Encourages identifying **internal triggers** and expanding your response options instead of defaulting to “yes” or overload.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Offers a trauma-informed lens for burnout, anxiety, and overstimulation. Promotes **self-compassion and narrative reframing**.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Explains that **labeling emotions reduces amygdala reactivity** and offers tools like **“Steering Sheets”** to align emotional state with capacity.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Offers grounding tools like **“Draw Your Feels”**, **pep talks**, and permission to say no. Includes **“Stop ‘Should’-ing All Over Yourself.”**
    

### 🧘‍♀️ Sensory Tools & Nervous System Soothing

- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Recommends **quiet rooms**, **noise-canceling headphones**, **sensory-safe spaces**, and **rhythmic breathing**.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _van der Kolk_  
    Advocates **top-down (mindfulness)** and **bottom-up (touch, movement, breath)** strategies to regulate the autonomic nervous system.
    
- **The Neurodiversity Edge** – _Maureen Dunne_  
    Supports **environmental design**: dim lights, scent sprays, quiet zones — not just for ADHDers, but for all.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Explores tools like **infrasonic vibration devices**, **HRV tracking**, and **soothing sound apps** to regulate from within.
    
- **It All Makes Sense Now** – _Meredith Carder_  
    Suggests **weighted blankets**, **showers**, and **walking in nature** to calm physical overstimulation.
    

### 🧠 Reducing Demands, Externalizing, & Getting Support

- **Start Finishing** – _Charlie Gilkey_  
    Recommends stepping back, **revisiting plans with perspective**, and reducing overload by refocusing.
    
- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Offers worksheets like **“Navigating Obstacles”** and **“Emotion Trackers”**, plus scripts to **ask for help** or create **buffer time**.
    
- **How to Keep House While Drowning** – _KC Davis_  
    Grounds strategies in **self-compassion**, and rejects outdated ideas about worth being tied to productivity.
    
- **The Power of Moments** – _Heath & Heath_  
    Emphasizes listening to **individual needs and cues**, showing how validation reduces resistance and stress.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Describes the **information overload loop** in ADHD and underscores the need for **supportive structure and trusted guidance**.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** – _Dawson & Guare_  
    Suggests starting with **smaller ramps** and internalizing the mantra: **“I do this as a gift to my future self.”**
