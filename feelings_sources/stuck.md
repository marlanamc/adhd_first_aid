# 📚 Sources: Feeling Stuck with ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of feeling stuck and offer strategies for emotional processing, reducing overwhelm, regaining control, and taking tiny steps forward — even when nothing feels possible.

---

## 💬 Acknowledge & Process Emotions

- **Feel-Good Productivity** – _Ali Abdaal_  
    Discusses how **self-forgiveness** alleviates guilt and helps restore momentum when stuck in procrastination loops.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Offers tools like **“Draw Your Feels”** and **Vent Space** to safely externalize emotions. Reframes guilt, insecurity, and shutdown into insight and action.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Explores **meltdowns, shutdowns**, and emotional overwhelm in ADHD and autism. Encourages identifying what your emotions are **trying to tell you**.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Recommends **free-writing** and affect labeling as tools to get unstuck. Frames resistance as useful data and explores **emotional agility**.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Stresses that emotions are **“messengers, not dictators.”** Understanding them clearly helps prevent emotional shutdown or paralysis.
    
- **Scattered Minds** – _Gabor Maté_  
    Emphasizes the **awareness-emotion link**, explaining how ADHD can disrupt emotional clarity and movement.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Suggests a **problem-solving log** to reduce the power of runaway emotions and repetitive negative thought loops.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk_  
    Describes how emotions and trauma are **physically embedded**, and how **writing, breathwork, and body awareness** can help process and release stuck energy.
    
- **The Mountain Is You** – _Brianna Wiest_  
    Recommends writing out every frustration to move out of **denial and paralysis** into clarity and movement.

## 🧹 Reduce Demands & Reclaim Control

- **Mad About Money** – _Maddy Alexander-Grout_  
    Supports repetition and simplification — essential when ADHD makes **everything feel too loud or complicated**.
    
- **The Coaching Habit** – _Michael Bungay Stanier_  
    Teaches how to **say no** with clarity and confidence to avoid overcommitment and burnout.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Introduces tools like a **“Commitment List”** and **“digital bankruptcy”** to reset without shame. Affirms that **no one is entitled to your time or attention**.
    
- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Builds systems from the ground up using **tiny, doable actions** that remove the mental wall of perfectionism.
    
- **Building a Second Brain** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Advocates for **externalizing thoughts** so your brain can focus on action, not storage. Offers **permission to archive everything** and start fresh.
    
- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid, or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Offers powerful reframes and practical advice like **“baby steps,” timelines, and project breakdowns** to regain momentum.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Includes **brain dump exercises**, prioritization tools, and frameworks to manage indecision and reduce internal clutter.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Encourages ADHDers to **“feed their strengths”** rather than scatter their energy.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Frames **boundaries and rest** as essential self-care. Reminds readers that **guilt doesn’t mean you’re wrong**.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Offers “**pairing methods**” and playful, exploratory tools for tackling hard or tedious tasks. Helps restore a sense of **agency and curiosity**.
    
- **Succeeding with Adult ADHD** – _Abigail Levrini_  
    Suggests **scaling back** and **building ramps** to meet yourself where you are — not where you wish you were.
    
- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Shares **permission slip worksheets** and strategies to opt out of internalized pressure or busywork.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Offers practical guidance for **choosing yourself** and putting systems in place to meet your needs first.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** – _Dawson & Guare_  
    Encourages **gradual progress** and help-seeking when stuck — with tools to map out **starting points** and build self-trust.

## 🧭 Embrace Curiosity & Trust the Process

- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Reframes stuckness as a call for **curiosity**. Suggests approaching life like an experiment, not a checklist.
    
- **Motivational Interviewing** – _Miller & Rollnick_  
    Emphasizes **hope, curiosity, and partnership** in moving through ambivalence. Describes how emotional support can unlock action.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Explains how **years of masking or shame** make it hard to trust new systems — and why **slow rebuilding** is worth it.
