# 📚 Sources: Feeling Stressed with ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of ADHD-related stress and offer strategies for emotional regulation, body-based relief, prioritization, boundary-setting, and compassionate self-talk — especially when everything feels like too much at once.

---

## 💬 Acknowledge & Validate Your Emotions

- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk_  
    Journaling is shown to improve emotional regulation and physical health by helping individuals **process upsetting events** through story and reflection.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Introduces “morning pages” and emotional agility practices. Notes that **labeling emotions can calm the brain** and that "uncomfortable emotions are not inherently bad."
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Offers tools for **tracking emotions**, **interoceptive awareness**, and exploring what emotions might be trying to communicate.
    
- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid, or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Explains how feelings stem from internal narratives — helping reframe emotional overwhelm.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Recommends **journaling emotional responses** as a tool for clarity and emotional self-awareness.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Encourages **kind and flexible thinking** as a counter to internal pressure. Recasting narratives builds **psychological flexibility**.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Emphasizes that **emotions are messengers**, not dictators. Clarity about feelings leads to better choices under pressure.

## 🧍‍♀️ Move & Ground Your Body

- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk_  
    Groundbreaking work showing how **trauma and stress live in the body**. Explores somatic strategies like yoga, visualization, and deep pressure.
    
- **Healthy Happy ADHD** – _Lisa Dee_  
    Recommends exploring **Peter Levine’s Somatic Experiencing** as a tool for nervous system recovery and stress relief.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Discusses **interoceptive regulation**, grounding, and sensory feedback as core tools for emotional regulation.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Describes **“Physical Zooming”** — the body’s movement patterns affect mood and cognition.

## 🧠 Rethink & Reframe Tasks

- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Demonstrates how **breaking tasks into small steps** makes action more achievable under stress.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Encourages “baby steps” to move through **procrastination and stress paralysis**.
    
- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid, or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Advocates for “**baby steps**” and **forgiveness** when delays happen — especially when overwhelm strikes.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Offers frameworks to reduce tension through structure, **clarity**, and permission to do less.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Notes that last-minute stress can **hijack good planning**, and suggests creating **clear, low-stakes options** in advance.

## 🧹 Reduce Demands & Reclaim a Tiny Sense of Control

- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Recommends **brain dumps** and externalization to reduce internal load.
    
- **Immunity to Change** – _Kegan & Lahey_  
    Uses a **“worry box”** to capture hidden stress and self-sabotaging thoughts.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Includes **brain-dump lists**, reflection tools, and scripts to **say “no”** and reduce external obligations.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Helps assess external demands and reconnect with a sense of **manageable control**.
    

---

## 🛑 Set Boundaries, Reach Out, and Let Go

- **The Coaching Habit** – _Michael Bungay Stanier_  
    Advocates for “**the courage to say No**” and teaches respectful refusal to reduce overload.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Says **guilt isn’t a compass** and helps redefine boundaries as a form of care, not selfishness.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Reframes **asking for help** as a self-compassionate act and explores **why it’s so hard** for neurodivergent people to do so.
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Urges compassion when receiving or offering help, and calls out the shame that **blocks connection** when you’re drowning in stress.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Encourages **seeking help** when stress outpaces internal resources.
    
- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Includes therapist search guides and reflection tools for **emotional overwhelm and recovery**.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Details how long-term **boundary struggles** come from negative experiences and internalized shame — and how to rebuild healthier limits.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Tells stories of individuals who had to **prioritize their own needs** to show up for others without burning out.
