# 📚 Sources: Mental Fog & ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of brain fog and offer strategies for clarity, focus, and function.

---

## 🧠 Executive Function, Attention, and Working Memory

- **ADHD 2.0** by Edward M. Hallowell & John J. Ratey  
    Explores the Default Mode Network (DMN) and how dysregulated focus and mental “drift” fuel fog.
    
- **Driven to Distraction** by Edward M. Hallowell & John J. Ratey  
    A foundational text on ADHD’s impact on attention, impulse control, and inner “tangles” that block mental clarity.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** by Nancy A. Ratey  
    Describes how erratic working memory and wandering attention create overwhelm and fogginess.
    
- **Succeeding With Adult ADHD** by Abigail L. Levrini  
    Explains how executive deficits — especially working memory and metacognition — contribute to distractibility and disorientation.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** by Peg Dawson & Richard Guare  
    Connects stress, poor sleep, and emotional dysregulation to attention struggles and cognitive fatigue.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD** by Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson  
    Covers burnout and overload from executive function difficulties, especially in neurodivergent individuals.
    

---

## 🌪️ Emotional Overwhelm, Shame, and Dysregulation

- **A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD** by Sari Solden, Michelle Frank, & Ellen Littman  
    Explains how emotional reactivity, shame, and gendered masking lead to overwhelm and mental collapse.
    
- **How to ADHD** by Jessica McCabe  
    Breaks down how attention dysregulation, sensory overload, and emotional intensity contribute to brain fog.
    
- **Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them** by Richard & Roxanne Pink  
    Identifies internalized shame and negative self-talk as fog-generating barriers to clarity and action.
    
- **The ADHD Effect on Marriage** by Melissa Orlov  
    Describes “flat” information processing in ADHD — the struggle to filter and prioritize — leading to cognitive paralysis.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** by Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright  
    Frames fog as the natural byproduct of executive overload, especially in emotionally demanding lives.
    

---

## 🍽️ Nutrition, Movement, and Lifestyle

- **Brain Brilliance** by Lucinda Miller  
    Details how gut health and nutrient deficiencies affect mental stamina and cognitive clarity.
    
- **Nutrition for ADHD and Dyslexia** by Emma Derbyshire  
    Explores the gut-brain connection and how “under-fueling” worsens task performance and fog.
    
- **Healthy Happy ADHD** by Lisa Dee  
    Offers lifestyle approaches for regulating dopamine and nervous system balance to reduce brain fog.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** by Bessel van der Kolk  
    Highlights how ADHD brains may have excess slow-wave activity, impairing judgment and clarity.
    
- **100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD**  
    Shares body-based and mindfulness interventions that help improve focus and reduce overwhelm.
    

---

## 🧘 Mindfulness, Systems, and Mental Load

- **6 Essential Mindfulness Practices** by Alexandra Loewe & The ADHD Centre  
    Discusses breathwork and body scans to quiet the mind and support executive regulation.
    
- **Building a Second Brain** by Tiago Forte  
    Offers tools for reducing mental clutter and offloading working memory to restore cognitive bandwidth.
    
- **Order from Chaos** by Jaclyn Paul  
    Connects task friction, attention dysregulation, and internal overwhelm to fog and inertia.
    
- **How to Keep House While Drowning** by KC Davis  
    Reframes mental fog as the result of stress and executive burnout, not laziness or failure.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** by Dani Donovan  
    Advocates “brain dumps” and micro-steps to cut through cognitive paralysis and fog.
    
- **One Weird Trick to Quit Stupid, Timewasting Content**  
    Encourages boredom tolerance and sensory check-ins to quiet overstimulation and restore clarity.
    

---

## 🧩 Identity, Hormones, and Development

- **It All Makes Sense Now** by Meredith Carder  
    Frames fog as a signal of system overload, not a personal flaw — especially during hormonal shifts.
    
- **Mad About Money** by Maddy Alexander-Grout  
    Links hormone fluctuations to fog and confusion, especially in ADHDers during PMS or menopause.
    
- **Scattered Minds** by Gabor Maté  
    Explores how trauma and attachment impact the brain’s ability to regulate thoughts and attention.
    
- **The Neurodiversity Edge** by Maureen Dunne  
    Discusses sensory overwhelm as a cognitive bottleneck and frequent trigger for mental shutdown.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** by Sari Solden  
    Highlights the fog of confusion and masking in women whose ADHD is undiagnosed or misunderstood.
    
- **You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy!** by Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo  
    Explains how mental fog is a neurological shutdown — not a failure of effort or intelligence.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** by James Kustow  
    Offers a “7-pillar” system tackling executive dysfunction, time blindness, and chronic overwhelm.
    

---

## 💡 Practical Guides & Coaching Approaches

- **Productivity With ADHD** by Ju DOMI  
    Explores environmental, physical, and mental clutter as contributors to fog and burnout.
    
- **Breaking Free From ADHD Procrastination** – ADDA  
    Unpacks how dopamine deficits, emotional turbulence, and overwhelm fuel task avoidance and fog.
