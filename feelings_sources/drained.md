# 📚 Sources: Drained & ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of emotional and physical depletion and offer strategies for restoring hope, listening to your body, regulating emotions, and recharging when life feels like too much.

---

## 🪫 Rebuilding Hope & Addressing Hopelessness

- **Feel-Good Productivity** – _Ali Abdaal_  
    Emphasizes **self-forgiveness and momentum-building** to combat hopelessness. Encourages taking one step — any step — to reactivate agency.
    
- **Immunity to Change** – _Robert Kegan & Lisa Lahey_  
    Explores **hidden mental blocks** that reinforce stuckness. Offers methods to dismantle internal resistance and rediscover forward movement.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Speaks directly to those who feel **“fed up and increasingly hopeless.”** Recommends building back self-worth through small, achievable steps.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Frames **hope as a skill**, not a trait. Recommends recasting negative narratives and taking guilt-free actions to build power and emotional clarity.
    
- **The Mountain Is You** – _Brianna Wiest_  
    Centers **self-sabotage and emotional stagnation**. Encourages meeting your hopelessness with reflection, honesty, and action.
    
- **You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo_  
    Validates the painful ADHD journey and reminds readers that **recovery is possible** — and worth the effort.

## ✍️ Reflection, Writing, & Emotional Processing

- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Introduces **free-writing and morning pages** to move through emotional clutter. Frames writing as a compassionate, judgment-free release.
    
- **Motivational Interviewing** – _William R. Miller & Stephen Rollnick_  
    Encourages **interactive journaling** to enhance insight and reduce resistance. Promotes hope as a tool for change.
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Offers practical alternatives to journaling, including **voice memos and verbal processing**, when writing feels hard.
    
- **Expressive Writing: Words That Heal** – _James Pennebaker & John Evans_  
    Demonstrates how writing about trauma, shame, and stress can **boost emotional and physical health**.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk, M.D._  
    Shows how **unprocessed experiences live in the body** and how expression — verbal, written, or artistic — can bring relief and understanding.

## 🧘 Body Awareness, Interoception & Emotional Reframing

- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson_  
    Teaches **interoception** and body-based emotion mapping. Encourages listening to physical cues and reframing emotions as **messengers**.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Explains that **emotions are signals**, not stop signs. Frames interoception as the skill of sensing internal physical and emotional states.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk, M.D._  
    Reinforces that trauma shows up in the body. Stresses the importance of understanding bodily reactions to stress, shutdown, and overwhelm.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Introduces **affective labeling** and **emotional agility**, noting that naming emotions reduces internal chaos and sharpens clarity.

## 🧠 Clearing Clutter with Brain Dumps

- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Features **brain-dump tools** and emotional unloading exercises. Encourages externalizing overwhelm when it’s too heavy to hold inside.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright_  
    Describes **brain dumps** as a key mental decluttering tool. Offers templates for offloading stress and reclaiming focus.
    
- **feelingspages.pdf**  
    Directly suggests brain dumps as a core ADHD-friendly strategy for clearing mental congestion and decision fatigue.

## 🌿 Reducing Demands & Taking Baby Steps

- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Teaches that **small changes lead to big shifts**. Supports the guide’s “baby steps” and “Reitoff day” approach for rebuilding gently.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Encourages **scaling back and creating flexibility**. Prioritizes nervous system support and emotional grace over hyper-productivity.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _Kustow_  
    Recommends gradual **“building-in” of sustainable changes**. Validates the need for pacing and rest in ADHD healing.
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Advocates for **doing less, more intentionally**. Suggests seeking the path of least resistance and letting go of unnecessary struggle.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** – _Dawson & Guare_  
    Recommends starting small and scaling slowly. Introduces mantras and mindset shifts that support **stress reduction and adaptive pacing**.
    
- **You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Emphasizes **baby steps** and the need for a **user-friendly life**, especially when your energy is tapped and your motivation feels gone.
