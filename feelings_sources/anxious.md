# 📚 Sources: Anxiety & ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of anxiety and offer strategies for boundaries, connection, self-compassion, professional support, and calming an ADHD nervous system on high alert.

---

## 🧠 Support Networks & Connection

- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk, M.D._  
    Highlights how **safety in relationships** is the strongest buffer against trauma and anxiety. Being seen and soothed by others is key to calming the nervous system.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson_  
    Frames **asking for help** as a courageous, compassionate act. Affirms that human connection is essential, especially when navigating rejection sensitivity.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Emphasizes the power of **peer support** and feeling validated by others who “get it.”
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Recommends using an **accountability partner** to feel less alone when anxiety blocks action.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Describes **vulnerability and lateral connection** as the human equivalent of a plant’s support system — we’re not meant to grow alone.
    
- **Mad About Money** – _Maddy Alexander-Grout_  
    Shares how seeking help from experts and mental health organizations has been a vital lifeline.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Introduces physician-led mental health platforms and emphasizes **structural care** and support access.
    
- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Offers a curated list of **organizations, videos, and communities** for ADHDers managing emotional overwhelm.

## 🔒 Boundaries & Guilt Management

- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Reminds us that **guilt isn’t a moral compass**. Teaches how to set boundaries clearly — without apology — even when guilt creeps in.
    
- **The Coaching Habit** – _Michael Bungay Stanier_  
    Notes that anxiety often comes from a compulsive “yes” — and learning to **say no** builds clarity and confidence.
    
- **Immunity to Change** – _Robert Kegan & Lisa Lahey_  
    Unpacks how anxiety often masks **unconscious commitments** to avoid perceived risks.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Identifies **emotional pain and shame** as core blockers to asking for help or asserting boundaries.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Addresses the internalized patterns that make **limit-setting feel dangerous** after years of rejection.
    

---

## 🧑‍⚕️ Therapy, Coaching & Professional Support

- **Succeeding With Adult ADHD** – _Abigail L. Levrini_  
    Recommends **coaching or therapy** to address anxiety rooted in ADHD — and offers strategies to recognize when it’s time to reach out.
    
- **A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD** – _Solden, Frank & Littman_  
    Encourages direct, affirming conversations with therapists to **rewrite self-narratives shaped by shame**.
    
- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Highlights how **therapy builds emotional literacy**, making it safer to feel, process, and cope.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Suggests seeking group programs or **ADHD coaches** for stubborn patterns that fuel anxiety.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Asserts that **professional guidance** can be transformative, especially when anxiety feels isolating or confusing.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Clarifies that **self-help ≠ treatment** — and includes guidance for **finding qualified care**.
    
- **Medical News Today** – _Does ADHD Coaching Work?_  
    Summarizes research on **coaching and psychotherapy** as tools for ADHD-related anxiety management.

## 🛏️ Rest & Self-Compassion

- **feelingspages.pdf**  
    “Rest isn’t earned. It’s maintenance.” ADHDers often tie rest to productivity, but anxiety eases when we stop chasing permission to feel safe.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Validates **rest and forgiveness** during burnout. Includes tools like a “Self-Compassion Pep Talk.”
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Encourages **letting go of shame**, reframing mistakes as data — not moral failures.
    
- **Healthy Happy ADHD** – _Lisa Dee_  
    Includes strategies for **nourishing the nervous system** and practicing body-based kindness.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Reinforces the value of **imperfection and grace**, especially when old memories surface.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Reframes boundaries and rest as **compassionate discipline**, not selfishness.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Frames **joy and calm** as part of daily function, not an afterthought. Encourages reducing recovery time from shame spirals.

## 🌿 Strategies for Managing Anxiety

- **Nutrition for ADHD and Dyslexia** – _Emma Derbyshire_  
    Explores how **complex carbs and gut health** impact anxious symptoms.
    
- **Succeeding With Adult ADHD** – _Abigail L. Levrini_  
    Offers techniques like **progressive relaxation**, thought reframing, and acceptance-based strategies for social/test anxiety.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Discusses how to **train the mind** to respond to anxiety instead of spiraling in rumination.
    
- **Immunity to Change** – _Kegan & Lahey_  
    Frames anxiety as a **predictable resistance** to transformation — and how to move through it.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Introduces **affective labeling** and the power of naming vague fear. Notes that **movement relieves anxiety** immediately.
    
- **Brain Brilliance** – _Lucinda Miller_  
    Shares research on **herbal and nutritional support** for calming the nervous system (e.g. magnesium, Bacopa, Passiflora).
    
- **Medical News Today** – _Mindfulness & Meditation for ADHD_  
    Notes that **yoga, meditation, and breathwork** are evidence-based strategies for ADHD-related anxiety.
    
- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Includes worksheets like “**Anxiety Vent Space**” and “**Insecurity List**” to gently unpack judgment spirals.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Acknowledges how ADHD creates a **chronic stress loop** — but with intention and tools, relief is possible.
