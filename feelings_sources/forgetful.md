
# 📚 Sources: Forgetfulness & ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of forgetfulness and offer strategies for supporting working memory, building external systems, reducing overwhelm, and practicing self-compassion while staying organized.

---

## 🧠 Working Memory, Cognitive Load & ADHD

- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo_  
    Describes **working memory “breaks in the chain”** and emphasizes building support systems rather than blaming yourself. Suggests bypassing weak links with tools like cheat sheets and multisensory anchors.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright_  
    Identifies **working memory and decision paralysis** as executive function challenges. Explains how **task-switching overloads memory** and encourages reducing that cognitive load through systems.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson_  
    Explicitly names **working memory deficits** as common for neurodivergent individuals. Encourages externalizing memory and emotional load.
    
- **Succeeding With Adult ADHD** – _Abigail L. Levrini_  
    Connects **metacognition and attention** with memory struggles. Warns that long, jargon-heavy materials often overwhelm ADHD brains.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Frames forgetfulness as the result of **prioritization and memory breakdowns**. Suggests coaching, written tracking, and environmental systems to manage chaos.

## 🗂 Building Better Systems & External Memory

- **Building a Second Brain** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Introduces the idea of an **external brain** to hold ideas, lists, and reminders — relieving cognitive pressure. Emphasizes action-first organization and “good enough” systems.
    
- **The PARA Method** – _Tiago Forte_  
    Shows how digital structure and “just-in-time” organization can protect attention and memory. Offers reassurance that it’s okay to lose track — systems are the safety net.
    
- **Order from Chaos** – _Jaclyn Paul_  
    Advises: **“Assume you will forget everything.”** Promotes **thought containers**, inbox consolidation, and recurring reviews to reduce friction and shame.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Encourages creating a **“single capture point”** and customizing planners to reduce overwhelm. Emphasizes regular reviews and **self-kindness when updating**.
    
- **The Disorganized Mind** – _Nancy A. Ratey_  
    Recommends establishing consistent homes for items and using **written tracking** to externalize cognitive processes.
    
- **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success** – _Dawson & Guare_  
    Introduces **offloading tasks** to technology, lists, or other tools. Validates using reminders and cues to ease the burden on working memory.

## ✍️ Writing Things Down & Reminder Strategies

- **How to ADHD** – _Jessica McCabe_  
    Drives home the message: **“Write it down. No, really. Write it down.”** Offers practical systems like legal pads, visual cues, and frequent calendar checks to overcome forgetfulness.
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _James Kustow_  
    Suggests using margins and reflection space to capture ideas in the moment. Emphasizes **mental clarity through external tools**.
    
- **Productivity With ADHD – Complete Guide** – _Ju DOMI_  
    Offers concrete tools like **calendar integrations, digital reminders, and checklists** for memory support and time awareness.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Includes templates for **brain dumps, checklist creation, and workflow tracking**. Highlights the importance of **visible calendars and review rituals**.
    

---

## 🐾 Baby Steps & Protecting Energy

- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Teaches that **tiny changes reduce resistance**. Ideal for ADHD brains that feel overwhelmed when routines are too rigid or complex.
    
- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Recommends **baby steps** to get unstuck and build confidence. Encourages simplifying rather than trying to “do it all.”
    
- **How to Thrive with Adult ADHD** – _Kustow_  
    Supports gradual implementation of supportive structures, particularly when **energy is low and shame is high**.
    

---

## ⚡ Energy-Conserving Systems & Real-Time Healing

- **The Anti-Planner** – _Dani Donovan_  
    Recommends **digital bankruptcy** and brain dumps to reset without guilt. Encourages enlisting others to help you use your own tools when memory fails.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Frames reframing and rest as tools for power, not failure. Highlights **“healing in real time”** as a worthy, slow, non-linear process.
    
- **A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD** – _Sari Solden, Michelle Frank, Ellen Littman_  
    Validates that **executive dysfunction is not a character flaw**. Encourages maintaining your energy and adapting your environment — not pushing harder.
    
- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Promotes **flexible, resilient systems** that grow with you. Emphasizes reflection without shame and tracking what works over time.
