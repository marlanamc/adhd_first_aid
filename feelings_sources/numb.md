# 📚 Sources: Feeling Numb with ADHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of numbness and offer strategies for identifying emotions, soothing sensory shutdown, navigating burnout, and gently reconnecting to yourself.

---

## 🧠 Why ADHD Can Lead to Numbness

- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Explains how **shutdowns, meltdowns, and emotional overload** often stem from sensory stress and unprocessed feelings. Offers tools for **reconnecting through interoception**, sensory grounding, and building a richer **emotional vocabulary**.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _Bessel van der Kolk_  
    Details how trauma and stress **mute self-sensing brain regions**, leading to **alexithymia** and disconnection. Recommends using **breath, movement, and expressive writing** to restore body awareness and calm the nervous system.
    
- **Do Hard Things** – _Steve Magness_  
    Encourages reframing emotions as **informational signals**, not dictators. Suggests that **clarity on feelings** allows for appropriate, not reactive, responses.
    
- **Tiny Experiments** – _Anne-Laure Le Cunff_  
    Shows how **labeling emotions reduces amygdala activation** and improves self-understanding. Recommends emotional tracking and naming for relief and clarity.

## 🧱 Burnout, Shame & Emotional Shutdown

- **A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD** – _Solden & Frank_  
    Explores how **shame, overgiving, and internalized expectations** deplete energy and lead to numbness. Advocates for **protecting your energy and feeding your strengths**.
    
- **Women with Attention Deficit Disorder** – _Sari Solden_  
    Describes the **slow erosion of self-worth** that causes masking, silence, and a deep **need for emotional protection**.
    
- **Real Self-Care** – _Pooja Lakshmin, M.D._  
    Discusses **burnout, chronic guilt, and perfectionism**, especially in women, and teaches how to reclaim space for your own emotions and needs.
    
- **Small Talk** – _Pink & Pink_  
    Identifies **Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria** as a core ADHD struggle and explains how emotional overwhelm leads to **emotional withdrawal or shutdown**.
    
- **Medical News Today**  
    The article “Does ADHD Coaching Work?” links RSD and chronic emotional pain with **protective disconnection**, validating the guide’s message.

## 🧰 Tools to Reconnect Gently

- **Unapologetically ADHD** – _Kinzer & Wright_  
    Recommends **brain dumps, system resets, and gentle reflection** — viewing missed goals as data, not failure.
    
- **Tiny Habits** – _BJ Fogg_  
    Demonstrates how **micro-actions restore momentum**, showing that even a **30-second win** can reawaken a sense of agency.
    
- **You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!** – _Kelly & Ramundo_  
    Emphasizes **“baby steps”** and the importance of **shame-free reentry** when you've shut down.
    
- **The Power of Moments** – _Chip & Dan Heath_  
    Highlights the healing power of **expressive writing** (via James Pennebaker) and encourages **externalization of emotion** through creative formats like drawing, mapping, or journaling.
    
- **The Body Keeps the Score** – _van der Kolk_  
    Cites Pennebaker’s work on expressive writing as a method to **access and release frozen emotions**. Also notes how **breathing and movement** activate the vagus nerve for calming.
    
- **The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook** – _Kemp & Mitchelson_  
    Suggests creating a **“grounding island”** — a space with familiar, sensory-soothing objects. Recommends **matching emotional labels to body cues** to restore awareness and engagement.
