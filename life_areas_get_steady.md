    Reduce overwhelm before brain-dumping tasks.  
    **Try:** Take 3 slow breaths before writing anything down.
    
2. 📍 **Stage your list tools**  
    Keep pen, paper, or app ready and visible.  
    **Try:** Leave your list open in a pinned tab or on your desk.
    
3. ⏳ **Set a short list-making window**  
    Avoid overthinking by limiting the time.  
    **Try:** Give yourself 5–10 minutes to make the list.
    
4. 🗂 **Start with top priorities**  
    Identify the most urgent or important first.  
    **Try:** Star or highlight your top three tasks.
    
5. 🎶 **Anchor with a sound cue**  
    Create a mental signal for list-making time.  
    **Try:** Play a short playlist you only use for planning.
    
6. ✅ **End with action clarity**  
    Make sure every item has a clear next step.  
    **Try:** Rewrite vague tasks (“work on project”) into specifics (“outline 3 slides”).

### **🗑 Trash & Recycling**

1. 🫁 **Ground before you start**  
    Reduce avoidance by calming your body.  
    **Try:** Inhale for 4, exhale for 6, three times.
    
2. 📍 **Stage supplies**  
    Have bags, bins, and gloves ready.  
    **Try:** Place them by the door before starting.
    
3. ⏳ **Set a short removal window**  
    Keep it manageable and quick.  
    **Try:** Give yourself a 10-minute limit.
    
4. 🗂 **Start with one bin or room**  
    Avoid decision fatigue by narrowing the scope.  
    **Try:** Empty only the kitchen trash first.
    
5. 🎶 **Add momentum with music**  
    Use energy-boosting sound to keep moving.  
    **Try:** Play an upbeat 2–3 song playlist.
    
6. ✅ **End with visual completion**  
    Notice and acknowledge the cleared space.  
    **Try:** Stand back and take in the result before moving on.

### **💼 Work Tasks**

1. 🫁 **Center before starting**  
    Calm your mind so you can focus.  
    **Try:** Take three slow breaths before opening your task list.
    
2. 📍 **Stage work tools**  
    Have all needed documents and apps open.  
    **Try:** Close unrelated tabs to reduce distractions.
    
3. ⏳ **Pick a focused work window**  
    Set a clear start and stop time.  
    **Try:** Use a 25-minute Pomodoro block.
    
4. 🗂 **Start with a small, clear win**  
    Boost momentum by finishing one easy task.  
    **Try:** Reply to one short email or update one spreadsheet cell.
    
5. 🎶 **Anchor with background noise**  
    Choose sound that helps you stay in the zone.  
    **Try:** Use instrumental music or a focus playlist.
    
6. ✅ **End with next steps noted**  
    Make it easy to re-enter the task later.  
    **Try:** Leave yourself a sticky note with your next action.

### **📧 Writing an Email**

1. 🫁 **Pause before typing**  
    Lower anxiety and improve clarity.  
    **Try:** One minute of deep breathing before drafting.
    
2. 📍 **Stage your info**  
    Have all details, files, and addresses ready.  
    **Try:** Gather attachments before you open the email.
    
3. ⏳ **Use a short drafting window**  
    Prevent over-editing and avoidance.  
    **Try:** Give yourself 10 minutes to write a first draft.
    
4. 🗂 **Start with the core point**  
    Make your message clear from the start.  
    **Try:** Write your main sentence before anything else.
    
5. 🎶 **Anchor focus with sound**  
    Choose a calming background sound.  
    **Try:** Play light instrumental music while you type.
    
6. ✅ **End with a send-ready check**  
    Avoid forgetting attachments or key points.  
    **Try:** Read once for clarity, attach files, and send immediately.
