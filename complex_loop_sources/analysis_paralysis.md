## 📚 Sources for Analysis Paralysis

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD fuels overwhelm, overthinking, perfectionism, and shutdown — and offer strategies like externalization, small steps, mindset shifts, and compassionate systems to help ADHD brains move from stuck to started.

---

### 🧠 Understanding ADHD & Paralysis

**ADHD 2.0**  
_Edward M. Hallowell, John J. Ratey (2017)_  
➤ Describes ADHD as a “Ferrari brain with bicycle brakes,” underscoring the importance of **self-regulation strategies** to overcome overwhelm, distraction, and shutdown.

**Driven to Distraction**  
_Edward M. Hallowell, John J. Ratey (1994)_  
➤ Offers foundational insight into ADHD across the lifespan, helping readers understand how chronic overwhelm and inaction stem from **under-addressed ADHD symptoms**.

**Scattered Minds**  
_Gabor Maté (1999)_  
➤ A trauma-informed, holistic look at ADHD — explores **how developmental and emotional factors interact with executive dysfunction** to create long-standing patterns of paralysis.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
_Nancy A. Ratey (2008)_  
➤ Focuses on **compensating for weak executive function** — like erratic memory, disorganization, and poor prioritization — through **personalized systems and creativity**.

**The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success**  
_Peg Dawson, Richard Guare (2015)_  
➤ Shares practical strategies for **working memory, task initiation, and self-control**, using checklists and structured decision-making to counter paralysis.

### 🧰 Strategies for Getting Unstuck

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
_Abigail L. Levrini (2025)_  
➤ Breaks down paralysis using **process-based goals**, “procrastination styles,” and **small, achievable steps**. Emphasizes progress over perfection.

**Order from Chaos**  
_Jaclyn Paul (2018)_  
➤ Advocates for **externalization and environmental design**. Includes “Containment Before Control” to manage mental clutter and task overwhelm.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
_James Kustow (2024)_  
➤ Offers a **seven-pillar plan** with a gentle, structured approach to breaking down tasks, identifying triggers, and gradually building follow-through.

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju Domi (2025)_  
➤ Promotes working _with_ ADHD traits — using **personalized environments, hyperfocus awareness, and self-care systems** to reduce overwhelm.

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
_KC Davis (2022)_  
➤ Brings in radical **self-compassion** and task simplification to reduce shame-driven shutdowns around daily responsibilities.

**Tiny Habits**  
_BJ Fogg (2019)_  
➤ Teaches how to **build momentum through tiny, low-pressure actions** — a proven way to bypass overthinking and perfection paralysis.

**Tiny Experiments**  
_Anne-Laure Le Cunff (2025)_  
➤ Targets perfectionism and over-optimization by encouraging **“intentional imperfection”** and embracing completion over polish.

**The PARA Method**  
_Tiago Forte (2023)_  
➤ A system to **declutter digital and mental space**, reduce decision fatigue, and resist “completionism” that stalls progress.

**Building a Second Brain**  
_Tiago Forte (2022)_  
➤ Reframes productivity around **external storage, creative incubation, and non-linear thinking**, reducing overwhelm from scattered ideas.

### 💡 Mindset Shifts & Emotional Roadblocks

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden, Michelle Frank, Ellen Littman (2019)_  
➤ Emphasizes **emotional healing, shame resilience**, and recognizing inner barriers that freeze action.

**Women with Attention Deficit Disorder**  
_Sari Solden (1995, 2013)_  
➤ Focuses on **structure, support, and strengths-based reframing** for women stuck in cycles of disorganization and emotional overload.

**You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
_Kate Kelly, Peggy Ramundo (1995, 2006)_  
➤ Validates ADHD experiences and offers tools like **ADD-friendly meditation and flexible routines** to reduce overwhelm and internalized blame.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer, Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Tackles **perfectionism, time blindness, and emotional dysregulation**, providing a framework for breaking down tasks and restoring momentum.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies**  
_Richard Pink, Roxanne Pink (2024)_  
➤ Debunks self-critical narratives and offers reframes to **disentangle self-worth from productivity**.

**The Neurodiversity Edge**  
_Maureen Dunne (2024)_  
➤ Links **sensory overload** to shutdown and decision fatigue — reminding readers to manage the environment as part of getting unstuck.

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
_Jennifer Kemp, Monique Mitchelson (2024)_  
➤ Offers gentle, **step-by-step tools rooted in self-compassion**, to build tolerance for discomfort and slowly increase task activation.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
_Lisa Dee (2025)_  
➤ Challenges “fix-it” culture and offers support for **trusting your instincts and resisting pressure to optimize everything**.

**Mad About Money**  
_Maddy Alexander-Grout (2025)_  
➤ Combats financial paralysis by breaking decisions into **micro-questions** and reducing overwhelm with **simple self-care prompts**.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
_Meredith Carder (2024)_  
➤ Encourages **“imperfect action” and brain dumps** to bypass mental traffic and unlock momentum through templates and automation.
