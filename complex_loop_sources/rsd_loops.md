## Sources for RSD Loops

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD magnifies Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD) — making perceived criticism feel unbearable, sparking shame spirals, and trapping you in loops of avoidance and overthinking. They also offer strategies rooted in emotional regulation, radical acceptance, and self-compassion to help you interrupt these cycles.

---

### 💔 Understanding RSD and ADHD

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
Nikki Kinzer, Pete D. Wright (2024)  
➤ Explains RSD as “intense emotional pain” from real or perceived rejection. Links it to ADHD shame spirals and low self-worth. Emphasizes **radical acceptance** and **reducing recovery time** from emotional spirals.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them**  
Richard Pink, Roxanne Pink (2024)  
➤ Calls RSD “one of the worst symptoms of ADHD,” where you believe “everyone secretly hates you.” Urges dismantling **toxic core beliefs** and building a foundation of **self-acceptance** and support.

**One Weird Trick to Quit Stupid, Timewasting Content**  
(Author & Year N/A)  
➤ Frames RSD as something to understand and work around — not use as an excuse. Encourages building **boredom tolerance** and emotional strength rather than relying on avoidance behaviors like doomscrolling.

### 🧠 Emotional Regulation & Compassionate Tools

**How to ADHD**  
Jessica McCabe (2024)  
➤ Describes RSD as part of **emotional dysregulation**. Emphasizes **self-compassion** and creating visibility for invisible obstacles. Recommends a “menu of tools” rather than one-size-fits-all fixes.

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
Jennifer Kemp, Monique Mitchelson (2024)  
➤ Encourages **self-compassion when facing perceived criticism**, challenging unrealistic expectations, and recognizing the link between RSD and burnout. Promotes sustainable emotional regulation tools.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
Meredith Carder (2024)  
➤ Lists RSD as a challenge driven by **shame, avoidance, and perceived failure**. Advises interrupting loops through **intentional rest** and reframing coping behaviors like phone scrolling.
