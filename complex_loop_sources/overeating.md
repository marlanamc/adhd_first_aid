## 🍽️ Sources for Overeating

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD affects hunger cues, impulse control, emotional regulation, and eating patterns — and offer strategies like fueling consistently, using visual cues, adding structure, and practicing mindfulness to reduce overeating and support a more regulated relationship with food.

---

### 🧠 Hunger, Hyperfocus & Fueling Gaps

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
_Meredith Carder (2024)_  
➤ ADHD hyperfocus disrupts **hunger/thirst perception**, leading to erratic fueling and rebound overeating. Recommends **visible, nutrient-dense snacks**, scheduled reminders, and seeking **professional support for disordered eating**.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
_Nancy A. Ratey (2008)_  
➤ Missed meals from hyperfocus lead to **fatigue and blood sugar crashes** that drive overeating. Suggests **scheduling breaks** to restore energy and improve regulation.

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD or Other Disorders**  
_(Author/Date Not Specified)_  
➤ Suggests **regular, protein-rich meals** to prevent skipping meals and **mindless overeating** later in the day.

### 💥 Impulsivity, Inattention & Overeating

**Nutrition for ADHD and Dyslexia**  
_Emma Derbyshire (2025)_  
➤ ADHDers may **overeat due to distractibility and poor attention** while eating. Recommends **minimizing electronics, eating slowly, chewing thoroughly, and mindful conversation** during meals to stay present.

**The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success**  
_Peg Dawson & Richard Guare (2015)_  
➤ Links overeating to **response inhibition** challenges. Suggests using **food journals**, **daily weighing**, and **delaying gratification** (“I’ll have it later”) to build self-awareness and impulse control.

**Does ADHD Coaching Work?**  
_Medical News Today (2023)_  
➤ Lists **overeating** as a recognized symptom linked to ADHD, underscoring its neurological rather than moral origin.

### 🧘‍♀️ Emotional Eating & Self-Regulation

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
_Lisa Dee (2025)_  
➤ Notes **mindless eating and disordered eating** are more prevalent in ADHD women. Recommends working with **health professionals familiar with ADHD + eating disorders** for integrated care.

**Real Self-Care**  
_Pooja Lakshmin, MD (2023)_  
➤ Recommends **Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)** for addressing emotional dysregulation and patterns like binge eating. Supports **mindfulness and emotional regulation** as essential tools.
