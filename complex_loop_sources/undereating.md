## Sources for Undereating

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack why ADHD brains may forget to eat — from hyperfocus and poor interoception to appetite suppression from medication — and offer strategies to support consistent, nourishing, ADHD-friendly fueling routines.

---

### 🍳 Irregular Fueling, Forgetting to Eat & Hyperfocus

**How to ADHD**  
Jessica McCabe (2024)  
➤ Discusses **erratic fueling patterns** due to hyperfocus and sensory issues. Recommends **visible snacks**, **calendar reminders**, and **"practical hunger"** — eating before you crash. Also names **ARFID** as a possible diagnosis in ADHDers.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
Meredith Carder (2024)  
➤ Undereating is framed as an effect of **hyperfocus and unreliable hunger cues**. Offers the same strategies: **visible, nutrient-dense snacks**, **reminder-based eating**, and embracing **"something is better than nothing"** on low-energy days.

**Nutrition for ADHD and Dyslexia**  
Emma Derbyshire (2025)  
➤ Notes ADHDers may **"forget to eat" or never sit down to do so**, especially when constantly on the go or in hyperfocus. Recommends **scheduling meals** and **stocking key foods** to reduce planning overwhelm.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
James Kustow (2024)  
➤ Encourages **simplifying nutrition**, noting that planning and prepping every meal isn’t realistic. Prioritizes **a “solid nutritional grounding”** to support the ADHD brain.

### 🍫 Protein, Practicality & Simple Systems

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD or Other Disorders**  
ADHD Success (2025)  
➤ Directly recommends **scheduled, protein-rich meals** to avoid both skipping meals and overeating later.

**Productivity With ADHD – Complete Guide**  
Ju DOMI (2025)  
➤ Links **fueling the brain** to executive function. Recommends **“minimal version” habits** like healthy snacking and simple meal prep for low-energy days.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
Lisa Dee (2025)  
➤ Encourages **simplifying your relationship with food**. Notes that some ADHDers unintentionally suppress appetite during stress or use food for stimulation rather than nourishment.

### 💊 Medication, Appetite Loss & Disordered Patterns

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
Abigail L. Levrini (2025)  
➤ Notes **loss of appetite as a common stimulant side effect**, encouraging collaboration with a provider to find the right dose without sacrificing nourishment.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
Nancy A. Ratey (2008)  
➤ Acknowledges appetite loss from medication and encourages integrating **nutrition as a core part of ADHD management**.

**Women with Attention Deficit Disorder**  
Sari Solden (2013)  
➤ Links **starvation to self-stimulation** in some ADHDers. Recommends **protein-based breakfasts** and regular fueling as **core treatment pillars**, especially for women.
