## 📚 Sources for Masking Exhaustion

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack the emotional and physical toll of masking — the act of concealing ADHD symptoms to appear neurotypical — and offer strategies rooted in radical acceptance, unmasking, self-compassion, and realistic life design to help ADHDers reclaim their energy and authenticity.

---

### 🧠 Why Masking is So Exhausting

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden, Michelle Frank, Ellen Littman (2019)_  
➤ Defines masking as the cycle of **“hiding” and “overexerting” to pass for neurotypical**, leading to burnout. Emphasizes honoring your **tipping points**, avoiding unrealistic expectations, and embracing **authentic neurodivergent expression** as the antidote to chronic overwhelm.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
_Lisa Dee (2025)_  
➤ Frames masking as **“pushing past your limits to fit in”**, which keeps the nervous system in **a chronic stress state**. Encourages seeking **accommodations and support** without shame and rejecting the idea that ADHD needs to be “hidden” to be valid.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
_Meredith Carder (2024)_  
➤ Describes masking as a cause of **mental and physical exhaustion**, including **headaches, irritability, and low patience**. Recommends honoring **natural energy rhythms** and building ADHD-compatible routines instead of forcing yourself to operate neurotypically.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies**  
_Richard Pink, Roxanne Pink (2024)_  
➤ Defines masking as **“pretending to be neurotypical”** and links it to internalized beliefs like “I am lazy” or “I am a bad person.” Suggests dismantling shame through **radical self-acceptance**, redefining “hard work,” and rejecting the pressure to constantly self-correct.

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
_Jennifer Kemp, Monique Mitchelson (2024)_  
➤ Directly connects **masking and burnout**, noting that long-term compensation for executive function challenges is **unsustainable**. Advocates for **compassionate unmasking**, prioritizing energy management and safe, authentic self-expression.

### 💡 Strategies for Energy Protection & Authenticity

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer, Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Highlights how **overcompensating and perfectionism** are forms of masking rooted in shame. Emphasizes that ADHDers are **not broken**, and encourages adjusting life systems to fit the brain _you have_, not the brain you think you “should” have.

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju Domi (2025)_  
➤ While not explicitly about masking, reinforces that **operating beyond your capacity** depletes mental resources. Encourages **self-care as foundational** to productivity, suggesting that trying to “keep up” without rest will always lead to burnout.
