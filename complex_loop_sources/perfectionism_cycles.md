## 💎 Sources for Perfectionism Cycles

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain how perfectionism shows up with ADHD — from paralysis and overthinking to toxic expectations and shame — and offer compassionate strategies like radical acceptance, intentional imperfection, and progress over perfection.

---

### 🧠 How ADHD Fuels Perfectionism

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
_Abigail L. Levrini (2025)_  
➤ Perfectionism is a **top cognitive distortion** in ADHD, especially "back-end" perfectionism (rigid standards that cause paralysis). Recommends **realistic goals**, time limits, and shifting from “perfect” to **progress**.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
_Meredith Carder (2024)_  
➤ Labels it **toxic perfectionism** — the push for an idealized life or task leads to **burnout and avoidance**. Advocates for **"imperfect action"**, curiosity, and the reminder that **you don’t have to be perfect at addressing perfectionism**.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Explains how **absolute thinking and overthinking** create setup paralysis. Promotes **radical acceptance** over self-fixing. “Perfect doesn’t exist” — **recovery plans > flawless systems**.

**How to ADHD**  
_Jessica McCabe (2024)_  
➤ Perfectionism keeps ADHDers **stuck in loops** or stops them from starting. Pushes back against the idea of acting “normal” — encourages functionality that aligns with **how your brain works**.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies**  
_Richard Pink & Roxanne Pink (2024)_  
➤ Perfectionism kills projects out of **fear of criticism**. Recommends a **“vent space” for perfectionist thoughts** and the reminder: **“Done is better than perfect.”** Change core beliefs first.

### 💬 Self-Compassion & Practical Alternatives

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook**  
_Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson (2024)_  
➤ Perfectionism often means **chasing others' ideals**. Encourages trying **one or two strategies at a time**, valuing done over ideal, and protecting energy from burnout.

**Tiny Experiments**  
_Anne-Laure Le Cunff (2025)_  
➤ Lists **multiple faces of perfectionism** — fear of mistakes, high standards, overthinking. Suggests **intentional imperfection** as a tool to create without burning out.

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
_KC Davis (2022)_  
➤ Uses Dr. Kristin Neff’s work on **self-compassion** to challenge perfectionism and build kinder self-talk into ADHD management.

**The Anti-Planner**  
_Dani Donovan (2022)_  
➤ Tackles **planning paralysis** caused by perfectionism. Emphasizes **small, messy action** over flawless systems.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
_Lisa Dee (2025)_  
➤ Warns against unsustainable “achievement streaks” like strict routines or diets. Encourages **accepting the ebb and flow**, and remembering that **“speed bumps aren’t roadblocks.”**

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD**
