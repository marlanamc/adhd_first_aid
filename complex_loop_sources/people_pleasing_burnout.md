## 🧍‍♀️ Sources for People-Pleasing Burnout

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD traits like rejection sensitivity, impulsive yeses, perfectionism, and shame can fuel a cycle of overcommitting, masking, and crashing — and offer strategies like boundary setting, radical self-acceptance, and energy-aware planning to break free from burnout.

---

### 🧠 Boundaries, Overcommitment & Burnout

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
_Meredith Carder (2024)_  
➤ ADHDers struggle with **saying no** due to in-the-moment thinking and perfectionism. Recommends building **“simple guardrails”** to limit overcommitment and redefining capacity to **break the burnout cycle**.

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden, Michelle Frank & Ellen Littman (2019)_  
➤ Emphasizes that ADHD women often **burn out trying to pass for neurotypical**. Encourages embracing difference, recognizing tipping points, and living authentically to reduce chronic overexertion.

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
_Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson (2024)_  
➤ Defines **neurodivergent burnout** and stresses that overextending to please others drains internal resources. Urges **assertive communication**, early burnout awareness, and rejecting worth = productivity narratives.

**Mad About Money**  
_Maddy Alexander-Grout (2025)_  
➤ Recommends adjusting **boundaries when overwhelmed**, and encourages transparency about ADHD needs to avoid stress from people-pleasing or overworking.

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD**  
_(Author/Date Not Specified)_  
➤ Core advice: **“Set boundaries and say no to avoid overcommitting.”** This simple shift protects energy and prevents burnout.

### 🔥 Perfectionism, Shame & Internal Pressure

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ ADHD planning is a **“high-calorie burn activity”** — the book advocates **radical acceptance**, energy-aware systems, and letting go of idealized expectations to **prevent shame spirals and crash cycles**.

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
_Abigail L. Levrini (2025)_  
➤ Identifies **perfectionism as a cognitive distortion** that drives overwork and anxiety. Recommends reframing rigid systems and identifying when stress stems from internalized expectations.

**The Anti-Planner**  
_Dani Donovan (2022)_  
➤ Suggests **self-forgiveness and compassion** as antidotes to the internal criticism that often fuels people-pleasing and performance pressure.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
_Nancy A. Ratey (2008)_  
➤ Encourages letting go of **shame around support needs**, and embracing delegation to avoid overextending due to perfectionist or people-pleasing tendencies.

**Tiny Experiments**  
_Anne-Laure Le Cunff (2025)_  
➤ Warns that constant **“relentless execution”** leads to burnout. Proposes **intentional imperfection** as a healthier path — good work ≠ perfect work.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies**  
_Richard Pink & Roxanne Pink (2024)_  
➤ Addresses core beliefs like **“I must make up for being lazy”**, which drive self-punishing overcommitment. Advocates for **changing beliefs first**, then behavior, to prevent burnout.
