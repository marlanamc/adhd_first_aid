## 📚 Sources for Constantly Tired

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD contributes to chronic fatigue — through sleep disruption, energy mismanagement, hyperfocus crashes, masking, nutritional deficiencies, and executive dysfunction — and offer strategies like movement, rest, sleep hygiene, energy-based scheduling, and compassionate reframing to support a more sustainable rhythm.

---

### 🧠 Understanding ADHD Fatigue

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
_Jennifer Kemp, Monique Mitchelson (2024)_  
➤ Defines **neurodivergent burnout** as a mismatch between demands and coping capacity. Highlights **sensory stressors, organizational complexity, and masking** as drivers of exhaustion. Suggests medication can reduce overwhelm and **ease task initiation and mental strain**.

**ADHD 2.0**  
_Edward M. Hallowell, John J. Ratey (2021)_  
➤ Frames **exercise as essential medicine** and encourages **sleep hygiene and screen curfews** to prevent overstimulation. Recommends checking for **sleep apnea**, and supports **omega-3s** for brain health and energy.

**How to ADHD**  
_Jessica McCabe (2024)_  
➤ Acknowledges **excessive daytime sleepiness** and disrupted sleep as common in ADHD. Offers tips for **stimulant timing**, **wind-down rituals**, and accepting **rest as productive**, even without full sleep.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
_James Kustow (2024)_  
➤ Explains how **chronic sleep debt** worsens symptoms and physical health. Shares strategies for managing **sleep disturbance**, especially in hyperactive/combined presentations, and touches on **nutrition for brain support**.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
_Lisa Dee (2025)_  
➤ Introduces 8 pillars for well-being, including **movement, protein intake, simplified food choices**, and **nervous system regulation**. Warns that **phone/email use can exhaust the brain early**, and promotes sustainable, ADHD-friendly energy systems.

### 🍳 Fueling Your Body & Brain

**Brain Brilliance**  
_Lucinda Miller (2024)_  
➤ Connects **gut health, immunity, and mood**. Recommends addressing **nutrient deficiencies** (e.g., iron, B12, zinc, folate) to improve energy, brain function, and reduce fatigue.

**Nutrition for ADHD and Dyslexia**  
_Emma Derbyshire (2025)_  
➤ Frames **fatigue as a brain-fuel issue**. Suggests optimizing intake of **omega-3s, magnesium, iron, and vitamin D**, especially in populations that **work harder to concentrate**.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
_Meredith Carder (2024)_  
➤ Connects **hyperfocus crashes and erratic fueling** to burnout. Offers strategies like **stocking snacks, building eating routines**, and addressing revenge bedtime procrastination that erodes rest.

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden, Michelle Frank (2019)_  
➤ Advocates for **protein-rich meals, better sleep hygiene**, and **rest as a form of productivity**. Encourages taking breaks and giving the brain space to recover.

### 🔋 Energy Management & Recovery

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju Domi (2025)_  
➤ Reframes self-care as **foundational to productivity**. Suggests **energy-based scheduling**, **restoration breaks**, and **environmental optimization** to avoid mental depletion.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
_Nancy A. Ratey (2008)_  
➤ Describes how **executive dysfunction and hyperfocus** cause energy crashes. Recommends **structured breaks** and personalized planning to offset fatigue.

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
_Abigail L. Levrini (2025)_  
➤ Emphasizes that **perfectionism and poor boundaries** can lead to burnout. Recommends working in **small focused chunks with breaks** to reduce mental drain.

**You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
_Kate Kelly, Peggy Ramundo (2006)_  
➤ Explores **burnout from unrealistic expectations** and time misjudgments. Offers **meditation techniques** and emphasizes that **ADHD medication can act as mental fuel** to reduce fatigue. Addresses **hormonal shifts and their impact on energy**, particularly in women.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies**  
_Richard Pink, Roxanne Pink (2024)_  
➤ Challenges beliefs like “I’m just lazy.” Advocates for redefining “hard work” to **include rest**, and points out that **stress and shame worsen energy crashes**.
