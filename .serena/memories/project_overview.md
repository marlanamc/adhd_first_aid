# ADHD First Aid Kit - Project Overview

## Purpose
An ADHD-focused web application designed to provide evidence-based crisis management strategies, coping mechanisms, and support tools specifically for people with ADHD. The platform emphasizes ADHD-first design principles including:
- Cognitive load management
- Visual hierarchy and clear navigation
- Crisis prevention and immediate intervention
- Personalized strategy recommendations
- Community support and expert validation

## Target Users
- People with ADHD experiencing crisis situations
- Family members and caregivers
- ADHD coaches and therapists
- Workplace managers seeking ADHD-supportive strategies

## Key Content Types
- **Complex Loops**: Behavioral patterns (analysis paralysis, bedtime procrastination)
- **Feelings**: Emotional states and coping strategies
- **Barriers**: Common obstacles and solutions
- **Identities**: Identity-specific content (ADHD, neurodivergent)
- **Tasks**: Task-based strategies
- **Life Areas**: Content organized by life domains

## Platform Philosophy
ADHD-first design means building specifically for executive dysfunction, sensory processing differences, and attention regulation challenges. Every feature considers cognitive load, distraction reduction, and accessibility.
