
### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Analysis Paralysis

Pick speed over certainty.  
**Try one of these:**

- 2️⃣ Limit to **two** viable options; flip a coin if tied.
    
- ⏳ Give yourself **90 seconds** to choose.
    
- ✅ Use the rule: “Choose the option that’s easiest to start.”
    
- 🧱 Do the **first brick** for 3 minutes, then reassess.
    
- 🗂️ Park all other choices on a “Decide Friday” list.

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Bedtime Procrastination

Future-you needs a landing strip.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🌙 Set a **power-down alarm** + put phone in another room.
    
- 🛁 Do a 5-minute wind-down: lights low, face wash, teeth, PJs.
    
- 📺 Trade doom scroll for one calm video or podcast, timer on.
    
- 🛏️ Get in bed _even if you’re not sleepy_; read 10 pages.
    
- 🗓️ Ask: “What am I avoiding tomorrow?” Write it down, close it.

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Can’t Fall Asleep

Calm the body, not the thoughts.  
**Try one of these:**

- 💨 4–7–8 breathing x 4 rounds.
    
- 🧊 Cold face splash or cool the room.
    
- 📝 Write your worry list; promise yourself you’ll handle it tomorrow.
    
- 🎧 Brown noise/boring audiobook, 15-minute timer.
    
- 🛏️ If awake after 20 minutes, get up and do a low-light routine for 10.

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Chronic Lateness

Shrink departure friction.  
**Try one of these:**

- ⏰ Set a **leave-by** alarm with a 10-minute buffer.
    
- 🎒 Pack the essentials only: keys/wallet/phone/meds/water.
    
- 🚪 Put shoes and bag by the door now.
    
- 🧭 Decide **first step out the door** (call elevator, start car).
    
- 📝 Text “Running 10 behind—see you at __.”

- 💤 15-minute horizontal rest (no phone).

- 🗓️ Cancel or defer one non-essential thing.

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Decision Overwhelm

Constrain the menu.  
**Try one of these:**

- 3️⃣ Keep **three** options: A/B/C. Cross out the rest.
    
- 🧭 Choose by single value: cheapest / fastest / kindest-to-me.
    
- ⏳ 2-minute timer: decide, then move.
    
- 🔁 If reversible → decide now; if irreversible → book a 24h review.
    
- 🗂️ Create defaults (e.g., Wednesday = leftovers).

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Difficult Conversations

Safety + script.  
**Try one of these:**

- 📝 Write a 3-line script: _“I feel… I need… Are you open to…?”_
    
- ⏳ Schedule a time; no ambush talks.
    
- 🧊 Ice wrist/face for 30 seconds before you start.
    
- 🎯 Name the goal: clarity, not victory.
    
- 🧠 If flooded, say: “I need a pause; I’ll come back at __.”
