
### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Already Failed

You didn’t ruin it. You paused. Pauses can end.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🔁 Say: “Reset, not restart.”
    
- 🕐 Choose a re-start time (e.g., 3:15 pm) and set a timer.
    
- 🧽 Do a 60-second reset (clear desk, close tabs, water).
    
- 📄 Write one line: “Next right step is ___.”
    
- 📱Text someone: “I’m jumping back in for 10 minutes. Hold me to it?”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Can’t Start

Starting is the hard part. Make it microscopic.  
**Try one of these:**

- ▶️ Do a **20-second action** (open doc, put shoes on, get bin).
    
- ⏳ Set a **2-minute timer** — quit after if you want.
    
- 🧩 Say: “Just the first puzzle piece.”
    
- 🎯 Move the task into view (place item in doorway/desk).
    
- 🗣️ Whisper: “I start messy, then improve.”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Can’t Stay Focused

Your attention is slipping, not your worth.  
**Try one of these:**

- ⏱️ Work in **8–10 minute sprints**; break 2 minutes.
    
- 🧹 Remove **one** distraction (put phone in a drawer).
    
- 🎧 Turn on brown/white noise.
    
- 📋 Write a **mini-to-do** with 3 checkboxes only.
    
- 🚶 Stand, stretch, sip water, sit — then resume.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Don’t Have Energy

You need a spark, not discipline.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🔋 Eat or drink something with protein/electrolytes.
    
- 🌬️ 4 breaths in, 6 out — 5 rounds.
    
- 🧦 Change into “work mode” clothes.
    
- 🧱 Do a **1-brick task**: one email, one plate, one line.
    
- 💤 If you’re wiped: 10-minute horizontal rest, then try again.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Don’t Have Time

Do less on purpose — on your terms.  
**Try one of these:**

- ✂️ Cut scope in half. Then cut it again.
    
- ⏰ Pick a hard stop time and set an alarm.
    
- 🧭 Decide the **one outcome** that actually matters.
    
- 🔁 Default to “good enough” template or last version.
    
- 📦 Defer or delete one non-essential piece _right now_.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Don’t Know How

You don’t need mastery to begin; you need a first move.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🔍 Write: “What’s unclear?” → list 3 questions.
    
- 🎥 Search one 5-minute tutorial and follow the first step only.
    
- 🧱 Build a dumb prototype: wrong is fine, visible is better.
    
- 🗣️ Ask for a pointer: “What would you do first?”
    
- 📑 Copy a similar example and modify.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Don’t Know Where to Start

Start anywhere small and true.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🧭 Pick the **start line**: materials, document, or space.
    
- 🗺️ Draw a 5-box map: Step 1 → Step 5. Fill in _only_ Step 1.
    
- 🎯 Choose the **Most Obvious First Action** (MOFA).
    
- ⏳ 5-minute timer: gather tools only.
    
- 🧠 Say: “Action reveals order.”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Feel Alone

You’re allowed to be supported.  
**Try one of these:**

- 📱Send a “body-double?” text to one person.
    
- 🖥️ Turn on a study-with-me video/lofi stream.
    
- 🧭 Tell someone your next 10-minute goal.
    
- 🧣 Put on something cozy — signal care to your nervous system.
    
- 🗣️ Say: “I can move with company, even virtual.”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Feel Emotionally Blocked

You can’t think your way through a locked door; try the body key.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🥶 Cold water on wrists/face, 30 seconds.
    
- 📝 Write one sentence: “I’m blocked because ___.”
    
- 🚶 3-minute walk; narrate one observation per step.
    
- 🎧 One song → when it ends, do one tiny task.
    
- 🫶 Place hand on chest: “It’s safe to take a micro-step.”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Feel Frozen

Motion melts ice. Any motion.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🐾 Move _something_ from A to B.
    
- ⏱️ 90-second tidy of the nearest surface.
    
- 🧊 Hold an ice cube and count to 20. Then choose one action.
    
- 🧠 Say: “I’m allowed to start ugly.”
    
- 🎯 Do the 10-second version of the task.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Feel Shame

Shame thrives in silence; bust it with truth and care.  
**Try one of these:**

- ✋ Hand on heart: “I’m still worthy.”
    
- 📝 Micro-repair: one apology, one reset, one plan.
    
- 🗣️ Tell one safe person, “I’m spiraling — can I vent?”
    
- 🧼 Do a tiny self-care act (wash face, fresh shirt).
    
- ❌ Replace “should’ve” with “next time I’ll…”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Forgot

Forgetting isn’t failure — it’s human.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🧾 Brain-dump everything you’re holding.
    
- ⏰ Set two reminders (time + place cue).
    
- 📍 Put the item where future-you will trip over it.
    
- 📨 Send yourself an email with the subject “DO THIS TODAY.”
    
- 🧠 Say: “Systems, not memory.”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Got Distracted

You’re not weak — your environment won. Change the field.  
**Try one of these:**

- 📵 Phone in another room for 15 minutes.
    
- 🧰 Set up a **Focus Box**: water, pen, sticky notes, headphones.
    
- 🔒 Full-screen the one app you need.
    
- ⏳ 7-minute sprint; tally distractions on paper.
    
- 🔁 Say: “Back to task” and physically turn your body toward it.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Keep Avoiding It

Avoidance = protection. Let’s make it safer and smaller.  
**Try one of these:**

- 📦 Do a **no-stakes preview**: open the file, look only.
    
- 👯 Body-double for the first 10 minutes.
    
- 🔍 Identify the scariest 10% and strip it out for now.
    
- 🧠 Script the first sentence you’ll say/type.
    
- 🎁 Promise a tiny reward when you touch the task.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I Run Out of Steam

You didn’t lose willpower; you used your fuel.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🍎 Protein or carb + water break.
    
- 🧭 Re-pick the target: “What actually matters to finish?”
    
- ⏱️ 5 on / 2 off x 3 rounds.
    
- ✂️ Cut two steps you don’t need.
    
- ✅ Do an easy win to get momentum, then return.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: I’m Afraid I’ll Fail

Fear is loud before the first move.  
**Try one of these:**

- 📝 Define “minimum viable success” (MVS) in one line.
    
- 🧠 Say: “Outcome uncertain, effort chosen.”
    
- 🔁 Do a deliberately bad first draft.
    
- 🤝 Ask for a quick gut-check from one person.
    
- ⏳ Two minutes of action, then reassess.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: It Feels Pointless

Meaning can be tiny and still count.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🧭 Tie it to a person or value: “Who benefits if I do this?”
    
- 🎯 Make it a game: score points for each 3-minute burst.
    
- ✂️ Shrink the goal to “move it forward 1%.”
    
- 🔁 Do it as a **future favor**: “Tomorrow-me says thanks.”
    
- 🎵 Add a vibe: music, candle, light — change the feel.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: It Feels Too Big

Big becomes doable when it becomes many smalls.  
**Try one of these:**

- 🧱 Break into **5 sticky notes** — one micro-step each.
    
- 📍 Choose the **first brick** only.
    
- ⏳ Work until the next song ends.
    
- 🧰 Gather tools only (no doing).
    
- 🗂️ Create a “Later” pile to park extras.
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: It’s Not Urgent

Urgency is optional; momentum is not.  
**Try one of these:**

- 📅 Schedule a **10-minute slot** today anyway.
    
- 🎯 Tie it to a routine (after coffee → 5 minutes on task).
    
- 🧾 Write the cost of not doing it (30 seconds).
    
- 🏁 Define a visible finish line (one page, one email, one drawer).
    
- 🧠 Say: “I do tiny things early to buy future ease.”
    

---

### 🆘 Crisis Mode: Too Many Decisions

Decision fatigue is real — constrain the menu.  
**Try one of these:**

- 1️⃣ Limit to **two** options; flip a coin if tied.
    
- ✅ Choose the option that is **easiest to start**.
    
- ⏳ Give yourself **90 seconds** to decide.
    
- 📋 Use a default rule: “If <15 minutes, do now.”
    
- 🗂️ Park non-critical choices on a “Decide Friday” list.
