## 🌅 Morning Routine

Mornings with ADHD can feel like waking up into chaos. Transitioning from sleep to action requires **activation energy**, **sequencing**, and **emotional regulation**, all areas of struggle. It's not that you're bad at mornings, your brain just needs extra support to get into motion.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
Mornings are **hard with ADHD**, **transitions**, **decisions**, and **pressure** hit all at once. You're not a **failure** if you **start slow**. **Stack habits**. **Simplify choices**. Build a routine that feels like **support**, not **punishment**.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
**Snoozing into chaos** is a **choice**. Stop pretending **mornings** are some **unsolvable mystery**. **Pick out clothes** the night before. **Put your alarm** across the room. **Build the routine**, or **stay stuck**.

### 🧠 Why Morning Routines Are Hard with ADHD

- You might:
  - Hit snooze 6 times and rush through your whole routine
  - Forget simple steps like brushing your teeth or packing your meds
  - Feel overwhelmed by where to start and freeze
  - Get stuck scrolling or daydreaming instead of transitioning to action

- Here's what's really going on:
  - 🧩 **Executive dysfunction** affects planning, sequencing, and initiation
  - ⏰ **Time blindness** distorts how long tasks take or how fast time is passing
  - 💥 **Dopamine regulation** makes "boring" tasks feel nearly impossible to start
  - 🔁 **Working memory issues** cause you to lose track of multi-step routines
  - 🧠 **Transitioning from sleep to action** is a complex neurological shift

### ⚓ Core Principles for ADHD-Friendly Mornings

- **"Good enough" mornings are good enough**: Your goal isn't perfection, it's momentum
- **Reduce decisions, externalize steps**: Simplify wherever possible
- **Don't stack habits, anchor them**: Attach new habits to existing ones
- **Minimum viable morning**: Just 1–3 must-do actions on hard days
- **There's no one right way, only your way**: Build what works for your brain

### 🛠️ Strategies for Morning Routine

#### ⏰ Getting Out of Bed

- **Better sleep hygiene**: Consistent bedtimes, wind-down rituals, dawn simulator alarms
- **Avoid the snooze trap**: Place alarm across room, use puzzle alarms, create "treat triggers"
- **Gentle motivation rituals**: Wake to music, podcasts, or smart speaker routines
- **Light and movement**: Sit by window, use light therapy, take 5-minute walk

#### 📋 Structuring Your Morning

- **Design "Morning Launch Sequence"**: Identify 1–3 must-do actions (meds, water, dressed)
- **Visual schedules & checklists**: Laminated step-by-step tasks, picture schedules
- **"Routine boxes"**: Separate bins for before/after work items
- **Time tools**: Visual timers, calendar blocks with 15-minute buffers
- **Automate what you can**: Prep breakfast night before, launchpad area for essentials

#### 🍳 Optimize Key Tasks

- **Get dressed right away**: Changes you into "action mode," lay out clothes night before
- **Protein-rich breakfast**: Supports dopamine production (eggs, Greek yogurt, protein shakes)
- **Streamline hygiene**: Make shower sensory-pleasing, visible toiletries
- **Review day visually**: Calendar or whiteboard to set priorities

#### 🎮 Boosting Motivation & Momentum

- **Anchor habits**: "After I brush teeth, I take vitamins" or "While kettle boils, I check schedule"
- **Gamify it**: "Can I be out the door before this song ends?"
- **Use rewards**: TikTok after launch sequence, coffee treat as dopamine incentive
- **Body doubling**: Text friend when up, morning Zoom coworking

#### 🧍 Ongoing Support & Reset Systems

- **Evening reset routine**: Load dishwasher, set out clothes, charge phone
- **Tomorrow's victory setup**: Add note, outfit, or treat for Future You
- **Permission for slow starts**: Some mornings are harder, try again in 10 minutes
- **Use coaching or community**: ADHD coaches, therapists, support groups

### 🧭 Morning Routine ≠ Perfect Performance

- You are not lazy, your brain has unique transition needs
- Shame slows you down, kindness keeps you moving
- You're allowed to wear the same thing 3 days in a row
- You're allowed to miss a step and still be doing great

> Every step you take is building trust with your future self.

### 💬 Encouragement to Take With You

> If you opened your eyes, that's a win. If you took your meds or got dressed, that's progress. If you brushed your teeth by 11am, that still counts. You're not failing, you're learning your rhythm.
