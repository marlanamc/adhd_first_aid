## 🧼 ADHD & Hygiene

Hygiene can be deeply impacted by ADHD, not because you don't care about being clean, but because routines require **initiation**, **consistency**, and **energy**. Overwhelm, time blindness, or low stimulation can make even a shower feel impossible. This is a neurological block, not a reflection of worth.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
You're not **gross**. You're **overwhelmed**. Hygiene requires **task switching**, **sensory tolerance**, and **energy**, all hard with **ADHD**. You don't need a **full shower** to care for yourself. Start with a **face wipe**, **fresh clothes**, or **brushing your teeth**. **Small steps** still count.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
You can't keep **skipping** basic **self-care**. Not **showering**, **brushing**, or **changing clothes** isn't just about **mood**, it affects your **health**. **Clean your body**. It's **not optional**. No one's coming to **rescue you**, you have to **show up for yourself**.

### 🧠 Why Hygiene Tasks Are Hard with ADHD

- You might:
  - Go days without showering or brushing your teeth
  - Feel overwhelmed by where to begin or how long it will take
  - Skip changing clothes or doing laundry until it feels urgent
  - Avoid grooming tasks because they feel boring, unpleasant, or overstimulating

- Here's what's really going on:
  - 🧩 **Executive dysfunction** impacts sequencing, initiation, and task-switching  
  - 🌪️ **Sensory issues** can make hygiene feel physically uncomfortable  
  - ⏰ **Time blindness** causes underestimation of how long tasks take  
  - 💥 **Low dopamine** makes boring tasks feel impossible to start  
  - 🔁 **Shame or negative self-talk** creates a cycle of procrastination and avoidance

### ⚓ Core Principles for ADHD-Friendly Hygiene

- **Start small**: Any hygiene action counts, even if it's just changing your shirt  
- **Remove barriers**: Keep supplies visible and accessible in multiple locations  
- **Work with sensory needs**: Find textures, scents, and routines that feel good  
- **Progress over perfection**: Consistent small wins beat sporadic deep cleans  
- **Self-compassion first**: You're not lazy, you're navigating a differently wired brain

### 🛠️ Strategies for Hygiene

#### 💖 Start with Self-Compassion

- **You are not dirty or lazy**: You are navigating a brain that needs different kinds of support.
- **Perfection is not the goal**: Focus on tiny wins that build momentum.
- **Try reframing hygiene as care**: Brushing your teeth isn't just a task, it's you showing up for yourself.

#### 🔄 Break Down the Tasks

- **Make it smaller**: "Take a shower" → "Turn on water," "Step in," "Wash hair," etc.
- **Use the 2-minute rule**: If it takes less than 2 minutes (like deodorant or mouthwash), just do it.
- **Start with what matters most**: If showering feels like too much, wash your face and change clothes instead.

#### 📃 Externalize and Simplify

- **Checklists for routines**: Keep a simple hygiene checklist in your bathroom or phone.
- **Leave supplies visible**: If it's out of sight, it's out of mind.
- **Pre-fill**: Refill your soap, prep clean towels, stock travel-size items in multiple rooms.
- **Make hygiene kits**: A face wipe and travel deodorant by your bed can be lifesavers on low-energy days.

#### 📅 Create Gentle Rhythms, Not Rigid Routines

- **Habit stack**: Pair hygiene with another task (e.g., brushing teeth while playing music).
- **Anchor it**: Link hygiene to natural transition points, like after using the bathroom or before putting on pajamas.
- **Use timers or alarms**: Set a "start winding down" alarm to cue hygiene tasks.

#### 🌈 Manage Sensory Sensitivities

- **Hate toothpaste texture?** Try a different flavor or a mouth rinse.
- **Shower water too harsh?** Use a gentle spray nozzle or lower the pressure.
- **Hair washing feels exhausting?** Use dry shampoo or keep short styles.
- **Sound/light bothers you?** Try soft lighting, earplugs, or calming music.

#### ✨ Make It Fun, Not a Chore

- **Gamify it**: Race the timer, give yourself points, or try a hygiene bingo.
- **Dress it up**: Use hygiene products you love, nice scents, fun packaging, fancy lotions.
- **Body double**: Video chat with a friend and both brush teeth at the same time.

#### 🚪 Reduce Environmental Barriers

- **Keep supplies in multiple locations**: Wipes, flossers, deodorant in your car, bag, or bedside.
- **Laundry bottleneck?** Have backup basics so you're not stuck if you're behind.
- **Batch tasks**: Wipe sink while brushing teeth, clean shower while in it.
- **Get support when needed**: Partners, roommates, or support networks can assist. Professional help is valid.

### 🧭 Hygiene ≠ Morality

- You're not gross. You're not lazy. You're a person navigating the world with a differently wired brain.  
- The fact that you care at all means you're already doing more than you think.
> Your worth isn't measured by how often you shower or brush your teeth.  
> You're building habits that work for your brain, not against it.

### 💬 Encouragement to Take With You

> If you brushed your teeth today, that's a win. If you put on clean clothes, that's a win. If you didn't, but you _wanted_ to, that still counts. You're showing up for yourself in the ways you can, and that's what matters.
