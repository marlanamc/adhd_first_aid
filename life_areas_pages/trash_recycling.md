## 🗑️ Trash & Recycling

Taking out the trash sounds simple, but it's a task that requires **awareness**, **timing**, and **activation**. For ADHD brains, it's out of sight, out of mind,  until it piles up. You're not dirty or careless,  your brain deprioritizes repetitive tasks that offer no real payoff.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
It's easy to **ignore trash** when it's "**not urgent**." But **clutter attracts clutter**,  and soon it's **overwhelming**. You deserve a home that **smells clean** and **feels fresh**. **One bag out** is a **win**.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
**Take the trash out**. It's that **simple**. Stop **walking past** the **overflowing bin** like it's **someone else's job**. **You live here**. **Act like it**.

### 🧠 Why Trash & Recycling Is Hard with ADHD

- You might:
  - Forget the bin is full until it's disgusting
  - Miss trash day every week
  - Let bags pile up because you don't want to go outside
  - Put it off "until later" and feel gross about it
  - Avoid opening the bin because it's already overflowing

- Here's what's really going on:
  - 🧩 **Executive dysfunction** makes it hard to plan and sequence multi-step tasks
  - ⏰ **Time blindness** distorts how long it'll take,  or when trash day even is
  - 💥 **Working memory limits** mean you forget it's full the moment you walk away
  - 🔁 **The Wall of Awful** builds from shame, past failures, and sensory dread
  - 🧠 **All-or-nothing thinking** convinces you it's not worth doing unless you clean everything

### ⚓ Core Principles for ADHD-Friendly Trash Management

- **Start stupid small**: "Tie bag shut" counts as a win
- **Use time tricks**: Recurring alarms and visual reminders
- **Design simple systems**: Extra liners, smaller bins, clear containers
- **Add support**: Body doubling, task trading, accountability texts
- **It's maintenance, not morality**: One bag out equals more space and peace

### 🛠️ Strategies for Trash & Recycling

#### 🧹 Start Stupid Small

- **"Tie bag shut"**: Counts as a win
- **"Put bag by the door"**: Is a step forward
- **"Take one bag out"**: Is enough
- **If it smells**: It's priority #1,  forget perfection, go for relief

#### ⏲️ Use Time Tricks

- **Set recurring alarms**: For trash and recycling days
- **Put reminders**: On your fridge, phone, or exit door
- **2-minute timers**: Or "one song sprints" during dopamine windows

#### 📦 Design a Simple System

- **Extra liners in bottom of bin**: Always ready for the next bag
- **Smaller bins**: Fill faster and cue action
- **"Take out" basket near door**: For any trash, recycling, or returns
- **Clear bins for recycling**: Stay visually cued

#### 👯 Add Support

- **Body double**: With roommate, partner, or FaceTime friend
- **Trade tasks**: You take trash, they do dishes
- **Accountability text**: "Taking out the trash now. Your turn?"

### 🧭 Trash ≠ Character

- You're not lazy,  your brain prioritizes danger and novelty, not banana peels
- One bag out equals more space, more peace
- You deserve a space that doesn't smell like old leftovers
- It's not all-or-nothing,  it's maintenance, and that counts

> It's also one of the fastest wins you can get.

### 💬 Encouragement to Take With You

> Trash isn't just trash,  it's emotional weight, sensory stress, and invisible overwhelm. But take one bag out. Light a candle. Let that be your reset.
