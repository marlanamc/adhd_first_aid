

### 🏠 **Homepage Flow**

> _“Where do you want to start?”_

The homepage offers seven clearly labeled entry points:

1. **💬 Start With a Feeling**  
    → Browse emotion-first pages like _Overwhelmed_, _Ashamed_, _Drained_
    
2. **🛠 Get Help With a Task**  
    → Pick a task like _Cleaning_, _Emails_, _Meal Prep_, _Errands_
    
3. **🧍 Life Context & Identity**  
    → Explore identity pages like _Caregiver_, _Parent_, _Job Seeker_
    
4. **⚙️ Build a System That Works** _(New: Systems Lab)_  
    → Browse, build, and share ADHD-friendly routines that solve real problems
    
5. **📚 Read Guides & Insights**  
    → Educational articles (e.g., _Habit Bundling_, _Diagnostic Guide_)
    
6. **🧪 Quizzes & Tools**  
    → Interactive experiences (e.g., _Structure Type Quiz_, _Sleep Calculator_)
    
7. **🗣 Scripts for Hard Moments**  
    → Prewritten things to say or send when words fail
    

---

### 🧩 **Content Types**

|Type|Description|
|---|---|
|**Feeling**|Emotion-first pages that validate & gently guide|
|**Barrier**|Functional/emotional blockers like “I can’t start” or “It’s too big”|
|**Task**|Concrete challenges like “cleaning” or “resetting space”|
|**Identity**|Life contexts (e.g. caregiver, student, burned-out worker)|
|**System**|Stacked routines with real-world strategy flows|
|**Guide**|Informational or explanatory content|
|**Script**|Prewritten communication templates|
|**Quiz/Extra**|Interactive tools (quizzes, calculators, flowcharts)|

### ⚙️ **System Lab (Interactive Hub)**

- Browse featured systems (e.g., _7AM Workout System_, _Post-Crash Reset_)
    
- Build your own (guided builder: goal → challenges → steps → tools)
    
- Submit/share your systems
    
- Filter systems by:
    
    - Task (e.g., “Leaving the house”)
        
    - Barrier (e.g., “Time blindness”)
        
    - Identity (e.g., “Neurodivergent adult”)
        
    - Energy level

### 🔄 Updated Flow With Strategy Explorer Option

---

### 🔹 User clicks: **Feeling: Stuck**

They land on the _Stuck_ page, which includes:

1. **🧊 Validation Header**
    

> “You’re not lazy. You’re frozen. Your nervous system has hit pause to protect you.”

2. **🪨 2–3 Micro Strategies**
    

> e.g.:

- Sit near the mess without doing anything
    
- Run warm water over your hands
    
- Press play on a 1-minute playlist
    

3. **✨ Button: “Show All Strategies for Stuck”**
    

> Opens or routes to an expanded strategy gallery, **pre-filtered** for “Stuck”

- User can then narrow it down by:
    
    - Barrier (e.g., “I don’t know where to start”)
        
    - Energy level (Low / Medium / High)
        
    - Type (Regulation / Activation / Task-Specific / Dopamine-Stacked)
        

4. **🛠 Optional Buttons Below**
    

- “Help with something specific?” → Go to Task Selector
    
- “Want to build a full routine?” → Go to Systems Lab
    

---

### 🧠 Why This Works:

- **You start small** (low decision fatigue)
    
- **You honor their emotional state** first
    
- Then offer **three clear next steps** based on their capacity:
    
    1. _Try something tiny_
        
    2. _See more ideas if ready_
        
    3. _Go deeper with a task or system_
