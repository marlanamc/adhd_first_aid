## Sources for AuDHD

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explore the intersection of autism and ADHD — from overlapping traits and masking to burnout, self-advocacy, and radical self-acceptance — and offer tools to support the unique experience of living with both neurotypes.

---

### 🧠 Understanding the AuDHD Brain

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
Kemp, Jennifer; Mitchelson, Monique (2024)  
➤ Explores overlapping challenges and strengths of ADHD and autism. Focuses on self-compassion, burnout prevention, and flexible, personalized coping tools like mind maps, voice notes, and sensory supports.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them**  
Pink, Richard; Pink, Roxanne (2024)  
➤ Defines AuDHD directly. Unpacks internalized shame and encourages self-acceptance, emotional validation, and deconstructing harmful labels like “lazy” or “stupid.”

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
Kustow, James (2024)  
➤ Frames ADHD (and co-occurring autism) as unique neurodevelopmental profiles. Encourages symptom mapping and personalized treatment, focusing on the threshold of impairment rather than strict diagnoses.

---

### 💡 Reframing & Self-Acceptance

**Mad About Money**  
Alexander-Grout, Maddy (2025)  
➤ Normalizes having multiple neurodivergent conditions. Encourages embracing your unique wiring and letting go of shame. Prioritizes self-acceptance and sustainable strategies over comparison.

**Tiny Experiments**  
Le Cunff, Anne-Laure (2025)  
➤ Acknowledges the double load of ADHD + autism in productivity systems. Promotes imperfect action and radical self-trust as a way to protect mental health from burnout.

**The Neurodiversity Edge**  
Dunne, Maureen (2024)  
➤ Promotes neurodivergence as a strength. Encourages transparency, psychological safety, and communication as essential tools for thriving with dual diagnoses.

**How to ADHD**  
McCabe, Jessica (2024)  
➤ Offers community-minded strategies that are inclusive of autism. Emphasizes normalizing invisible obstacles and building systems that work for your brain, not against it.

---

### 🛠 Strategies for Living Well with AuDHD

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
Kinzer, Nikki; Wright, Pete D. (2024)  
➤ While focused on ADHD, the tools apply to AuDHD through shared experiences like shame spirals and RSD. Encourages radical acceptance and self-designed planning systems.

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD or Other Disorders**  
(ADHD Success) (2025)  
➤ Frames ADHD as part of a larger neurodivergent picture. Encourages universal neurodivergent-friendly strategies like environmental design, self-care, and externalization.

**It All Makes Sense Now**  
Carder, Meredith (2024)  
➤ Discusses burnout, masking, and overwhelm—key challenges for AuDHD. Focuses on reframing ADHD as a strength and structuring life to match your brain.

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
Solden, Sari; Frank, Michelle; Littman, Ellen (2019)  
➤ Though centered on women with ADHD, the book’s core message of reclaiming neurodivergence with power and compassion is deeply resonant for AuDHD readers.
