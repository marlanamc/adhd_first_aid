## Sources for The Creative

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources celebrate the ADHD brain's strengths in creativity, innovation, and divergent thinking — and offer strategies for embracing your originality while building sustainable routines, focus systems, and emotional resilience.

---

### 🎨 Embracing Creative ADHD Strengths

**ADHD 2.0**  
Hallowell, Edward M.; Ratey, John J. (2022)  
➤ Frames the ADHD brain as a “Ferrari engine” of creativity and originality. Encourages leaning into unique cognitive wiring rather than masking or correcting it. Creativity is not a side effect — it's a strength.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
Dee, Lisa (2025)  
➤ Describes ADHD traits as rocket fuel for innovation and bold thinking. Advocates for holistic, personalized care to support creative energy and mental health. Encourages readers to dazzle — not fit in.

**The Neurodiversity Edge**  
Dunne, Maureen (2024)  
➤ Highlights research on “unconstrained creative generation” and lateral thinking in neurodivergent individuals. Champions neurodiversity as a competitive advantage in innovation and leadership.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them**  
Pink, Richard; Pink, Roxanne (2024)  
➤ Dismantles the myth that success only comes from structure and discipline. Highlights creativity, vivid imagination, and brainstorming brilliance as core ADHD gifts — especially when paired with self-compassion.

---

### 🧠 Designing Systems for Creative Brains

**Productivity With ADHD**  
DOMI, Ju (2025)  
➤ Emphasizes that the ADHD brain thrives with dynamic, personalized systems. Recommends dedicated time for idea generation, structured outlets for hyperfocus, and flexible tools that support momentum.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
Ratey, Nancy A. (2008)  
➤ Encourages readers to build structure around their creative talent, not in spite of it. Offers coaching frameworks that align with how ADHD brains think, plan, and innovate.

**How to ADHD**  
McCabe, Jessica (2024)  
➤ Offers a toolbox of ADHD-friendly strategies. Embraces strengths like hyperfocus and big-picture thinking, while helping creatives address time blindness, task-switching, and execution gaps.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
Kinzer, Nikki; Wright, Pete D. (2024)  
➤ Celebrates ADHDers as “trailblazers” and “outside-the-box thinkers.” Encourages creative professionals to reject shame, stop apologizing, and build planning systems that reflect their brilliance.

---

### 💡 Identity, Expression & Reframing

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
Solden, Sari; Frank, Michelle (2019)  
➤ Validates the emotional and creative strengths of ADHD women. Helps readers overcome shame, trust their instincts, and build lives that reflect their true wiring — not societal expectations.

**Women with Attention Deficit Disorder**  
Solden, Sari (2013)  
➤ Describes ADHD as a “surplus of ideas” and urges readers to “disorganize the status quo.” Encourages restructuring life around creativity, not perfectionism.

**You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
Kelly, Kate; Ramundo, Peggy (2006)  
➤ A foundational reframe of ADHD. Positions creative professions like art, comedy, and entrepreneurship as ideal ADHD outlets. Emphasizes forgiving past struggles and designing a life that fits your brain.
