## Sources for Immigrant

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explore how ADHD intersects with immigration — including cultural expectations, masking, trauma, and systemic barriers — and offer tools for reclaiming identity, releasing shame, and building support systems that reflect your lived experience.

---

### 🌍 ADHD, Culture & Identity

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them**  
Pink, Richard; Pink, Roxanne (2024)  
➤ Speaks directly to immigrant experiences, highlighting how cultural and familial expectations often lead to masking and self-shame. Encourages unlearning “ADHD Lies,” reframing struggles through a lens of executive function challenges, and advocating for your real needs.

**How to ADHD**  
McCabe, Jessica (2024)  
➤ Features perspectives from first-generation immigrant ADHDers. Explores how cultural pressure and survivalism lead to hypervigilance and masking. Offers solidarity, normalizes intersectional experiences, and encourages self-advocacy without shame.

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
Solden, Sari; Frank, Michelle (2019)  
➤ Highlights the emotional toll of being perceived as "different" — both visibly and invisibly. Shares composite stories of women of color navigating ADHD in high-expectation families. Encourages creating life structures based on authenticity, not perfectionism.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
Dee, Lisa (2025)  
➤ Acknowledges that many ADHDers, especially those from marginalized backgrounds, have navigated life by pushing past limits and masking pain. Offers tools for releasing shame, reclaiming agency, and building wellness systems grounded in identity and self-trust.

---

### 🧠 Masking, Burnout & Emotional Processing

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD**  
Kemp, Jennifer; Mitchelson, Monique (2024)  
➤ Addresses how social exclusion, microaggressions, and masking lead to burnout. Encourages self-compassion and practical strategies for managing energy, setting boundaries, and challenging invalidating cultural narratives.

**The Body Keeps the Score**  
van der Kolk, Bessel A. (2015)  
➤ Offers a trauma-informed lens for understanding how stress — including generational, developmental, and cultural — shapes emotion and behavior. Validates the lived experiences of those navigating immigration-related trauma alongside ADHD.

**Scattered Minds**  
Maté, Gabor (2019)  
➤ Argues that ADHD must be understood in context — including the cultural and environmental experiences of immigrants. Urges readers to reject a purely genetic view and consider how adaptation, displacement, and systemic stress contribute to ADHD expression.

---

### 💡 Reframing, Self-Acceptance & Advocacy

**You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
Kelly, Kate; Ramundo, Peggy (2006)  
➤ Helps reframe ADHD through compassion and self-understanding. Especially relevant for immigrants navigating stigma and cultural misunderstanding. Encourages self-education, community support, and designing systems that match your brain — not society’s expectations.
