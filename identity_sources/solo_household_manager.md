## Sources for the Solo or Household Manager

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD impacts daily life when you’re responsible for managing a household — and offer practical, personalized strategies for organization, energy conservation, routines, and self-compassion.

---

### 🧠 Managing ADHD at Home

**You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
Kelly, Kate; Ramundo, Peggy (2006)  
➤ Reframes ADHD as an explanation, not an excuse — and calls for using external structure to create internal order. Offers mess management tips, realistic time planning, and support for avoiding burnout in day-to-day home tasks.

**Order from Chaos**  
Paul, Jaclyn (2018)  
➤ Offers realistic, compassionate strategies for staying organized with ADHD. Stresses the need for external containers (not your brain), weekly reviews, and understanding your “why” for organizing. Encourages mess management over perfection.

**Succeeding with Adult ADHD**  
Levrini, Abigail (2025)  
➤ Provides evidence-based tools to manage executive function deficits in time, organization, and goal-setting. Recommends small, measurable steps and flexible structure over micromanagement for household success.

**The Disorganized Mind**  
Ratey, Nancy A. (2008)  
➤ Advocates self-coaching and individualized routines. Offers strategies for managing finances, laundry, and clutter — while emphasizing that structure is essential when managing ADHD’s daily impact.

---

### 🏠 Routines, Clutter, and Domestic Systems

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
Davis, KC (2022)  
➤ Introduces the philosophy of “rhythms over routines” — flexible systems that let you skip a beat and keep going. Encourages shame-free approaches to cleaning and chores for neurodivergent individuals.

**Productivity with ADHD: Complete Guide**  
DOMI, Ju (2025)  
➤ Positions organized space as the foundation for productivity. Recommends using timers, time-blocking, external reminders, and digital systems to manage tasks — while embracing sustainable, imperfect progress.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
Kustow, James (2024)  
➤ Links physical disorganization to nervous system threat and chronic illness. Offers decluttering strategies, sensory-friendly tools, and home detoxing tips to support well-being and household function.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
Dee, Lisa (2025)  
➤ Recommends templates, automations, and nervous system regulation to make managing a home less overwhelming. Encourages personalized routines that prioritize rest, nourishment, and hormonal balance.

---

### 💡 Tools, Support, and Self-Compassion

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
Solden, Sari; Frank, Michelle (2019)  
➤ Encourages women managing homes solo to ditch shame and build personalized systems. Highlights burnout risks, optimal energy periods, and the importance of protein, sleep, and self-kindness.

**The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook**  
Kemp, Jennifer; Mitchelson, Monique (2024)  
➤ Centers burnout recovery, sensory needs, and realistic expectations. Urges readers to get support, ask for help with physical tasks, and build home systems around your actual energy and function.

**How to ADHD**  
McCabe, Jessica (2024)  
➤ Validates unconventional ADHD-friendly systems. Highlights the value of coaching, accountability, and external tools for managing memory, planning, and tasks in solo living.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies**  
Pink, Richard; Pink, Roxanne (2024)  
➤ Dispels myths around laziness and self-worth. Encourages solo household managers to reject burnout culture, challenge shame, and redefine productivity around your needs, not societal expectations.
