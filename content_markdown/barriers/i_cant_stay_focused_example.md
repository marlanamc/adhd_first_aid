# I Can't Stay Focused

> **Quick Help**: Pick any strategy below. They all work on their own - no need to do everything!

## 🚨 Right Now: First Steps

1. **Quick Environment Check**
   - [ ] Clear your immediate workspace
   - [ ] Adjust lighting (brighter for energy, dimmer for calm)
   - [ ] Check noise level
   - [ ] Temperature comfortable?
   - [ ] Water within reach?

2. **Reset Your Focus** (Pick One):
   - 🧘‍♀️ Take 3 deep breaths
   - 👋 Shake out your hands
   - 💧 Take a sip of water
   - 🪟 Look out a window for 30 seconds

3. **Ground Yourself** (Pick One):
   - Name 5 things you can see
   - Press your feet firmly into the ground
   - Squeeze and release your hands
   - Roll your shoulders back

## 🎯 Focus Tools That Help

### Timer Techniques
- **Classic Pomodoro**: 25 min work → 5 min break
- **Tiny Timer**: 5 min work → 1 min break
- **Energy Match**: High energy → 45 min work, Low energy → 10 min work

### Body Doubling Options
- Work next to someone quiet
- Virtual body doubling
- Study with me videos (no music)
- Quiet coffee shop sounds

### Task Chunking
- Break task into 10-minute pieces
- Use "done for now" points
- Create clear stopping spots

## 🔄 Getting Back on Track

### When You Notice You're Off Task:
1. **Pause & Notice**
   - No judgment - this is normal
   - Take a breath
   - Notice where your attention went

2. **Quick Reset** (Pick One)
   - Stand and stretch
   - Take a drink of water
   - Roll your shoulders
   - Look out window

3. **Return to Task**
   - Find your last clear step
   - Start with something small
   - Set a timer for 5 minutes

## 🎮 Focus Control Panel

### Noise Level
- **Too Quiet?** Add:
  - White noise
  - Nature sounds
  - Instrumental music
  
- **Too Noisy?** Try:
  - Noise-canceling headphones
  - Earplugs
  - Change rooms

### Lighting
- **Too Bright?** 
  - Dim lights
  - Use lamp instead of overhead
  - Adjust screen brightness
  
- **Too Dark?**
  - Add task lighting
  - Open blinds
  - Increase screen brightness

### Movement
- **Fidget Options:**
  - Quiet fidget toy
  - Foot rocker
  - Doodle pad
  
- **Mini Movements:**
  - Chair twists
  - Ankle rolls
  - Shoulder shrugs
