## 🌀 ADHD First Aid Template: Restless
It’s not lack of focus • It’s not just boredom • It’s ADHD meeting internal urgency, unspent energy, and a nervous system that won’t settle.

Restlessness isn’t just about needing to move — it’s about needing _something_. Something to engage you, redirect you, ground you. ADHD brains are wired for stimulation and novelty, and when that’s missing, your body and mind rebel. You pace, scroll, snack, fidget — not because you’re unfocused, but because you're _understimulated_.

You’re not scattered — your brain just needs motion or meaning.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
That twitchy, can’t-sit-still feeling is your body asking for movement. It’s not wrong — it’s just misdirected. Try walking, shaking, stretching, pacing. Restlessness isn’t a problem — it’s an outlet waiting to happen.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
You’re fidgeting through your life. Use the energy — don’t just let it leak. Move with intention. Channel it into something useful. Otherwise you’re just burning out in place.

### 🧠 Why ADHD Often Feels Like Restlessness:

- ⚡ Dopamine-seeking behavior kicks in when tasks feel dull or unstructured
    
- 🧠 Lack of task clarity or purpose fuels the need to move or switch constantly
    
- 🧍‍♂️ Understimulated nervous systems search for “something” — anything — to regulate
    
- 🎯 Hyperfocus rebound can leave you jittery when switching gears
    
- 🔁 Restlessness can mask emotional avoidance or the urge to escape discomfort

---

### 1. 🧭 Recognize What Restlessness Is Telling You

Your brain wants motion, stimulation, or relief. Start by tuning in without judgment.

**Try this:**

- 🔍 Name it clearly: “I feel on edge,” “My body wants to move,” or “My brain feels too full.”
    
- 📝 Externalize the swirl: Brain dump your thoughts or talk it out to stop the loop.
    
- 💥 Notice signals: Fidgeting, snapping at people, pacing, or zoning out? These are signs to intervene gently.
    

> 💡 Tip: Restlessness is your nervous system asking for release, not punishment.

---

### 2. 🏃‍♀️ Discharge the Energy Physically

You can’t outthink restlessness — you need to move it out of your system.

**Try this:**

- 🕺 Dance it out or go for a brisk walk
    
- 🧘 Stretch or do a somatic “shake out” (arms, legs, neck)
    
- 🪑 Use fidget toys, swivel chairs, or change your position (e.g., lie on the floor)
    
- 🧶 Add mindful movement to boring tasks — knitting, rocking, walking while thinking
    

> 💡 Tip: Even 2 minutes of movement can change your state completely.

---

### 3. 🌬️ Regulate Your Nervous System & Environment

When you’re amped up, your system needs calming cues — both inside and around you.

**Try this:**

- 🌬️ Box breathing or extended exhale to shift out of fight-or-flight
    
- 🧘 Try the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding method or a quick body scan
    
- 🎧 Use sensory aids: noise-canceling headphones, dim lights, cozy textures
    
- 🛁 Hot shower, tight clothing, compression vest, or weighted blanket
    

> 💡 Tip: Tiny sensory tweaks can dramatically change your internal state.

---

### 4. 🧠 Reframe the Thoughts Fueling Restlessness

Your mind might be spinning with boredom, pressure, or too many options.

**Try this:**

- 🧩 Break it down: Start with _any_ small part of a task. “Open the document” is progress.
    
- 🎯 Add novelty or time pressure: Race the clock or try “reverse pomodoros” to gamify dull tasks
    
- 💬 Flip the script: “I’m not lazy — I’m under-stimulated and overloaded at once.”
    
- ✅ Use defaults: Eat the same lunch, wear a go-to outfit, or repeat yesterday’s to-do
    

> 💡 Tip: Boredom mimics burnout in ADHD brains. Create stimulation without chaos.

---

### 5. 🧍‍♀️ Get Support & Build in Recovery

Restlessness builds when you’re overwhelmed and alone. Step into connection and restoration.

**Try this:**

- 🙋 Ask someone to listen while you talk out your “mind tornado”
    
- 👯 Body double with someone while you move through a task
    
- 🛑 Say “no” to new commitments and protect your recharge time
    
- 🛏️ Let yourself rest — even if it feels uncomfortable at first
    

> 💡 Tip: Stillness might feel foreign, not wrong. You don’t have to earn it — just practice it.

---

### 🔁 Starting Point Suggestions

→ Take a 3-minute walk outside without your phone  
→ Turn on music and move your body however it wants  
→ Do a 5-minute brain dump and end with 3 calming breaths
