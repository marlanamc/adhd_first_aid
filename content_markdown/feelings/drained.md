## 🪫 ADHD First Aid Template: Drained
It’s not laziness • It’s not poor time management • It’s ADHD meeting mental fatigue, sensory overload, and emotional overexertion.

When you’re drained, it’s not just about being tired — it’s about having nothing left in the tank. ADHD brains burn energy faster than most people realize. Every little task requires more activation, more decisions, and more self-regulation. Add in interruptions, unmet needs, and pressure to mask or perform, and it’s no wonder you feel wiped out.

Sometimes it’s not the big things — it’s the 1,000 tiny things that got you here.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
You’ve been pushing too hard for too long. No wonder you’re empty. You’re allowed to pause. Recharge doesn’t mean you’re lazy — it means you’re wise enough to protect your energy. Refill before you break.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
You’re not a phone on 1% — you’re a person with choices. Sleep. Eat. Get off the screen. Rest like it matters — because it does. Then get back to it.

### 🧠 Why ADHD Is So Exhausting:

- 🔋 Constant executive function demands make even small tasks energy-draining
    
- 🌀 Switching tasks, context, or environments burns mental fuel fast
    
- 🎭 Masking in professional or social settings takes a hidden toll
    
- 📉 Poor interoception (awareness of body signals) can lead to burnout before you realize it’s coming
    
- 🔁 Emotional dysregulation + decision fatigue = brain shutdown

---

### 1. 🧠 Pause and Ask: What’s Draining Me?

Feeling drained is often a _signal_, not a flaw. It usually means your body or brain has unmet needs.

**Try this:**

- 🛑 Stop and check in:
    
    - Have I eaten or had water today?
        
    - Am I overstimulated? Under-rested?
        
    - Is my body tense or heavy?
        
- ❓ Ask: “What is my body trying to tell me right now?”
    
- 🧠 Consider causes:
    
    - Analysis paralysis
        
    - Too many decisions
        
    - Overcommitted schedule
        
    - Unclear expectations
        
    - Emotional labor or masking
        

> 💡 Tip: Your body gives quiet signals before it screams. Let’s listen early.

---

### 2. 🌬 Regulate Before You Recharge

Don’t push through. Start by _soothing_ your system — physically, emotionally, and sensorially.

**Try this:**

- 🧘 Deep breathing or box breathing (4-4-4-4)
    
- 🛋 Retreat to a quiet, dim space — or use noise-canceling headphones
    
- 😭 Let it out: cry, stomp, stretch, shake
    
- 🤲 Do something sensory: hold a warm drink, lay under a weighted blanket
    
- 📺 “Mindless recharging”: soft background TV, puzzles, comfort games
    
- 🛑 Repeat this mantra: “Rest is a right, not a reward”
    

> 💡 Tip: Recovery starts the moment you stop forcing.

---

### 3. 🔋 Reduce Demands & Conserve Energy

You’re running on fumes — let’s clear the board, not add more tasks.

**Try this:**

- 🧾 Brain dump: offload what’s in your head
    
- 🔢 Break tasks into tiny “baby steps”
    
- 🕰 Use a Pomodoro timer (25 on / 5 off) to prevent burnout
    
- 📆 Cancel, reschedule, or snooze non-urgent stuff
    
- 🔁 Create end-of-day transitions (walk, idea dump, music)
    
- 📊 Track your energy patterns and avoid peak fatigue hours
    

> 💡 Tip: Do less to unlock more. Rest now to move better tomorrow.

---

### 4. 💗 Be Gentle with Your Inner Voice

Drained doesn’t mean defective. Let’s shift your mindset from shame to strategy.

**Try this:**

- 💬 Say: “I’m having the thought that I’m lazy” → it’s just a thought
    
- 💡 Reframe: “This is my brain protecting me — not sabotaging me”
    
- 🧘 Recognize _all_ types of rest: mental, sensory, creative, emotional
    
- ✍️ Label your feelings: they’re information, not enemies
    
- 🎯 Focus on what you _can_ control (even if it’s just brushing your teeth)
    

> 💡 Tip: Self-compassion isn’t a reward for doing well — it’s the tool that gets you there.

---

### 5. 🤝 Get Support — You Don’t Have to Recharge Alone

Sometimes what you need isn’t a break — it’s someone beside you during the pause.

**Try this:**

- 🙋‍♀️ Ask for help — from a friend, coach, or coworker
    
- 🧍 Body double a task with someone who gets it
    
- 🧭 Say “no” or “not right now” without apology
    
- 🌐 Join a support group or community space
    
- 🧠 Talk to a therapist or ADHD coach if burnout is constant
    

> 💡 Tip: You’re not too much. You’ve just been carrying too much alone.
