## 🍽️ Overeating 

Eating isn't always about hunger, especially with ADHD. It can be a way to manage **boredom**, **emotions**, or **dopamine crashes**. You might forget to eat all day, then binge at night. This isn't about willpower, it's a nervous system trying to self-regulate through food.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
This isn't about willpower. Your body may be trying to **regulate stress, soothe discomfort**, or compensate for **under-eating earlier**. Let's look at what your body **really needs**, and offer it **care, not punishment**.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
**You're not hungry, you're numbing**. You keep eating past fullness and then wondering why you feel worse. **Pause. Drink water. Walk away from the kitchen**. Face the emotion you're avoiding.

### 🧠 Why Overeating Is Hard with ADHD

- You might:
  - Skip meals all day and binge late at night
  - Eat out of boredom or overwhelm
  - Struggle to stop even when full
  - Crave sugar or carbs constantly
  - Feel ashamed or out of control

- Here's what's really going on:
  - 🍭 **Dopamine dysregulation** drives cravings for quick, high-reward foods
  - ⚡ **Impulsivity** overrides the pause between urge and action
  - 🕰 **Time blindness** means forgetting to eat until it's urgent
  - 💭 **Weak interoception** makes hunger/fullness cues hard to read
  - 🥱 **Understimulation** leads to snacking for entertainment or escape
  - 📦 **Executive dysfunction** makes meal planning, cooking, and cleaning feel impossible

### ⚓ Core Principles for ADHD-Friendly Eating

- **You Are Not Broken**: This is your brain seeking balance
- **Perfection Isn't the Goal**: Aim for progress, not food rules
- **Feed Your Brain First**: Strategic nutrition supports executive function
- **Systems Over Willpower**: External structure beats internal motivation
- **Self-Compassion Always**: Shame makes everything harder

### 🛠️ Strategies for Overeating

#### 🍳 Feed Your Brain (Strategic Nutrition)

- **Don't Skip Breakfast**: A high-protein breakfast helps stabilize blood sugar and dopamine
- **Pre-Decide Meals**: Pick 2-3 easy go-to meals per meal slot. Repetition equals less decision fatigue
- **Snack Smart**: Stock grab-and-go protein snacks like jerky, cheese sticks, nuts, or pre-made shakes
- **Hydrate First**: Drink water before reaching for food; thirst often masks as hunger
- **Eat Before Overwhelm**: Set timers/reminders to eat before you get "hangry"

#### 🛠 Reduce Friction Around Food

- **Batch Cook or Buy Prepped Food**: Reduce the barrier to making a decent meal
- **Use Visual Cues**: Keep healthy options eye-level. Out of sight equals out of mind
- **Meal Kits or Delivery**: It's okay to outsource when executive function is low
- **Make Cleanup Easier**: Use paper plates or clean-as-you-go systems

#### 🧠 Regulate and Reflect

- **Mindful Pausing**: Before eating, pause and ask: "What am I feeling right now?" "What do I need?"
- **Slow Down Eating**: Set down your fork between bites or chew 20 times
- **Create Eating Zones**: Eat at the table, not at the desk/sofa/bed. Helps cue fullness
- **Emotional Check-In**: If you're craving food when not hungry, journal or voice memo how you feel

#### 🚧 Externalize and Automate

- **Use Alarms**: Set recurring timers for meals/snacks
- **Shopping List Template**: Keep a reusable list of staples to make shopping easier
- **Prep Stations**: Keep bowls, spoons, snacks, and protein powder in one area
- **Track Patterns**: Use a simple note or app to notice patterns (e.g., skipped lunch equals evening binge)

#### 💛 Practice Self-Compassion

- **Celebrate Tiny Wins**: Ate a solid lunch? That's a win. Drank water before snacking? Win
- **Name the Shame Voice**: Give it a silly name and talk back
- **Progress Over Perfection**: You don't need perfect eating, just nourishing choices when possible
- **Remember Your Why**: You deserve to feel energized and cared for

### 🧭 Overeating ≠ Moral Failure

- You're not greedy or out of control
- You're not lazy or lacking willpower
- You have ADHD, and your brain is trying its best
> Your next best step doesn't have to be perfect, it just has to be doable.
