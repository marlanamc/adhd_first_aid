## 📚 Sources: I Don’t Know Where to Start

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of task initiation struggles and offer strategies for executive function scaffolding, breaking through overwhelm, reframing shame, externalizing next steps, and building momentum through micro-action.

---

### 🧠 Understanding ADHD & Task Initiation Challenges

- _ADHD 2.0_ — Describes how shame, overwhelm, and inertia result from executive function blocks.
    
- _Driven to Distraction_ — Establishes core symptoms like task paralysis and disorganization.
    
- _The Disorganized Mind_ — Explains the “confused feeling of where do I start?” and supports separating setup from task.
    
- _Smart but Scattered_ — Defines task initiation as a skill and offers tools like Post-it note sequencing and brainstorming with others.
    
- _How to ADHD_ — Validates that task initiation difficulty is rooted in executive dysfunction, not laziness, and shares scaffolding and barrier log tools.
    

---

### 🔍 Reframing Inertia & Shrinking Shame

- _The Anti-Planner_ — Says “You’re not fucking lazy” and offers micro-step tools like word-vomit lists and visual breakdowns.
    
- _Tiny Habits_ — Advocates shrinking behaviors until they're “laughably easy” and emphasizes prompts over willpower.
    
- _Tiny Experiments_ — Names “not knowing how to begin” as the #1 barrier to self-renewal and promotes action as learning.
    
- _A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD_ — Reframes avoidance and internal blocks, encourages externalizing the first step, and breaks down shame.
    
- _Order from Chaos_ — Connects overwhelm to identity shame and encourages checklists and asking for help as shame-free tools.
    

---

### 🗺️ Strategy Systems & Clarity Tools

- _Building a Second Brain_ — Provides clarity techniques like the PARA method, Intermediate Packets, and the Hemingway Bridge to help start.
    
- _Start Finishing_ — Outlines project pyramids, focus blocks, and “GATES” strengths as entry points into stuck projects.
    
- _Succeeding With Adult ADHD_ — Offers frameworks for micro-goals and reframing fear of beginning.
    
- _Unapologetically ADHD_ — Promotes external workflows and the idea that “you don’t need to remember what your system remembers.”
    
- _Real Self-Care_ — Encourages using an internal compass and saying no to external noise to reduce overwhelm.
    

---

### 🤝 Asking for Help & Co-Regulation

- _Motivational Interviewing_ — Introduces a guiding style to elicit someone’s own solutions.
    
- _The Coaching Habit_ — Encourages buddying up and asking clarifying questions like “What’s the first step?” and “And what else?”
    
- _Women with Attention Deficit Disorder_ — Emphasizes the role of a coach or partner in helping pick starting points.
    
- _Small Talk_ — Promotes body doubling and rejecting internalized “laziness” narratives.
    
- _Does ADHD Coaching Work?_ — Validates asking for help as a strategic and compassionate intervention.
    
- _The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook_ — Normalizes needing support and teaches how to advocate for help when the first step feels inaccessible.
