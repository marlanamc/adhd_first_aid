## 📚 Sources: It Feels Pointless

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological and emotional roots of ADHD-related demotivation — and offer strategies for reconnecting to purpose, reducing internal resistance, honoring your limits, and taking small steps that actually feel doable.

---

### 🧠 Understanding ADHD Motivation & Inertia

- **Kelly, K., & Ramundo, P. (2006). _You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!_.**  
    Names the emotional toll of ADHD and challenges the myth of laziness. Explains that forcing effort when the brain is depleted leads to shutdown.
    
- **Kemp, J., & Mitchelson, M. (2024). _The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook_.**  
    Validates how executive dysfunction, shame, and mismatched strategies contribute to the "what's the point?" feeling. Promotes tools, community, and compassion.
    
- **Dawson, P., & Guare, R. (2015). _Smart but Scattered Guide to Success_.**  
    Explains how limited executive function effort leads to avoidance. Suggests off-loading and reframing ADHD as an explanation — not an excuse.
    
- **Alexander-Grout, M. (2025). _Mad About Money_.**  
    Counters internalized shame and reinforces working _with_ your brain’s operating system instead of blaming yourself for differences.
    

---

### 🔍 Reframing Pointlessness as Information

- **Le Cunff, A.-L. (2025). _Tiny Experiments_.**  
    Frames procrastination as a signal, not a flaw. Suggests practical tools like task-pairing, body doubling, and curiosity over judgment.
    
- **Miller, W. R., & Rollnick, S. (2023). _Motivational Interviewing_.**  
    Emphasizes evoking _why_ before tackling _how_. Encourages understanding resistance instead of pushing through it.
    
- **Kegan, R., & Lahey, L. (2009). _Immunity to Change_.**  
    Explores hidden emotional blocks and competing commitments that sabotage motivation. Helps uncover what you’re really avoiding.
    
- **Donovan, D. (2022). _The Anti-Planner_.**  
    Validates the invisible barriers that make everything feel hard. Offers irreverent, flexible strategies and encourages seeking help from others.
    

---

### 🛠️ Action Through Acceptance & Experimentation

- **Abdaal, A. (2023). _Feel-Good Productivity_.**  
    Offers tools for reducing friction, finding next steps, and overcoming inertia by reconnecting to values and energy.
    
- **Kinzer, N., & Wright, P. D. (2024). _Unapologetically ADHD_.**  
    Describes the ADHD shame spiral and how rejection sensitivity fuels worthlessness. Promotes radical acceptance, values-aligned work, and tools like brain dumps and self-reflection.
    
- **Paul, J. (2018). _Order from Chaos_.**  
    Encourages reframing every failure as a data point. Connects organizing and effort to personal values and emotional healing.
    
- **Solden, S., Frank, M., & Littman, E. (2019). _A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD_.**  
    Validates emotional burnout and the weight of internalized expectations. Promotes support systems like coworking and reframing success through compassion and identity alignment.
