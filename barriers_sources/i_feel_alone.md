## 📚 Sources: I Feel Alone

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain how ADHD can contribute to chronic loneliness, social struggles, and internalized shame — and offer strategies for building connection, navigating RSD and masking, and reclaiming your worth with compassion and community.

---

### 🧠 Understanding Loneliness & ADHD

- **Hallowell, Edward M., & Ratey, John J.** _ADHD 2.0_ – Explains how ADHD amplifies loneliness and recommends connecting with other ADHD adults for shared support.
    
- **Kinzer, Nikki & Wright, Pete D.** _Unapologetically ADHD_ – Defines Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD) and how shame, masking, and low self-worth create emotional isolation.
    
- **Kustow, James.** _How to Thrive with Adult ADHD_ – Offers strategies for reducing shame and isolation, and highlights the emotional cost of masking.
    
- **Solden, Sari.** _Women with Attention Deficit Disorder_ – Explores how shame, gender norms, and invalidation fuel withdrawal and disconnection.
    
- **Ratey, Nancy A.** _The Disorganized Mind_ – Describes the role of ADHD coaching and accountability partners, and emphasizes that “You can’t do this alone.”
    
- **Donovan, Dani.** _The Anti-Planner_ – Encourages reaching out, explains how to set up support without feeling like a burden, and includes prompts for self-validation.
    

---

### 💔 The Emotional Toll of Shame & Masking

- **Pink, Richard & Pink, Roxanne.** _Small Talk_ – Identifies the false beliefs that ADHDers internalize (e.g. “I’m useless”) and the emotional fallout of masking.
    
- **Carder, Meredith.** _It All Makes Sense Now_ – Emphasizes the importance of community and self-acceptance to counteract feelings of shame and otherness.
    
- **Kelly, Kate & Ramundo, Peggy.** _You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!_ – Validates the ADHD experience and promotes group support and peer education.
    
- **Kemp, Jennifer & Mitchelson, Monique.** _The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook_ – Provides tools for self-compassion, emotional safety, and overcoming help-avoidance.
    
- **Lakshmin, Pooja.** _Real Self-Care_ – Frames receiving help and love as a skill you can develop, especially when you’ve been conditioned to feel undeserving.
    

---

### 🤝 Practical Tools for Building Connection

- **McCabe, Jessica.** _How to ADHD_ – Offers peer support, reframes loneliness through validation, and emphasizes that “You are not alone. You belong.”
    
- **Alexander-Grout, Maddy.** _Mad About Money_ – Suggests finding ADHD-safe accountability and support systems without shame.
    
- **Dawson, Peg & Guare, Richard.** _The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success_ – Recommends external scaffolding and emphasizes that everyone needs help sometimes.
    
- **Fagan, Abigail.** _“3 Common Negative Core Beliefs of People with ADHD,” Psychology Today_ – Describes how criticism and rejection lead to self-isolating beliefs.
