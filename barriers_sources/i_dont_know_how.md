## 📚 Sources: I Don’t Know How

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of task paralysis and offer strategies for shrinking tasks into steps, building scaffolding, reframing shame, and asking for help when you're stuck.

---

### 🧠 Understanding Executive Function and ADHD

- _The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success_ – Peg Dawson & Richard Guare  
    Offers practical ways to compensate for executive dysfunction using cues, task scaffolding, and support.
    
- _How to ADHD_ – Jessica McCabe  
    Breaks down why knowing what to do isn’t the same as knowing how to do it, due to working memory gaps, task initiation issues, and weak mental scaffolding.
    
- _You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!_ – Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo  
    Validates ADHD struggles and explains how to start building solutions when you feel stuck.
    
- _ADHD 2.0_ – Edward M. Hallowell & John J. Ratey  
    Introduces the "race car brain with bicycle brakes" analogy and emphasizes the power of externalizing steps and ditching shame.
    
- _Driven to Distraction_ – Edward M. Hallowell & John J. Ratey  
    Provides a foundational understanding of ADHD-related confusion and overwhelm.
    
- _The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook for Autism and ADHD_ – Jennifer Kemp & Monique Mitchelson  
    Highlights emotional barriers to asking for help, executive function gaps, and step-by-step self-advocacy.
    
- _Succeeding With Adult ADHD_ – Abigail L. Levrini  
    Gives concrete strategies for breaking goals into small, actionable pieces and setting process-based objectives.
    
- _Unapologetically ADHD_ – Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright  
    Encourages personalized systems and shifting mindset from shame to strategy when you feel directionless.

### 🧩 Overcoming Mental Blocks & Reframing Shame

- _How to Keep House While Drowning_ – KC Davis  
    Focuses on functional barriers and the emotional shutdown that can occur when you don’t know where to start.
    
- _It All Makes Sense Now_ – Meredith Carder  
    Explores how understanding your brain and leveraging accountability can help when things feel too big to begin.
    
- _Tiny Habits_ – BJ Fogg  
    Teaches how to lower the barrier to entry by making behaviors smaller and easier to start.
    
- _Tiny Experiments_ – Anne-Laure Le Cunff  
    Encourages experimentation and progress through small, non-linear steps — helpful when you don’t know how to proceed.
    
- _The Anti-Planner_ – Dani Donovan  
    Breaks down how to identify invisible mental barriers and cognitive distortions when tasks feel overwhelming or unclear.
    
- _Feel-Good Productivity_ – Ali Abdaal  
    Explains how fear and confusion create inertia, and how to regain momentum by addressing the emotional roots of procrastination.
    
- _Lazy, Crazy, and Disgusting_ – Alexandra Brewis & Amber Wutich  
    Provides insight into how shame and stigma interfere with action, reinforcing the need for support and compassion.

### 🛠️ Systems, Tools & Externalization

- _Building a Second Brain_ – Tiago Forte  
    Introduces PARA and progressive summarization to organize ideas for action and eliminate friction when getting started.
    
- _The PARA Method_ – Tiago Forte  
    Helps reduce overwhelm by creating flow between Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives.
    
- _The Coaching Habit_ – Michael Bungay Stanier  
    Advocates for simple coaching conversations with questions that guide people from insight to action.
    
- _The Power of Moments_ – Chip Heath & Dan Heath  
    Describes how insight and action can be sparked by small moments of clarity — useful when stuck in “I don’t know how.”
    
- _Order from Chaos_ – Jaclyn Paul  
    Offers checklists and routines to move forward when executive function gets in the way of starting.
    
- _The Disorganized Mind_ – Nancy Ratey  
    Shows how coaching and structure can turn a sense of helplessness into forward motion.
    

---

### 💬 Community, Coaching & Asking for Help

- _Real Self-Care_ – Pooja Lakshmin  
    Encourages value-based decision-making and compassion, even when you're unsure what to do next.
    
- _The Neurodiversity Edge_ – Maureen Dunne  
    Helps neurotypical and neurodivergent thinkers build mutual understanding — crucial when explaining “I don’t know how.”
    
- _Mad About Money_ – Maddy Alexander-Grout  
    Reinforces how small wins (like paying a single bill) can build momentum and reduce shame.
    
- _Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them_ – Richard & Roxanne Pink  
    Addresses limiting beliefs about executive dysfunction and offers clarity for taking first steps.
    
- _How to Thrive with Adult ADHD_ – James Kustow  
    Offers a step-by-step plan for ADHDers to grow without fear or perfectionism.
    
- _A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD_ – Sari Solden, Michelle Frank & Ellen Littman  
    Combines executive function guidance with emotional healing and community support.
    
- _Women with Attention Deficit Disorder_ – Sari Solden  
    Emphasizes the deep shame ADHDers may carry and how naming that shame can unlock help-seeking.
    
- _Motivational Interviewing_ – William R. Miller & Stephen Rollnick  
    Outlines a method for drawing out internal motivation and identifying your own “how” with support.
    
- _Does ADHD Coaching Work?_ – Medical News Today  
    Confirms the effectiveness of ADHD coaching in helping people figure out where to start and how to move forward.
    
- _ADDA (Attention Deficit Disorder Association)_ –  
    A key resource hub reminding users that they’re not alone, and that “not knowing how” doesn’t mean something is wrong with you.
