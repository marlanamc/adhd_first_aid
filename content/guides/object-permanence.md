# 👀 Object Permanence (Out of Sight, Out of Mind)

> [!quote]
> "I bought more toothpaste because I thought we were out. We have 6 tubes in the drawer."

---

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Object Permanence** issues mean if you can't see an item, your brain forgets it exists.
- This leads to **"Doom Piles"**, rotten food in drawers, and buying duplicates.
- Traditional "hide it away" organizing **fails ADHDers**.
- We need **Visual Storage**, **Point of Performance** placement, and **Landing Strips**.

---

## 🧐 The Science

Technically, "Object Permanence" is a developmental stage babies go through. For ADHDers, we use the term loosely to describe a glitch in our working memory.

> [!info]
> If an object is not in our direct visual field, our brain "deletes" it from our active RAM to save energy.

This is why:

- You forget vegetables in the crisper drawer until they turn to liquid.
- You leave vital documents on your desk instead of filing them.
- You have "Doom Piles" (Don't Organize, Only Move).

---

## ❌ Why Traditional Organizing Fails Us

Traditional advice says: _"Put everything away in drawers and bins. Clear surfaces."_

For an ADHD brain, a drawer is a black hole. Things go in and never come out. When we try to be "neat," we often end up losing our tools, forgetting our meds, or buying duplicates.

---

## ✅ ADHD-Friendly Strategies

### 1. Visual Storage (transparent is best)

- ✅ **Clear Bins:** Use clear plastic bins so you can see the contents.
- ✅ **Wire Baskets:** Better than opaque boxes.
- ✅ **Remove Doors:** Some ADHDers take cabinet doors off the kitchen cupboards or closets. (Extreme, but effective).

### 2. The "Point of Performance" Rule

Store items exactly where you use them, not where they "belong."

> [!example]
> **Meds:** Keep them next to the coffee maker or toothbrush, not in the medicine cabinet.
> **Deodorant:** Keep one in the bathroom, one in your gym bag, one in your desk.
> **Scissors:** Buy 5 pairs. Put one in every room.

### 3. The "Landing Strip"

You will dump your keys/wallet/phone when you walk in. Don't fight it.
Create a "Landing Strip" at your door.

- A bowl or hook _exactly_ where your hand naturally goes.
- Make it impossible to miss.

### 4. Vegetable Preservation

Stop using the crisper drawer for veggies. It is where produce goes to die.

> [!tip]
> **The Fridge Hack:** Put condiments (which don't rot quickly) in the drawers. Put fresh veggies on the door shelves or right in the middle at eye level.

### 5. "Doom Pile" Management

We all make piles using the "I'll need this later" logic. Instead of fighting the pile: **Contain the pile.**

1.  Get a nice looking basket or tray.
2.  Put the pile _inside_ it.
3.  Now it looks like a design choice, but you can still see the top layer.

---

## 💡 Mantra

> [!note]
> **"If I need to use it, I must be able to see it. If I need to store it, I must label it."**
