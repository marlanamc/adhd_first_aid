# 👯 Body Doubling: The Magic of Company

> [!quote]
> "Just sit there. You don't have to help. Just sit there."

---

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Body Doubling** is working in the presence of someone else who isn't necessarily helping you.
- It reduces **task anxiety** and acts as an "anchor" for your attention.
- It works due to **social facilitation** (we perform better when watched).
- You can do it **in-person** or **virtually**.

---

## 🤔 What is Body Doubling?

**Body Doubling** is the practice of doing a task in the presence of another person. The other person (the "body double") doesn't need to help with the task, or even talk to you. They just need to _be_ there.

It sounds simple, but for ADHD brains, it is often more effective than medication, timers, or threats.

> [!info]
> **The Science:** Humans are social creatures. When we see someone else being calm and productive ("social modeling"), our mirror neurons fire and help regulate our own nervous system, dampening the panic of "I can't do this."

---

## 🛠️ How to Do It (The Menu)

### Option 1: The "Passive" Double

_Good for: Cleaning, cooking, taxes._

- Ask a friend to come over and sit on your couch while you clean.
- They can read, scroll TikTok, or nap.
- **The Rule:** They just have to exist in the room.

### Option 2: The "Virtual" Double

_Good for: Computer work, admin, emails._

- FaceTime a friend. Mute the audio.
- Say: "I'm going to work for 45 minutes."
- Prop the phone up so you can see them working too.

### Option 3: Community Doubling

_Good for: When friends aren't available._

- Use platforms like **Focusmate** (video co-working).
- Join an ADHD Discord with a "Study Hall" voice channel.
- Go to a coffee shop (strangers are essentially unpaid body doubles).

---

## 🚫 Common Pitfalls

❌ **Too much chatting:** If you and your body double start gossiping, the productivity breaks.

> [!tip]
> Agree on "quiet hours" or set a timer: "We can chat for 5 mins, then 25 mins of silence."

❌ **The "Judgey" Double:** If your body double is stressed or critical ("Why are you doing it that way?"), it will backfire.

> [!warning]
> Only choose people who make you feel safe and un-judged.

❌ **Dependence:** While great, try not to _only_ be able to work with a double. Use it as a tool for the hardest tasks.

---

## 🗣️ Scripts: How to Ask

Sometimes saying "be my body double" sounds weird. Try these:

> [!example]
> "Hey, I really need to wash the dishes but I'm stuck. Would you mind just sitting in the kitchen with me for 15 minutes? You can just scroll on your phone. It just helps me focus."

> [!example]
> "I have a deadline. Can we do a silent Zoom call for an hour? Just so I don't wander off?"
