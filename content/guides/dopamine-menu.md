# ⚡ The Dopamine Menu

> [!note]
> When you have ADHD, your brain is constantly seeking stimulation. If you don't choose the source, your brain will choose it for you (often doomscrolling or sugar).

---

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Boredom is painful** for ADHD brains; it feels like a lack of oxygen.
- We usually grab **"junk dopamine"** (phone, snacks) because it's fast and easy.
- A **Dopamine Menu** is a pre-made list of activities for different energy levels.
- You need **Appetizers** (quick), **Main Courses** (deep), and **Desserts** (consumed intentionally).

---

## 🍽️ What is a Dopamine Menu?

A **Dopamine Menu** (originally coined by Eric Tivers and popularized by Jessica McCabe) is a curated list of activities that give you stimulation, categorized by how much energy they take and "how filling" they are.

Think of it like a restaurant menu. You don't always want a 5-course meal (too much effort), and you shouldn't only eat candy (quick fix but leaves you feeling bad). You need options for every appetite.

---

## 🥗 The Menu Sections

### 1. The Appetizers (Quick Dopamine)

_Usually take 5-10 minutes. Good for transitions or small breaks._

✅ **Try adding these:**

- Petting the dog/cat
- Doing 10 jumping jacks
- Listening to one favorite song
- Eating a crunchy snack
- Drinking a glass of cold water
- A quick doodle on a sticky note

### 2. The Main Courses (Deep Satisfaction)

_These are activities that truly fill your cup and leave you feeling restored. They usually require more time._

✅ **The "Real Food" for your brain:**

- Going for a walk in nature
- Playing a musical instrument
- Working on a hobby (knitting, coding, painting)
- Reading a book (paper, not screen)
- Cooking a nice meal
- Deep conversation with a friend

### 3. The Sides (Add-ons)

_Things you can do WHILE doing boring tasks to make them bearable._

> [!tip]
> Use these to "hack" boring tasks like laundry or data entry.

- Listening to a podcast while cleaning
- Using a fidget toy during meetings
- Body doubling (working with someone else nearby)
- Listening to lo-fi beats while studying

### 4. The Desserts (The "Doom" Box)

_Highly stimulating but low nutritional value. It's okay to have them, but practice moderation._

- Social media scrolling
- Mobile games
- Reality TV binge
- Online shopping

> [!warning]
> **The Rule for Desserts:** Don't use them to _start_ your day, or you might not be able to stop. Save them for designated "treat" times.

---

## 🛠️ Build Your Menu

**Don't just read this. Do it now.**

1.  **Brainstorm:** Grab a sticky note or open your Notes app.
2.  **Write 3 Appetizers:** What are 3 things you can do in 5 mins that make you smile?
3.  **Write 1 Main Course:** What is that one hobby you miss doing?
4.  **Post It:** Put this list on your fridge or save it as your phone background.

---

## 💡 Why It Works

ADHD brains are low on dopamine. When we feel "bored," it can actualy be physically painful. We often reach for the easiest source of dopamine (phone), but that often drains us further.

> [!info]
> By having a "pre-approved" list of stimulating activities, you remove the decision fatigue of "what should I do now?" and make it easier to choose something that actually makes you feel good.
