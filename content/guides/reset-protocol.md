# 🚑 The Emergency 'Reset' Protocol

> [!danger]
> **Use this guide when:** You haven't showered in 3 days, there are no clean dishes, you're overwhelmed to the point of paralysis, and you feel like crying.

---

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Stop fighting.** You are at capacity. Your goal is survival, not perfection.
- **Triage the body first:** Meds, water, food.
- **Clear visual noise:** Box up the clutter (don't sort it).
- **Sanitation only:** Wash ONE dish. Wash essentials only.
- **Reset the nervous system:** Sensory shower.

---

## 🛑 Step 0: Stop Fighting the Reality

You cannot "muscle through" this. You are in a state of depletion. Acceptance is the first step.

> [!quote]
> Say this out loud: _"I am currently at capacity. I am not lazy; I am out of gas. My goal today is not to 'fix everything,' it is to survive."_

---

## 🔋 Step 1: Biological Triage (10 mins)

Do not touch a single chore yet. Your machine (body) is overheating.

✅ **Checklist:**

- [ ] **Meds:** Have you taken them? If no, take them.
- [ ] **Water:** Chug one glass. Right now.
- [ ] **Blood Sugar:** Eat something that requires zero prep. A spoonful of peanut butter, a slice of cheese, a handful of nuts.
- [ ] **Sensory Check:** Is it too loud? Put on headphones. Too bright? Dim the lights. Too hot? Change clothes.

---

## 📦 Step 2: The "Doom Box" Sweep (15 mins)

The visual clutter is draining your battery. We aren't organizing; we are clearing visual noise.

1.  Get a laundry basket or a big cardboard box.
2.  Go to the area that stresses you out the most (usually living room or bedroom).
3.  Set a timer for 10 minutes.
4.  **The Sweep:** Pick up everything that doesn't belong and throw it in the box. Do not sort it. Do not look at it. Just clear the flat surfaces (floor, desk, table).
5.  **The Secret:** Put the box in a closet or corner. Cover it with a blanket if needed.

> [!note]
> _You are allowed to deal with this later._ Today is about clearing space to breathe.

---

## 🍽️ Step 3: The "Sanitation" Run (15 mins)

Forget "cleaning." We are doing "sanitation" — just enough to be functional.

> [!tip]
> **Permissible Shortcut:** It is morally neutral to use paper plates during burnout. If you have them, use them.

1.  **Trash only:** Walk through with a big trash bag. Throw away obvious garbage.
2.  **Dishes Triage:** You don't have to wash them all.
    - Wash **one** bowl, **one** spoon, and **one** cup.
3.  **Laundry Triage:** Do you have clean underwear for tomorrow?
    - **Yes?** Ignore the laundry pile.
    - **No?** Wash _only_ underwear and socks. Leave the rest.

---

## 🚿 Step 4: The Reset Shower

This isn't a hygiene shower; it's a nervous system reset.

- Make the water hot (or cold, if you need a shock).
- Sit on the floor of the shower.
- Let the water hit your back for 5 minutes.
- Imagine the static noise in your brain washing down the drain.
- Put on clean, soft clothes (pyjamas are fine).

---

## 🛌 Step 5: Permissible Rest

You have reset your body and your immediate environment. You are safe.
Now, you must rest _without guilt_.

> [!warning]
> Watching TV while feeling guilty is not rest; it's stress.

Watching TV while saying "I have done my Reset, and now I am refueling" is rest.

**You did it. You survived. That is enough.**
