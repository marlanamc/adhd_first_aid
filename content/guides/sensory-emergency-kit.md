# 🎧 The Sensory Emergency Kit

> [!note]
> Sensory overload isn't "being dramatic." It's your nervous system registering safety threats because the lights are too bright or the tag on your shirt is scratching you.

---

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Sensory Overload** can look like sudden rage, inability to speak, or panic.
- You need a **physical kit** to intervene immediately.
- The kit needs: **Audio Control**, **Tactile Regulators**, **Smell**, and **Visual Blockers**.
- **Shock (Sour candy/Cold)** works fastest to stop a spiral.

---

## 🚨 Signs You Need the Kit

If you notice these, you are in the "Yellow Zone" (warning):

- ⚠️ Sudden irrational rage at a small sound (chewing, clicking).
- ⚠️ Inability to understand what someone is saying (auditory processing creates "fog").
- ⚠️ Feeling like your skin is "tight" or crawling.
- ⚠️ Strong urge to bolt or hide.

When this happens, you need immediate physical intervention.

---

## 🎒 Build Your Kit

Grab a small pouch (pencil case or makeup bag size) that stays in your main bag/car.

### 1. Audio Control (Crucial)

- ✅ **Loop Earplugs / Flare Audio:** Lowers background decibels while letting you hear speech.
- ✅ **Noise Cancelling Headphones:** For total shutdowns.
- ✅ **Emergency Playlist:** A pre-made playlist of brown noise, rain sounds, or one specific "safe song" on repeat.

### 2. Tactile Regulators

- ✅ **Fidgets:** Something subtle (spinner ring) and something satisfying (Pop-it or heavy metal roller).
- ✅ **Texture Strip:** A piece of velvet or satin in your pocket to rub.
- ✅ **Weighted Object:** A heavy stone or a weighted lap pad (if at a desk).

### 3. Olfactory (Smell) Reset

Smell is the fastest meaningful route to the brain.

- ✅ **Essential Oil Roller:** Lavender (calming) or Peppermint (grounding).
- ✅ **Coffee Beans:** Small jar of beans to sniff (resets the nose).

### 4. Visual Blockers

- ✅ **Sunglasses:** Wear them indoors. It's okay. It signals to others "I am having a headache" (socially acceptable) while protecting your brain.
- ✅ **Hat/Hoodie:** To create a "blinder" effect.

### 5. Oral input (The Secret Weapon)

> [!tip]
> **Sour Shock:** Keep "Warheads" or "Toxic Waste" candy. The intense sourness shocks the vagus nerve and can snap your brain out of a panic loop immediately.

- **Chewelry:** Or really crunchy snacks (almonds, gum).

---

## 🛡️ Usage Protocol

**Phase 1: Early Warning**

- _Symptom:_ Getting irritable.
- _Action:_ Put in Loops/Earplugs. Drink water.

**Phase 2: The Red Zone**

- _Symptom:_ Want to scream.
- _Action:_ Retreat to bathroom/car. Eat sour candy. Put on hoodie. Rock back and forth (stimming regulates the vestibular system).

**Phase 3: The Aftermath**

- You will be exhausted (the "adrenal dump").
- Do not return to high-demand tasks immediately.

---

## 🗣️ Script for Explaining It

> [!example]
> _"I'm getting a bit of a sensory migraine from the lights/noise. I'm going to pop my sunglasses on/step out for 5 minutes so I can stay focused. Thanks for understanding."_
