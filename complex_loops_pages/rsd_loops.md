## 💔 Rejection Sensitivity Loops

Rejection Sensitivity Dysphoria isn’t just a feeling,  it’s a **spiral**. A comment, text, or silence can launch hours of **self-doubt**, **rumination**, and **emotional pain**. You’re not overreacting,  you’re wired to feel interpersonal threat more intensely, and it’s exhausting.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
You feel it **deeply** because you **care deeply**. **Rejection**, even the **possibility of it**, can hit like a **wound**. That doesn't mean you're **dramatic**. It means your brain is **wired to feel threat** in connection. You don't need to **toughen up**. You need **tools** that help you **pause**, **process**, and **protect your peace**.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
**Not everyone is rejecting you**. Not every **delay**, **look**, or **silence** is about you. **RSD isn't your fault**, but it becomes your **responsibility** when you let it **shape your relationships**. **Sit with the discomfort**. **Don't react**. **Don't spiral**. You're **stronger than this loop**.

### 🧠 Why Rejection Sensitivity Loops Are Hard with ADHD

- You might:
  - Spiral after a text or a vague look
  - Assume someone's silence means they hate you
  - Rehearse arguments that never happened
  - Withdraw or lash out to avoid being hurt
  - Feel stuck and unmotivated after rejection
  - Say yes to everything to avoid disapproval

- Here's what's really going on:
  - 💥 **Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria** triggers real emotional pain, like a punch to your nervous system  
  - 📉 **Low self-worth** turns small feedback into a referendum on your value  
  - 🔁 **Negative self-talk** creates loops of "I'm too much," "I'm unlovable," "I always mess this up"  
  - 🚪 **Avoidance and people-pleasing** become armor to dodge future pain  
  - 🧊 **Paralysis** sets in from fear of failure or judgment, it's safer not to try  
  - 🧠 **Working memory overload** means spirals crowd out action or focus

### ⚓ Core Principles for ADHD-Friendly RSD Management

- **Name it to tame it**: RSD spirals lose power when you recognize them as brain patterns, not truth  
- **Pause before reacting**: Your first emotional impulse in RSD is often not your wisest response  
- **External validation isn't your worth**: You are valuable regardless of others' responses  
- **Motion breaks emotion**: Moving your body can interrupt spiraling thoughts  
- **Connection heals**: Isolation feeds RSD, safe relationships break the cycle

### 🛠️ Strategies for Rejection Sensitivity Loops

#### 💬 Notice, Name, and Normalize

- **Say it out loud:** “This is an RSD spiral. It feels real, but it might not _be_ real.”

- **Use the N.I.C.E. technique:**

    - **Notice** what’s happening
    
    - **Inhale** slowly to calm your body
    
    - **Comfort** yourself like you would a friend
    
    - **Explore** the trigger once the panic settles
    
- **Reality check gently:** Ask, “What else _might_ be true?” before assuming rejection

- **Write it down or voice memo** to break the loop and externalize thoughts


#### 🧠 Protect Your Brain from Loops

- **Flip the script:** Challenge thoughts like “I’m too much” with “I’m allowed to have needs”

- **Label ADHD lies:** “I’m lazy” → “My brain needs scaffolding, not shame”

- **Reframe rejection as redirection:** Not every “no” is personal

- **Create a self-trust file:** Keep screenshots or notes of kind words, compliments, or wins

- **Vent safely:** Rant in a journal or record a voice memo instead of sending the angry text


#### 🛠 Break the Freeze with Gentle Action

- **Break tasks into pebbles**: Choose something so small it feels silly, send one email, drink water
- **Try the "mood follows action" trick**: Move your body or tidy a corner to shift your state
- **Use "body doubling"**: Work near someone (IRL or virtually) to feel anchored
- **Build a "low-risk" to-do list**: Tasks that feel safe, easy, and not rejection-prone
- **Talk to a "sympathetic encourager"**: Someone who won't dismiss your spiral but won't fuel it either


#### 🧱 Strengthen Boundaries and Resilience

- **Name people-pleasing when it shows up:** “I’m saying yes out of fear, not desire”

- **Delay reactive texts:** Wait 20 minutes before replying to anything emotionally charged

- **Say no with kindness, not guilt:** “I’d love to, but I don’t have the capacity right now”

- **Get comfy with discomfort:** Rejection _hurts_, but it won’t kill you. Let it pass through, not root in.

- **Use scripts for hard moments:** “Hey, can I check in? I noticed a shift and just want to be clear.”


#### 🤝 Build Long-Term Support

- **Work with an ADHD coach or therapist** who gets RSD and executive dysfunction

- **Join neurodivergent spaces** where your emotional depth isn’t “too much”

- **Talk about it with trusted people**: Say, "Sometimes my brain sees rejection where there isn't any. I may need clarity."
- **Make a post-rejection routine**: Comfort items, movement, nature, hydration
- **Practice receiving help**: Ask for support even if it feels vulnerable


#### 💥 What To Do When It's Already Happening

- **Say: "This is RSD. I can survive this feeling"**
- **Breathe and delay action**: Don't text, quit, or lash out right away
- **Switch locations or tasks**: Even a tiny move can shift the spiral
- **Contact someone safe**: "Can I talk this out before I act on it?"
- **Recover with kindness**: Hydrate, rest, or treat yourself like you matter
- **Capture the learning**: Later, ask: "What can I do differently next time?"

### 🧭 Rejection Sensitivity Loops ≠ Morality

- You're not broken. You're not too much. Your brain just feels emotional pain more intensely.  
- RSD lies, but you don't have to believe it. Your worth isn't decided by silence, feedback, or a look.
> You can pause the spiral, soothe the pain, and stay rooted in your truth.  
> Connection gets safer when you feel safe inside.

### ✨ Encouragement to Take With You
- If you noticed an RSD spiral and named it, that's a win.  
- If you paused before reacting emotionally, that's a win.  
- If you reached out for support instead of isolating, that's a win.  
> You're learning to trust yourself even when your brain feels under threat.
> You are worthy of love and connection, exactly as you are. RSD doesn't get to decide that.
