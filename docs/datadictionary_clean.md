# 📚 ADHD Strategies Database: Data Dictionary

## 🎯 Overview
This data dictionary defines all categories, tags, and classification systems used in the ADHD strategies database. Each category is designed to capture specific aspects of the ADHD experience and help users find relevant strategies.

---

## 😊 Feelings Categories

*How you're feeling right now - what's going on emotionally or mentally*

### 🌪️ Emotional Turmoil
*You can't think — your feelings are loud, heavy, or painful*

- **Ashamed** - You feel like you're not good enough or that you've disappointed someone — maybe even yourself
- **Anxious** - Your mind is racing with worry or fear about what might happen
- **Guilty** - You feel bad about something you did (or didn't do), and it's weighing on you
- **Stressed** - There's too much coming at you, and it's pressing on your body and brain

### 🔋 Low Energy
*You can't start — you feel blank, still, slow*

- **Stuck** - You want to move forward but just can't — like something invisible is holding you in place
- **Hopeless** - It feels like nothing will work, and you're not sure it's even worth trying

### 🧠 Mental Clutter
*You can't focus — your thoughts are noisy or fractured*

- **Forgetful** - Things keep slipping — plans, ideas, or even why you walked into the room
- **Scattered** - Your thoughts are all over the place, and it's hard to grab onto just one

### 🚨 Too Much at Once
*You can't cope — your system is overloaded or collapsing*

- **Overwhelmed** - Everything feels like too much at once — too many tabs open in your mind
- **Overstimulated** - Your senses are overloaded — lights, sounds, and tasks are all too loud
- **Burned Out** - You've been pushing for too long, and now you feel empty, drained, or numb

### 🏃 Physical/Body Feelings
*You can't regulate — your body feels off or needs something*

- **Restless** - You feel fidgety, like you need to move or do something
- **Drained** - You feel depleted, like your battery is at 5%
- **Wired** - You feel overly energized but can't channel it productively

### 🎭 Emotional Complexity
*You can't process — your emotions are layered or conflicted*

- **Frustrated** - You're annoyed or irritated, things aren't working
- **Defeated** - You feel like you've tried everything and nothing works
- **Numb** - You don't feel much of anything, emotionally flat

### 👥 Social/Interpersonal
*You can't connect — you feel isolated or misunderstood*

- **Misunderstood** - You feel like others don't get your struggles
- **Lonely** - You feel isolated in your ADHD experience

*What's getting in your way - the specific problems you're experiencing*

- **Can't Start** - Tasks feel impossible to begin
- **Can't Finish** - Tasks get abandoned partway
- **Avoidant** - Avoiding tasks on purpose or by instinct
- **Frozen/Shut Down** - Paralyzed under pressure or overload
- **Avoiding Routine** - Rejecting repetitive structure
- **Task Initiation** - Specific difficulty with starting tasks

### 💭 Mental State Issues
- **Bored** - Lack of stimulation or engagement
- **Anxious** - Fear, dread, or unease interfering with action
- **Depressed / Hopeless** - Emotionally flattened or directionless
- **Spiraling** - Thoughts are racing, looping, or catastrophizing
- **Can't Decide** - Stuck in indecision or perfectionism
- **Rejection Sensitive** - Feel crushed or defensive at even small signs of criticism
- **Perfectionist** - Fixate on doing it "right," so nothing gets done
- **Time Blind** - Misjudge how long things take or when to start
- **Hyperfocused** - Get absorbed and can't stop when you need to
- **Anxiety Management** - Struggling with worry, fear, or anxious thoughts
- **Emotional Regulation** - Managing emotions, reactions, and emotional intensity

### 📅 Time + Planning Issues
- **Poor Time Estimation** - Chronically underestimate how long things take
- **Missed Appointments** - Trouble keeping track of events or deadlines
- **Overcommitted** - Said yes to too much, now overwhelmed

### 🧠 Memory / Organization Issues
- **Forgetful** - Lose track of details, plans, or ideas
- **Disorganized** - Struggle to manage clutter, routines, or mental systems

### 🛌 Energy / Body Issues
- **Sleep Issues** - Trouble falling or staying asleep
- **Sensory Overload** - Environment is too loud, bright, chaotic
- **Can't Focus** - Mind wanders or won't stay on track

## 🚧 Barrier Types

*The underlying challenges that make things difficult*

### 🧠 Cognitive Barriers
- **Time Blindness** - Misjudge how long things take or when to start
- **Working Memory Failures** - Forget steps, details, or what you were just doing
- **Task Initiation** - Struggle to get started, even with simple tasks
- **Executive Dysfunction** - Struggle to plan, prioritize, or follow through on tasks
- **Inertia** - Stay stuck in one mode — can't shift gears easily
- **Decision Fatigue** - Feel drained by too many choices, even small ones
- **Decision Paralysis** - Freeze when faced with decisions — fear making the wrong one
- **Perfectionism** - Fixate on doing it "right," so nothing gets done

### 💥 Emotional Barriers
- **Emotional Dysregulation** - React strongly to small triggers or feel stuck in intense emotions
- **Rejection Sensitivity** - Feel crushed or defensive at even small signs of criticism
- **Masking & Shame** - Hide your needs or struggles to appear "normal," then crash later
- **Low Motivation** - Struggle to care or act, even when you want the outcome

### 🌪️ Sensory / Physical Barriers
- **Overstimulation** - Feel overwhelmed by noise, lights, notifications, or general input
- **Environmental Cues** - Your surroundings make it harder to focus or take action

### 🔧 Structural Barriers
- **Task Structuring** - Difficulty breaking down or organizing complex tasks
- **Working Memory Engagement** - Struggle to hold information in mind while working
