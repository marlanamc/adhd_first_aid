## 🫥 ADHD First Aid Template: Numb
It’s not apathy • It’s not that you don’t care • It’s ADHD meeting emotional shutdown, nervous system overload, and a brain that’s run out of safe places to land.

Numbness can feel like nothing — but it often follows _everything_. When you’ve been overstimulated, overwhelmed, or emotionally flooded for too long, your brain might hit the brakes completely. You stop feeling, stop thinking, stop reacting. It’s not because you’re cold or careless — it’s because you’re over capacity.

ADHD doesn’t always show up as hyper — sometimes it shows up as nothing at all.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
Numbness is protection. When everything’s too much, your brain sometimes shuts down. That doesn’t mean you’re broken — it means you’ve hit capacity. Give yourself a gentle sensory reset. Music, nature, light movement. Come back slowly.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
You’re not feeling because you’ve been ignoring your needs. Get up. Eat something. Move your body. Numb is a signal — not a home. Shake yourself out of autopilot.

### 🧠 Why ADHD Can Cause Emotional Numbness:

- 🔋 Burnout from constant masking or high-pressure environments
    
- ⚡ Sensory overload that shuts down processing instead of activating it
    
- 🔁 Emotional dysregulation cycling into emotional _shutdown_
    
- 😔 Rejection or shame leading to protective disconnection
    
- ⏳ Time blindness masking how long you’ve gone without tending to your needs

---

### 1. 🧠 Recognize What Numbness Is Trying to Say

Numbness isn’t apathy — it’s often a full-body freeze response to chronic stress, overwhelm, or pain.

**Try this:**

- 🤔 **Ask yourself**: “What is my body trying to tell me right now?”
    
- 🩺 **Check for signals**: blankness, disconnect, emotional emptiness, physical stillness, “I don’t know” answers
    
- 🔎 Look for root causes:
    
    - Burnout or prolonged masking
        
    - Emotional avoidance or trauma response
        
    - People-pleasing or overexertion
        
    - Unmet needs (sleep, food, space)
        

> 💡 Tip: Numbness is your nervous system pulling the emergency brake. Start by noticing — not fixing.

---

### 2. 🌬 Start With the Body — Reconnect Gently

You may not feel much right now. That’s okay. Use small physical cues to bring yourself back online.

**Try this:**

- 🌫️ **Calm your nervous system**: Deep breathing, box breathing, or inhale 6 / exhale 8
    
- 🧍 **Move something**: Rock, sway, stretch, shake out limbs
    
- 🤲 **Apply sensory pressure**: Weighted blanket, tight hug, firm squeeze on knees
    
- 🎧 **Block noise**: Retreat to a dark room, wear headphones, soften lighting
    
- 🐾 **Find a grounding “island”**: Warm drink, pet a dog, hold a comfort object
    

> 💡 Tip: You don’t need to _feel better_ right now — just feel _something safe_.

---

### 3. 💬 Gently Reconnect with Emotions

Your feelings didn’t disappear — they’ve just gone quiet. Let’s give them a safe space to return.

**Try this:**

- 🧠 **“Name it to tame it”**: Use an emotion wheel or body scan to describe sensations
    
- ✍️ **Free-write for 10 minutes**: No filter — just describe what’s happening
    
- 🔄 **Separate the physical from the emotional**: “My chest feels heavy” vs. “I feel sad”
    
- 🎨 **Draw or map how your body feels** (heat, cold, weight, etc.)
    
- 🧘 **Reframe emotions as messengers**: “What might this numbness be protecting me from?”
    

> 💡 Tip: You don’t need full clarity. Curiosity is enough.

---

### 4. 🪫 Reduce Demands & Reclaim a Tiny Sense of Control

Numbness often comes from decision fatigue, emotional overload, or identity exhaustion. Let’s make things lighter.

**Try this:**

- 🧾 **Brain dump**: Externalize everything you’re holding (or _not_ holding)
    
- 🧮 **Do less to unlock more**: Cancel non-essentials or take a guilt-free “Reitoff” day
    
- 🪜 **Start with “baby steps”**: Pick one 5-minute task or break something big into a tiny action
    
- 📅 **Track your energy**: Use “red/yellow/green” zones to plan your day
    
- 🧠 **Use tools to offload memory**: Reminders, whiteboards, sticky notes — let the system carry it
    

> 💡 Tip: You don’t need motivation. You need momentum — even the smallest kind.

---

### 5. 🤝 Let Someone In — Slowly, Safely

Numbness loves isolation. Connection is often the antidote.

**Try this:**

- 📞 Text someone you trust with “I feel off today. Just wanted to say hi.”
    
- 🙋 Ask a friend or coach to help you with one small task
    
- 🧍 Body double with someone (even on video or chat)
    
- 🧠 Seek a therapist or ADHD coach if the numbness feels persistent or scary
    
- ✋ Set boundaries to protect your energy — “Not now” is a valid answer
    

> 💡 Tip: Support doesn’t mean fixing everything. It means not holding it all alone.
