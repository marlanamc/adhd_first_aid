## ⚡ ADHD First Aid Template: Wired
It’s not true energy • It’s not productivity • It’s ADHD meeting overstimulation, adrenaline spikes, and a nervous system stuck in go-mode.

That wired feeling might look like motivation, but underneath is often anxiety, overcompensation, or pure nervous tension. With ADHD, you might chase deadlines, jump between tasks, or talk a mile a minute — not because you’re energized, but because your brain is flooded with stimulation and has no safe “off” switch.

It’s not a flow state — it’s a flood state.

**🧸 Gentle Advice:**  
You’re not actually energized — you’re overstimulated. That buzz in your body might feel productive, but it’s adrenaline, not peace. Slow down. Ground yourself. You don’t have to run on hyperdrive to be valuable.

**🔥 Stern Advice:**  
You’re not focused — you’re frazzled. The caffeine, the chaos, the scrolling — it’s frying your nervous system. Sit still. Turn things off. Don’t confuse being “on” with being in control.

### 🧠 Why ADHD Can Feel Like Being Constantly Wired:

- ⚡ Emotional and sensory dysregulation create nervous system overdrive
    
- 📱 Dopamine-chasing (scrolling, snacking, overworking) keeps you overstimulated
    
- 🕓 Poor sleep, over-caffeination, and lack of transitions make rest feel impossible
    
- 🔄 Hyperfocus aftermath can leave your body buzzing and restless
    
- ❌ Difficulty winding down leads to “tired but wired” syndrome — exhausted but unable to stop

---

### 1. 🎛️ Recognize What “Wired” Feels Like in Your Body

Wired isn’t just energetic — it can feel **irritable, stuck in your head, or trapped in motion**.

**Try this:**

- 🧠 Describe it: “I feel like a motor that won’t shut off” or “My thoughts are flipping like TV channels.”
    
- 📝 Brain dump: Get the noise out of your head and onto a page or into a voice note.
    
- 🔍 Early signals: Tapping, jaw tension, re-reading the same line, overplanning — all signs it’s time to reset.
    

> 💡 Tip: You don’t need a reason to feel this way. Wired is a state, not a flaw.

---

### 2. 🏃‍♂️ Discharge the Extra Energy

You can’t think your way out — you’ve got to move it through.

**Try this:**

- 🤸 Take a short, brisk walk or do jumping jacks
    
- 🧘 Shake it out — arms, legs, shoulders. Stomp or stretch
    
- 🪑 Fidget freely: roll on a yoga ball, swivel chair, or use putty/fidget toys
    
- 🧶 Try rhythmic movement like knitting, pacing, dancing, or rocking
    

> 💡 Tip: Even 2–3 minutes of movement can give your brain a new track.

---

### 3. 🌬️ Regulate Your Environment & Nervous System

Your brain is in high-alert mode. Help it come back down.

**Try this:**

- 🌬️ Breathing: Box breathing, humming, or a long slow exhale
    
- 🪨 Grounding: Try the “5 things” exercise or focus on pressure (hugging a pillow, wall sits)
    
- 🔇 Sensory support: Dim lights, quiet music, tight clothes, or noise-canceling headphones
    
- 🛁 Reset tools: Warm bath, heavy blanket, dark room, or nature sounds
    

> 💡 Tip: Overstimulated doesn’t mean broken — just full. Create calm input to balance out the storm.

---

### 4. 🧠 Calm the Mental Spiral

Your brain might be racing with ideas, fear, or endless “what ifs.”

**Try this:**

- 💬 Flip the script: “I’m not losing control — I’m overloaded with input.”
    
- 🧩 Break down any tasks into 1 tiny step (open the tab, make the call, etc.)
    
- 🧠 Reframe: Label the hyper energy as excitement if it fits. It helps redirect it.
    
- ✨ Add “containment” to your thoughts: set a 5-minute timer to spiral, then pause
    

> 💡 Tip: You don’t have to stop thinking — just slow the loop.

---

### 5. 🧍‍♀️ Reach Out and Rest

Wired often spirals in isolation. Reach out — and downshift together.

**Try this:**

- 🫂 Talk it out with someone who gets it
    
- 🧭 Body double for gentle, non-pressure accountability
    
- 🛑 Say no to new demands while you're still riding the energy wave
    
- 💤 Rest without guilt — even if that means staring at a wall or wrapping yourself up like a burrito
    

> 💡 Tip: Stillness might feel foreign. That’s okay. Rest is a practice, not a reward.

---

### 🔁 Starting Point Suggestions

→ Do 1 minute of shaking, then 1 minute of deep breathing  
→ Use a weighted object (blanket, vest, etc.) and sit in silence for 3–5 minutes  
→ Vent to a voice memo without worrying about being “coherent”
