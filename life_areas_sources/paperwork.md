## 🧠 Executive Function & Strategy

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD or Other Disorders**  
_(No authors or year specified)_  
➤ Offers general organization tips such as **using calendars and reminder apps**, **breaking down tasks**, and building consistent habits that can support paperwork management even if not addressed directly.

**ADHD 2.0**  
_Edward M. Hallowell, M.D., & John J. Ratey, M.D. (2022)_  
➤ Stresses the power of **externalized systems** like apps, alarms, and reminders to compensate for working memory. Suggests **creating structure** to keep paperwork and task follow-through from falling through the cracks.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
_James Kustow (2024)_  
➤ Offers a clear approach to handling documents: **use "to-do" wallets** for active paperwork and a **"to-read" tray** for reference material. Also encourages structured handling of **emails and texts**, tying everything into a system.

**The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success**  
_Peg Dawson & Richard Guare (2015)_  
➤ Emphasizes the use of **checklists** to support executive function weaknesses, especially **working memory**. Provides practical tools to help follow through on multi-step tasks like sorting, reading, or returning paperwork.

## 🗂️ Physical Paperwork & Pile Management

**Order from Chaos**  
_Jaclyn L. Paul (2018)_  
➤ Breaks down paperwork overwhelm using **Inbox Zero**, **Bullet Journaling**, and **GTD** strategies. Suggests scanning and shredding to reduce physical clutter and stresses the need for **external containers**—physical or digital.

**You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
_Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo (2006)_  
➤ Discusses paperwork resistance in depth. Suggests **"Meeting with your paper" at regular times** to tackle incoming clutter, reduce shame, and maintain flow. Acknowledges emotional barriers and proposes creative, tailored systems.

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
_KC Davis (2022)_  
➤ Introduces the **Five Things Tidying Method**, which helps frame paper clutter as just one more category in the home—not a personal failure. Offers a **non-shaming, task-neutral approach** to re-entry and sorting.

## 💻 Digital Organization & Workflow Tools

**The PARA Method**  
_Tiago Forte (2023)_  
➤ Focuses on digital paperwork and files. Organizes **for action**, with documents grouped by **project or goal**, not broad topic. Encourages storing all relevant assets together to avoid scattered resources.

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju DOMI (2025)_  
➤ Offers a broad framework for **digital and physical systems**. Encourages **consistent maintenance**, **file management**, and **task tracking**, while recognizing that rigid perfection isn't sustainable for ADHD.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Presents the **"Workbox" system**, starting with **"Capture and Collect"**—scanning inboxes (physical and digital) and putting new info in a trusted spot. Promotes **reference systems** and having a **recovery plan** for getting back on track after falling behind.

## 💬 Emotional Reframing & Support

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden & Michelle Frank (2019)_  
➤ Emphasizes self-compassion and personalization in strategies. Encourages a mindset shift when dealing with disorganization or paper piles: **you are not broken—your system is just not ADHD-friendly yet**.
