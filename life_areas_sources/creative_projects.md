## 📚 Sources for Creative Projects

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources support the unique strengths — and challenges — ADHDers face in creative work: from bursts of inspiration and idea overload to motivation crashes, perfectionism, and finishing struggles. Learn how to leverage hyperfocus, manage project overwhelm, and build scaffolding that honors your creative brain.

---

### ✨ Embracing ADHD Creativity

- **Hallowell, Edward M. & Ratey, John J.** _ADHD 2.0_  
    Frames ADHD as a “gift, if managed well.” Highlights creativity, imagination, and hyperfocus as superpowers. Introduces the concept of **“Find Your Right Difficult”** to unlock flow in creative projects.
    
- **Dunne, Maureen.** _The Neurodiversity Edge_  
    Describes how ADHDers excel in originality, flexibility, and idea generation. Offers real-world examples of how neurodivergent thinkers thrive in **less constrained creative environments**.
    
- **McCabe, Jessica.** _How to ADHD_  
    Encourages working _with_ your ADHD brain and building a toolbox of **nontraditional strategies**. Validates non-linear work patterns and the power of embracing your **idea-driven nature**.
    
- **Pink, Richard & Roxanne.** _Small Talk_  
    Debunks the “I’m lazy” myth and reframes **bursty creativity** and inconsistent motivation as valid productivity patterns. Supports overcoming perfectionism and honoring creative pacing.
    
- **Solden, Sari.** _Women with Attention Deficit Disorder_  
    Describes a “surplus of ideas, creativity, excitement, and interest” in ADHDers. Encourages reframing disorganization and daydreaming as part of the creative process.
    
- **Solden, Sari & Frank, Michelle.** _A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD_  
    Champions **purposeful distractibility as creativity**. Focuses on neurodivergent strengths and encourages alignment with values and passions.
    
- **Dunne, Maureen.** _Neurodiversity Edge_  
    Emphasizes ADHDers' ability to connect novel ideas. Validates impulsivity and nonlinear thinking as creative advantages.
    
- **Maté, Gabor.** _Scattered Minds_  
    Argues that creativity is a natural outlet for ADHD energy. Emphasizes process over outcomes and advocates for **emotional authenticity** in creative work.

### 🎯 Turning Ideas Into Action

- **Gilkey, Charlie.** _Start Finishing_  
    Focuses on moving from **idea generation to execution**. Introduces concepts like **momentum planning** and GATES (Gifts, Aptitudes, Talents, Experiences, Strengths) to push past inertia.
    
- **Donovan, Dani.** _The Anti-Planner_  
    Offers tools like **gamified project boards, creative rituals, and customization**, ideal for ADHDers who resist rigid systems. Encourages using artistic expression and playful systems to organize.
    
- **Kinzer, Nikki & Wright, Pete D.** _Unapologetically ADHD_  
    Redefines productivity for ADHDers. Offers systems built around **joy, creativity, and hyperfocus**. Validates designing workflows that honor your unique energy cycles.
    
- **Forte, Tiago.** _Building a Second Brain_ & _The PARA Method_  
    Focuses on **organizing ideas and creative fragments** so that inspiration can turn into execution. Encourages storing and retrieving thoughts for long-term creative flow.
    
- **Le Cunff, Anne-Laure.** _Tiny Experiments_  
    Encourages an experimental, iterative mindset. Warns of **getting stuck in planning systems** and advocates for using curiosity to sustain momentum on creative paths.
    
- **Ducommun, Jules.** _Productivity With ADHD_  
    Shows how to **optimize hyperfocus**, manage creative bursts, and **build structure without rigidity**. Encourages tailoring systems around ADHD cycles of engagement and rest.

### 🛠️ Executive Function & Creative Structure

- **Levrini, Abigail.** _Succeeding With Adult ADHD_  
    Offers practical tools for staying on track in long-term projects. Encourages using structured planning, self-reflection, and scaffolding for tasks like **writing, editing, and design work**.
    
- **Kemp, Jennifer & Mitchelson, Monique.** _The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook_  
    Teaches how to make tasks **NICE** (Novel, Interesting, Challenging, Emergency) to maintain interest. Supports **body doubling and self-accommodation** for ADHD attention cycles.
    
- **Paul, Jaclyn.** _Order from Chaos_  
    Challenges the myth that **creative = messy**. Offers practical strategies to **channel hyperfocus** into follow-through and create external structure for idea-heavy brains.
    
- **Kustow, James.** _How to Thrive with Adult ADHD_  
    Recommends creative workarounds and encourages leaning into your **“ADHD signature.”** Validates bursts of energy and the need for recovery time after deep creative work.
    
- **Kelly, Kate & Ramundo, Peggy.** _You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!_  
    Encourages collaboration with others who balance out your challenges. Suggests that ADHD energy can be productive when channeled toward **passion-led projects**.
