## 📚 Sources for Car Maintenance

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources support ADHD-friendly approaches to recurring, low-urgency responsibilities — like car maintenance — with strategies for scaffolding, system setup, emotional reframing, and collaborative support.

---

### 🛠️ Systems & Scheduling

- **Dawson, Peg & Guare, Richard.** _Smart but Scattered Guide to Success_  
    Recommends digital off-loading, checklists, and time estimation — supporting “Chunk Large Tasks,” “Set Recurring Reminders,” and “Car Note in Your Phone.” Encourages asking others for help and using accountability partners.
    
- **Forte, Tiago.** _Building a Second Brain_ & _The PARA Method_  
    Offers frameworks for organizing tasks and reference info, backing strategies like “Dedicated Car Folder,” “Car Note in Your Phone,” and organizing car tasks by _area_ of responsibility.
    
- **Paul, Jaclyn.** _Order from Chaos_  
    Encourages task and system sustainability over perfection. Promotes checklists and digital calendars for recurring maintenance and appointment tracking.
    
- **DOMI, Ju.** _Productivity With ADHD: Complete Guide_  
    Recommends using tools like Notion or Google Drive to track maintenance, service history, and reminders — supporting visibility and externalization.
    
- **Gilkey, Charlie.** _Start Finishing_  
    Offers strategies for breaking larger projects into smaller steps and building systems with deadlines — ideal for low-urgency tasks like car upkeep.
    
- **Kinzer, Nikki & Wright, Pete D.** _Unapologetically ADHD_  
    Reinforces that “maintenance is a system, not a moral trait.” Encourages using time-blocking, workboxes (calendar + task manager), and choosing tools that match _you_. Strongly supports “You’re not lazy” and “Systems matter.”
    
- **Kelly, Kate & Ramundo, Peggy.** _You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid, or Crazy?!_  
    Emphasizes use of planners and logs for recurring responsibilities. Encourages building electronic reminders and “outsourcing” cognitive load.
    

---

### 🧠 Emotional Reframing & Mindset

- **Davis, KC.** _How to Keep House While Drowning_  
    Establishes that care tasks are morally neutral. Directly informs the “Car Maintenance ≠ Morality” section — “You don’t need to love car care to be good at life.”
    
- **Lakshmin, Pooja.** _Real Self-Care_  
    Advocates for self-compassion, mindset shifts, and releasing guilt around responsibility — aligning with “Progress over Perfection.”
    
- **Le Cunff, Anne-Laure.** _Tiny Experiments_  
    Encourages “intentional imperfection,” emotional experimentation, and scaffolding instead of rigid goal-setting. Supports shame-free initiation of low-urgency tasks.
    
- **McCabe, Jessica.** _How to ADHD_  
    Offers ADHD-specific tools like permission slips and “scaffolding systems.” Promotes breaking through overwhelm and building confidence with small progress wins.
    

---

### 🧍‍♀️ Support & Delegation

- **Donovan, Dani.** _The Anti-Planner_  
    Suggests using external reminders and “accountability buddies.” Encourages letting others empower you to follow through — especially with unexciting or repetitive tasks.
    
- **Miller, William R. & Rollnick, Stephen.** _Motivational Interviewing_  
    Guides how to collaborate without shame or pressure — informing strategies like “Body Double for Scheduling” and “Let Someone Else Be the Car Brain.”
    
- **Solden, Sari.** _Women with Attention Deficit Disorder_  
    Encourages realistic self-assessment and delegating tasks when appropriate. Supports outsourcing as a strength, not failure.
    
- **Ratey, Nancy A.** _The Disorganized Mind_  
    Discusses how support partners can help with task follow-through and progress tracking, particularly in routine or detail-heavy responsibilities.
    

---

### 🧾 Financial Tie-Ins

- **Alexander-Grout, Maddy.** _Mad About Money_  
    While focused on finances, this book supports linking bills and recurring car costs to predictable cycles like paychecks — supporting “Link to Paychecks” strategy.
