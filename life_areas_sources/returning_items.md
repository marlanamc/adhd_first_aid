## 📦 Return Reminders & Visual Systems

**73 ADHD-Friendly Ways to Organize Your Life Now**  
_ADDitude Experts (2012)_  
➤ To avoid forgetting returns, **place items near your “launchpad”** — a designated area for things you need before leaving the house. This creates a **visual reminder** to bring the item with you when heading out.

## 💸 ADHD Tax & Financial Impact of Unreturned Items

**Mad About Money: Managing Finances (and Life) with ADHD**  
_Maddy Alexander-Grout (2025)_

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them**  
_Richard Pink & Roxanne Pink (2024)_  
➤ Introduce the concept of **“ADHD Tax”** — extra financial burdens from ADHD symptoms like **forgetting to return purchases** or missing return windows. Emphasize that these seemingly small mishaps **accumulate into real costs**, often driven by executive dysfunction or overwhelm.
