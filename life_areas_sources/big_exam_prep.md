## 📚 Sources for Big Exam Prep (Long-Term Studying)

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources explain the neurological roots of long-term study challenges and offer strategies for motivation, organization, task breakdown, mindset, and more.

---

### 💻 Executive Function & ADHD Strategy

- **Dawson, Peg & Guare, Richard.** _Smart but Scattered Guide to Success_  
    Offers strategies for breaking down big tasks, using checklists for working memory, and modifying tasks to reduce friction. Encourages accountability, rewards, and environmental support.
    
- **Donovan, Dani.** _The Anti-Planner_  
    Focuses on ADHD-friendly task breakdowns, using micro-steps, visual boards (Kanban, "Ta-Do" lists), and sensory rituals to jumpstart momentum.
    
- **Kinzer, Nikki & Wright, Pete D.** _Unapologetically ADHD_  
    A comprehensive ADHD planning framework promoting externalization, time-blocking, emotional regulation, and values-aligned prioritization.
    
- **McCabe, Jessica.** _How to ADHD_  
    Offers practical, research-backed ADHD tools, including externalizing strategies, permission slips, and mindset shifts rooted in self-compassion.
    
- **Levrini, Abigail L.** _Succeeding With Adult ADHD_  
    Practical tools like the Cornell note-taking method, active reading strategies, and exercises for managing anxiety and building consistency.
    
- **Kemp, Jennifer & Mitchelson, Monique.** _The Neurodivergence Skills Workbook_  
    Encourages self-compassion and leaning into personal strengths, with actionable tips like checklist usage, intentional imperfection, and reflection.

### 🧠 Mindset, Motivation & Self-Compassion

- **Dee, Lisa.** _Healthy Happy ADHD_  
    Emphasizes visualization and “future self” thinking — foundational for pre-commitment and motivation strategies in exam prep.
    
- **Le Cunff, Anne-Laure.** _Tiny Experiments_  
    Introduces the idea of iterative, low-stakes learning and reframing effort as experimentation. Highlights the role of metacognition.
    
- **Lakshmin, Pooja.** _Real Self-Care_  
    Promotes boundary-setting and guilt reduction as part of sustainable productivity — crucial for long-term prep.
    
- **Kegan, Robert & Lahey, Lisa.** _Immunity to Change_  
    Helps uncover internal resistance through tools like the “immunity map” — useful for identifying mindset barriers in studying.
    
- **Gilkey, Charlie.** _Start Finishing_  
    Offers momentum-building strategies like environmental setup, cold-start routines, and project chunking to move from idea to action.
    

---

### 🧰 Tools, Systems & Externalization

- **Forte, Tiago.** _Building a Second Brain_ & _The PARA Method_  
    Advocates for capturing information in external systems — notes, outlines, project trackers — to reduce cognitive load and increase follow-through.
    
- **Fogg, B. J.** _Tiny Habits_  
    Demonstrates how to create momentum with "Minimum Viable Effort." Encourages celebrating micro-wins to reinforce habit formation.
    
- **Paul, Jaclyn.** _Order from Chaos_  
    Promotes ADHD-friendly organization tools like the Bullet Journal and reframes failures as learning data, not personal flaws.
    

---

### 🧭 Coaching, Support & Emotional Safety

- **Kimsey-House, Karen et al.** _Co-Active Coaching_  
    Offers a coaching-based approach to accountability, self-reflection, and meaningful action planning — echoed in the guide's partner/tutor strategies.
    
- **Miller, William R. & Rollnick, Stephen.** _Motivational Interviewing_  
    Highlights how to support behavior change by developing clarity, surfacing obstacles, and fostering intrinsic motivation — skills echoed in self-talk and accountability strategies.
    
- **Solden, Sari.** _Women with Attention Deficit Disorder_  
    Emphasizes emotional validation and breaking work into small, achievable steps, especially in high-pressure or shame-prone environments.
