**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
_James Kustow (2024)_  
➤ Emphasizes that ADHD disrupts **time-based planning and execution**. **The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success**  
_Peg Dawson & Richard Guare (2015)_  
➤ Focuses on **strengthening executive skills**. Recommends **off-loading tasks** into external systems and using **checklists** to manage working memory. Warns against info overload and encourages **realistic goal setting** and gradual consistency building.

## ⏰ Scheduling Tools, Time Management & Systems

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju DOMI (2025)_  
➤ Addresses **time blindness** and promotes **specialized scheduling strategies** like a **Productivity Framework** with **anchor points, buffer zones, and recovery time**. Recommends using **structured time logs**, **timers**, and tools like Asana and Beeminder. Encourages flexible systems over rigid routines.

**Order from Chaos**  
_Jaclyn L. Paul (2018)_  
➤ Advocates for externalizing all tasks: **“You need a container… and it cannot be inside your brain.”** Recommends **GTD, Bullet Journaling, and Weekly Reviews**. Introduces **hyperfocus corrals** and prioritization tips to avoid overloading calendars.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Offers a full **planning workflow** (Capture & Collect, Prioritize, Plan) with an emphasis on **realistic durations** and **recovery plans**. Introduces the **ADHD Priority Matrix**, recommends **Workboxes**, and suggests scheduling short, efficient **Weekly Focus sessions**.

**The PARA Method**  
_Tiago Forte (2023)_  
➤ Applies project-based digital organization for immediate action. Encourages **gathering all related notes/documents in one location** to reduce time spent searching and make planning fluid and task-driven.

## 🌀 Personalization, Rhythms & Emotional Regulation

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden & Michelle Frank (2019)_  
➤ Emphasizes **customized routines**, avoiding **slump hours**, and honoring one's natural rhythm. Encourages readers to **strategize your strategies** and integrate support systems (medication, therapy, coaching) that reflect identity and autonomy.

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
_KC Davis (2022)_  
➤ Introduces **“rhythms over routines”** as a gentle alternative to rigid scheduling. Promotes adaptable patterns that allow you to **“skip a beat and return to the groove”**, offering a more forgiving and sustainable approach to care tasks.

**You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
_Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo (2006)_  
➤ Encourages **personally designed organizational systems** and advises **meeting with your paperwork regularly**. Acknowledges overestimation of time and distraction as core planning issues. Recommends using **color and environmental design** for focus.
