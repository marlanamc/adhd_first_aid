## 🧠 Executive Function & Organizational Strategy

**100+ Successful Tips for Adults with ADHD or Other Disorders**  
_(No authors or year specified)_  
➤ Offers actionable strategies like **breaking down large tasks**, using **Pomodoro timers**, and minimizing distractions. Emphasizes **dedicated workspaces**, **digital tools**, and **visual scheduling** for maintaining organization.

**The Smart but Scattered Guide to Success**  
_Peg Dawson & Richard Guare (2015)_  
➤ Focuses on **executive skill development**, especially for **working memory, planning, and task initiation**. Highlights **checklists** as essential for building consistency and supporting ADHD brains with weaker internal organization systems.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
_James Kustow (2024)_  
➤ Presents a **seven-pillar framework** that includes stepwise habit change and **decluttering strategies**. Advises reducing **information overload** and integrating changes gradually to promote sustainable organizational systems.

**ADHD 2.0**  
_Edward M. Hallowell, M.D., & John J. Ratey, M.D. (2022)_  
➤ Recommends building structure through **schedules and to-do lists**, reinforced neurologically by the act of writing. Encourages using **alarms, sticky notes, and partner nudges** to support follow-through.

## 🏡 Physical Organization & Home Life

**Order from Chaos**  
_Jaclyn L. Paul (2018)_  
➤ Breaks down the ADHD approach to organization with **external containers, system personalization, and acceptance of failure**. Offers **GTD**, **Bullet Journaling**, and **Inbox Zero** as adaptable tools, and stresses the need for **weekly reviews** and realistic expectations.

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
_KC Davis (2022)_  
➤ Advocates for **“rhythms over routines”** and prioritizing **function over perfection**. Uses rituals like the **Five Things Tidying Method** to reduce decision fatigue and build sustainable home systems.

**Healthy Happy ADHD**  
_Lisa Dee (2025)_  
➤ Encourages women with ADHD to build structure **around wellness routines**, helping improve mood, focus, and internal calm. Organization becomes a byproduct of well-supported daily systems.

**You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
_Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo (2006)_  
➤ Frames ADHD disorganization as **a memory and processing issue**, not a moral failing. Emphasizes emotional repair, **creative systems**, **external accountability**, and the value of **professional organizers** or “bridges” to support lasting change.

## 💻 Digital & Planning Systems

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju DOMI (2025)_  
➤ Encourages building a **unified personal system** for managing physical and digital spaces. Advocates for **time blindness tools**, **habit-friendly digital organization**, and planning environments that flex with ADHD brains.

**The PARA Method**  
_Tiago Forte (2023)_  
➤ Offers a digital filing framework based on **Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives**. Focuses on **organizing for action**, not aesthetics, and emphasizes “just-in-time” organizing to support creative focus and reduce search time.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Introduces tools like the **Workbox** and **Planning Workflow**, rooted in radical acceptance. Emphasizes **externalized planning**, **intentional weekly review**, and **recovery plans** to minimize shame and support re-engagement.

## 💬 Emotional Reframing & Support

**A Radical Guide for Women with ADHD**  
_Sari Solden & Michelle Frank (2019)_  
➤ Encourages organizing from a place of **self-compassion**, not shame. Offers permission to **“strategize your strategies”** and tailor organizational tools to match individual needs rather than conforming to neurotypical expectations.
