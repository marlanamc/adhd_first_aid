## Sources for Trash & Recycling

Explore the books, guides, and research that shaped this page. These resources unpack how ADHD affects disorganization, object permanence, and clutter overwhelm — and offer strategies to build systems that are manageable, forgiving, and ADHD-friendly.

---

### 🗑️ Trash & Recycling Systems

**73 ADHD-Friendly Ways to Organize Your Life Now**  
ADDitude Experts (2012)  
➤ Encourages proactive sorting of incoming clutter like mail over a recycling bin and immediate trashing of unnecessary paperwork.

**Order from Chaos**  
Jaclyn L. Paul (2018)  
➤ Advocates for SCAN and SHRED policy to manage papers; toss receipts and recyclable items instead of filing them.

**You Mean I'm Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo (2006)  
➤ Endorses aggressive decluttering with trash cans and organizing strategies like assigning permanent homes for items.

---

### 💬 Emotional Reframes & Philosophies

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
K.C. Davis (2022)  
➤ Reframes throwing away recyclables as necessary survival when overwhelmed; permission to prioritize functioning.

**Small Talk: 10 ADHD Lies and How to Stop Believing Them**  
Richard Pink & Roxanne Pink (2024)  
➤ Challenges the need for perfect cleanliness and validates ADHD messiness; encourages self-acceptance.

**Women with Attention Deficit Disorder**  
Sari Solden (2013)  
➤ Reframes disorganization as creative potential rather than a flaw; supports designing systems that honor your energy and uniqueness.

---

### 🧠 Clutter & Executive Function

**Productivity With ADHD**  
Ju DOMI (2025)  
➤ Explains that clutter overloads cognitive processing and stresses the importance of organizing both physical and digital spaces.

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
Abigail L. Levrini (2023)  
➤ Addresses chronic disorganization and suggests creating realistic systems for manageable decluttering.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)  
➤ Recommends the “workbox” system for physical inboxes and a searchable digital cabinet for notes and resources.
