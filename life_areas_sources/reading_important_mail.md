## 📬 Physical Mail & Paper Management

**73 ADHD-Friendly Ways to Organize Your Life Now**  
_ADDitude Experts (2012)_  
➤ Recommends sorting **daily over a recycling bin**, immediately categorizing into **Action Required**, **Someday**, **Reference**, or **Trash**. Encourages **adding action items to your to-do list** and reducing incoming junk mail by signing up for opt-out services.

**How to Keep House While Drowning**  
_KC Davis (2022)_  
➤ Suggests using an **accordion file** for papers, writing **due dates on bills** and hanging them visibly (e.g., fridge or corkboard). Recommends **scanning sentimental items** to preserve memories digitally and reduce physical clutter.

**How to Thrive with Adult ADHD**  
_James Kustow (2024)_  
➤ Encourages a **physical “to-do” file** for active papers and a **“to-read” tray** for non-urgent items. Urges opening and sorting mail **immediately on arrival** to prevent buildup. Transfer action items to a to-do list and digital/physical systems.

**Order from Chaos**  
_Jaclyn Paul (2018)_  
➤ Recommends consolidating **all incoming channels** into centralized inboxes. **Process one item at a time**, never putting it back once removed. For physical mail, suggests a **“scan and shred” policy**, transparent folders for visibility, and **checklists** for recurring mail-related tasks like paying bills.

**You Mean I’m Not Lazy, Stupid or Crazy?!**  
_Kate Kelly & Peggy Ramundo (2006)_  
➤ Advises immediately discarding unneeded papers, **tearing out just the useful pages**, and logging items into a **planning notebook**. Promotes using **“hot folders”** for high-priority documents and teaching yourself to **code and file information in your preferred format** (visual/verbal/auditory).

## 💻 Digital Mail, Email & Inbox Management

**Start Finishing**  
_Charlie Gilkey (2019)_  
➤ Urges a shift from **“checking” email to “processing” it**, meaning you only open it when you're ready to take action. This reduces distraction and builds trust in your inbox system.

**Productivity With ADHD**  
_Ju DOMI (2025)_  
➤ Promotes **digital file hierarchies** and **established email protocols**. Recommends **regular reviews** and systems that prioritize **progress over perfection**, helping prevent email pileup and overwhelm.

**Unapologetically ADHD**  
_Nikki Kinzer & Pete D. Wright (2024)_  
➤ Encourages **“Capture and Collect”** practices across all inputs (mail, notes, email, texts) into designated inboxes. Advises **processing inboxes regularly**, setting **email workflows**, and designating **one notebook as your capture point**. Writing something down isn’t commitment—it’s a way to sort and delegate.

**Succeeding With Adult ADHD**  
_Abigail L. Levrini (2025)_  
➤ Recommends **consulting others** to design filing systems that work for your brain (date-based, category-based, etc.). For clarity, **request written confirmation of important tasks** and **file or discard documents quickly** to prevent accumulation.

## 🧠 Structure, Support & Emotional Reframing

**The Disorganized Mind**  
_Nancy A. Ratey (2008)_  
➤ Explains how ADHD makes daily mail and finances feel “stifling.” Suggests creating **reliable systems for structure** to reduce stress and avoid the spiraling impact of disorganization.
