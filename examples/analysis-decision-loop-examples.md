# Analysis & Decision Loop Examples

## 🧠 Analysis Paralysis
### 🧠 The Thinking Spiral: When Research Becomes Quicksand

**The Trigger:** You need to make a decision (buy something, choose a path, start a project)

**The ADHD Amplification:** Your brain craves certainty but struggles with executive function
- **Executive dysfunction** makes weighing options feel overwhelming
- **Working memory** gets overloaded juggling all the possibilities
- **Perfectionism + RSD** make the "wrong" choice feel catastrophic
- **Low dopamine** creates shutdown when there's no clear "best" answer

**The Mental Traffic Jam:**
1. You start researching to find the "perfect" choice
2. Each new piece of information spawns 3 more questions
3. Options multiply instead of narrowing down
4. Your brain gets stuck in analysis mode, unable to switch to action

**The Paralysis:** Research becomes a way to avoid the anxiety of deciding, but never actually reduces it

## 🤯 Decision Overwhelm  
### 🧠 The Thinking Spiral: When Choices Become Chaos

**The Trigger:** Too many options (restaurant menu, career paths, what to watch)

**The ADHD Amplification:** Choice overload crashes your executive system
- **Executive dysfunction** can't prioritize or filter options effectively
- **Working memory limits** get maxed out by trying to hold multiple possibilities
- **All-or-nothing thinking** makes every choice feel permanent and critical
- **Time blindness** makes quick decisions feel rushed and scary

**The Mental Traffic Jam:**
1. Your brain tries to process every option simultaneously
2. Analysis branches into infinite "what if" scenarios  
3. Fear of missing out keeps you from eliminating choices
4. Mental energy gets depleted by decision-making before you even choose

**The Paralysis:** You avoid choosing until external pressure forces a hasty decision you second-guess immediately

## 🎯 Perfectionism Cycles
### 🧠 The Thinking Spiral: When Good Enough Never Is

**The Trigger:** Starting any task where the outcome matters to you

**The ADHD Amplification:** Your brain equates imperfection with failure
- **All-or-nothing thinking** tells you if it's not perfect, it's worthless
- **RSD and fear of judgment** make criticism feel like rejection of your entire self
- **Executive dysfunction** makes it hard to know when something is "done"
- **Working memory gaps** lose track of what actually matters vs. perfectionist details

**The Mental Traffic Jam:**
1. You start a task with impossibly high standards
2. Each imperfection feels like evidence you're "not good enough"
3. You restart, revise, or abandon work that's actually fine
4. Fear of judgment prevents you from sharing or finishing anything

**The Paralysis:** Perfectionism becomes procrastination - avoiding the task feels safer than risking imperfect results
