# Energy & Capacity + Task & Communication Loop Examples

## ⚡ Energy & Capacity Loops

### 😷 Masking Exhaustion
#### ⚡ The Depletion Pattern: When Camouflaging Costs Everything

**The Demand:** Social and professional situations require you to appear "normal"

**The Overextension:** Your ADHD brain burns 3x the energy to do what looks effortless
- **Masking** requires constant cognitive monitoring and suppression of natural impulses
- **Working memory** gets hijacked by managing your presentation vs. focusing on tasks
- **Executive function** depletes rapidly from constant self-regulation
- **Emotional regulation** becomes harder when you're already at capacity

**The Crash:**
1. You successfully "pass" as neurotypical all day/week
2. Mental resources become completely depleted
3. You collapse in private - shutdown, meltdown, or complete withdrawal
4. Recovery time is longer than the masking period

**The Shame Cycle:** You feel guilty for being tired from "just existing," leading to more pressure to mask better

### 🏃‍♀️ Workout Avoidance
#### ⚡ The Depletion Pattern: When Self-Care Feels Impossible

**The Demand:** Exercise requires executive function, energy, and sensory tolerance

**The Overextension:** Your ADHD brain miscalculates the true cost of working out
- **Executive dysfunction** makes the multi-step process of exercising overwhelming
- **Sensory sensitivity** to clothing, sounds, or environments creates additional barriers
- **All-or-nothing thinking** makes anything less than a "real workout" feel pointless
- **Energy miscalculation** doesn't account for the activation energy required

**The Crash:**
1. You plan to exercise but realize you're already depleted
2. The thought of changing clothes, going somewhere, and being active feels impossible
3. You skip the workout and feel guilty about "being lazy"
4. Shame makes future exercise feel even more loaded

**The Shame Cycle:** Physical inactivity affects mood and energy, making exercise feel even more necessary but harder to start

## 📋 Task & Communication Loops

### 📧 Email Overwhelm
#### 📋 The Avoidance Spiral: When Inboxes Become Mountains

**The Task:** Email seems simple but requires complex executive processing

**The Executive Breakdown:** Each email needs sorting, prioritizing, decision-making, and follow-through
- **Executive dysfunction** breaks down at the "triage and respond" stage
- **Working memory** forgets the context by the time you finish reading
- **Perfectionism** makes every response feel high-stakes
- **Time blindness** makes "quick email check" consume hours

**The Avoidance:**
1. You open your inbox and feel immediately overwhelmed by the volume
2. Each email spawns mental tasks you can't track or prioritize
3. You close the inbox to "deal with it later" when you have more energy
4. Emails accumulate faster than you can process them

**The Mountain Effect:** What started as manageable communication becomes an insurmountable pile that proves you're "bad at adulting"

---

### 💬 Replying to Texts
#### 📋 The Avoidance Spiral: When Messages Become Burdens  

**The Task:** Texting appears effortless but requires emotional and executive energy

**The Executive Breakdown:** Each message needs processing, tone interpretation, response crafting, and sending
- **Working memory** drops the message context between reading and responding
- **Executive dysfunction** struggles with the "craft appropriate response" step
- **Social anxiety** makes tone interpretation and response crafting stressful
- **Time blindness** makes you lose track of how long you've been "meaning to respond"

**The Avoidance:**
1. You read a message and mentally compose a reply
2. You get distracted before sending and forget you never actually responded
3. Too much time passes, making the response feel awkward or insufficient
4. Shame about delayed response makes replying feel even more loaded

**The Mountain Effect:** Your relationships suffer from communication gaps that feel intentional but are actually neurological
